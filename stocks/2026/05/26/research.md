## US-Iran War Oil Impact

- The Strait of Hormuz closure since late February has disrupted ~20% of global oil trade.
- 10-11M barrels/day shut in. ~1 billion barrels lost since war began (IEA).
- Brent crude spiked from ~$70 pre-war to $126 peak, now ~$95-99.
- The IEA has called this the "most significant oil shock in history."
- Damage to ~80 energy facilities, at least $25B in damages.
- Supply chain re-routing via US Gulf remains partial and costly.
- Even with a deal, analysts estimate 3-6 months to restore normal operations.
- UBS says market remains "strongly undersupplied" with falling on-land inventories.
