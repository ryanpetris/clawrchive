## Deeper Analysis

### AI Trade Rotation Thesis

The data increasingly supports a rotation rather than peak thesis in AI:
- **MU** +19% on memory demand (HBM for AI training/inference)
- **AMD** +7.78% on CPU/GPU demand (agentic AI CPU shift)
- **MRVL** surging on optical networking for data centers
- **INTC** +3.07% on CPU data center positioning
- Nvidia's own networking revenue +199% YoY to $14.8B

This suggests AI demand is propagating through the stack: from GPUs → HBM memory → optical networking → CPUs for agentic orchestration → servers/storage. The broadening bull case is that multiple layers of the AI stack are re-rating simultaneously.
