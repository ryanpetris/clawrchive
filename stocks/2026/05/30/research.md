# Research Notes — May 30, 2026

## Market Structure Observations

### S&P 500 Milestone
The S&P 500 closed above 7,500 for the first time this week. The index has now posted 9 consecutive weekly gains — an extraordinary streak. Key driver: AI infrastructure earnings momentum combined with falling oil prices on Iran ceasefire expectations.

However, the breadth question remains: the rally is heavily concentrated in semiconductors and AI infrastructure. The S&P 500's equity-bond correlation breakdown (stocks up while bonds fall) complicates the macro signal.

### Nasdaq Outperformance
Nasdaq Composite +8% in May vs. S&P 500 +5% and Dow +2%. This dispersion is notable — the megacap tech and semiconductor names are pulling far ahead of value and cyclicals. AI-led, narrow rally.

### Semiconductors: Record Valuations But Underlying Unit Weakness
- March WSTS: total semi sales +88.1% YoY, but -8.5% MoM from February's record-breaking month.
- Growth entirely from ICs (+99.5% YoY), specifically Memory (+269.1%) and Logic (+38.9%).
- IC growth excluding memory: just 28.3%. Analog +13.8%, Micro +8.8%.
- **Critical concern:** IC unit growth just 20.3% annualized — ASP-driven growth, not demand-driven. Memory will crash when new capacity comes online.
- Future Horizons is the only analyst forecasting a downturn "maybe this year, if not next." They caution the AI boom accounts for <1% of chip unit shipments despite 25-50% of revenue.

### Memory Hype Cycle Risk
Baird's Ross Mayfield explicitly called memory chip stocks "priced beyond perfection" and a "dicey game." This is a direct warning on MU, SK Hynix, Samsung. The UBS target of $1,625 on MU drove a 19% single-day move — that much of a re-rating from an analyst note carries mean-reversion risk.

### Dell: The New Wall Street Darling
Dell's earnings are a pivotal data point:
- Revenue $43.8B (+88% YoY)
- AI server revenue $16.1B (+757% YoY)
- $24.4B in new AI orders, backlog $51.3B (record)
- FY27 AI server forecast raised to ~$60B
- Supply chain shortages: DRAM > NAND > CPUs > hard drives
- JPMorgan raised PT to $500, Morgan Stanley admitted "misjudgment"
- Stock surged 32.8% Friday; YTD +150%

The Dell narrative confirms the AI buildout is real and accelerating. But the scale advantage question is important: Dell's supply chain power during shortages may pressure SMCI and HPE margins.

### Microsoft's AI Run Rate
MSFT closed at $450.24 (+5.45% Friday) after disclosing a $37B AI revenue run rate. The "One Copilot" super app rumor suggests Microsoft is consolidating GitHub Copilot, AI chat, and agentic tools into a single platform — similar to the super app trend in Asia.

### Computex: Nvidia's Moment
This is arguably Nvidia's most consequential product event of 2026:
- **N1/N1X Arm PC processors** — 20-core CPU, up to 6,144 CUDA cores, 180-200 TOPS AI performance (4x Qualcomm Snapdragon X). This would make Nvidia a full-stack computing company (data center + edge + PC) and expand TAM dramatically.
- **Vera Rubin timing** — Next-gen AI platform. Vera CPU sold as standalone product opens $200B TAM.
- **Agentic AI TAM** — Lynx Equity expects Jensen to frame a $200B addressable market for agentic workloads.
- **$150B Taiwan spending pledge** — Annual procurement from Taiwanese suppliers, up from $100B.
- **Key risk:** If N1X or Vera Rubin timelines disappoint, the stock could sell off given elevated expectations.

### Export Control Overhang
- China stepped up military moves near Taiwan.
- Taiwan prosecuted three individuals for smuggling Nvidia chips to China via Japan using servers.
- Nvidia's guidance excludes all China data-center compute revenue.
- Samsung/Anthropic $30T won deal attempt — supply chain diversification away from TSMC.

### Token-Maxing Backlash
- Amazon scrapped "KiroRank" employee AI usage rankings.
- Meta discontinued "Claudenomics" token tracking.
- Uber exhausted annual AI budget in 4 months on Claude Code without clear productivity gains.
- Salesforce introduced "Agent Work Unit" (AWU) as alternative to token counts.
- **Implication:** Enterprise AI adoption is real but ROI measurement is immature. Could lead to budget scrutiny in H2 2026.

### Samsung Foundry / Anthropic
Samsung is bidding for Anthropic's AI accelerator chip contract — potentially $30T won (~$20B). Samsung's advantage: only company that can independently handle HBM, foundry, and advanced packaging. Potential TSMC share loss.

### LG Surge on Jensen Huang Korea Visit
LG Electronics +29.93% in a single session on expectations of physical AI cooperation with Nvidia (robotics, smart factories). LG has risen 218.8% YTD. This is thematic momentum — actual deal details are unconfirmed.

### Cathie Wood Trims AMD
ARK Invest trimmed AMD positions (~$16.2M across 3 ETFs on May 19, continuing a May pattern) despite AMD's 135% YTD return and record quarterly results. Lisa Su revised server CPU TAM to >$120B by 2030 (35%+ CAGR). Book profit or sector rotation signal.

## Potential Scenarios

- Computex delivers on N1X, Vera Rubin, agentic AI vision → NVDA re-rates higher
- AVGO beats and raises AI revenue outlook → semi complex extends rally
- NFP lands at or below consensus → yields stabilize, rate-hike fears ease

### Mixed Case
- AI earnings strong but macro concerns cap upside
- Sector rotation from semis to software/defensive
- Range-bound S&P 500 as earnings and macro data offset each other

## Key Uncertainty: AI Duration vs. Magnitude
The market is increasingly focused on the *duration* of the AI boom, not the *magnitude*. The question is whether hyperscaler capex can sustain at $710B+ for multiple years. Dell's $51.3B backlog suggests visibility through 2027, but supply constraints (not demand) may be the limiting factor. Future Horizons warns the semiconductor cycle is driven by ASPs, not units — a structural weakness that will eventually correct.

## Bottom Line
Next week is the most consequential for tech since Nvidia's earnings (May 21). The combination of Computex, AVGO earnings, and NFP creates multiple binary catalysts. Positioning should account for the 8-9% implied vol on AVGO and the broader macro sensitivity. The AI bull case is intact, but the concentration of catalysts means single events can drive outsized moves.
