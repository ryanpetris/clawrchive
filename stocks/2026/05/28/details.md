## 7. Outlook For Today

**Trading Setup:** Futures pointing lower with a cautious tone. Two competing forces: (1) powerful semiconductor/AI momentum with MU, SNOW, AMD all showing extraordinary strength; (2) macro headwinds from Iran tensions (oil +2%), PCE data risk, and potential profit-taking after records.

**Main Upside Catalysts:**
- Semis momentum — MU momentum still accelerating; SOXX/SMH could extend
- SNOW blowout lifting data infrastructure names
- DELL earnings (after close) — Pentagon deal sets positive tone
- PCE prints in-line or below expectations → rates relief
- Oil spikes reverse on Hormuz de-escalation

**Main Downside Risks:**
- PCE hotter than expected → rates selloff
- Iran situation escalates further → oil spike, risk-off
- DELL earnings disappoint or guide conservatively
- Profit-taking after S&P/Nasdaq record highs
- MU mania reversing (overbought, valuation concerns from Morningstar)

**Sector Strength:** Semiconductors (MU, AMD, AVGO) > Cloud infra (SNOW, DELL) > Mega-cap platforms (AMZN, GOOGL, META) > Software (CRM weak) > Cybersecurity (ZS weak).

**Sector Weakness:** Software names with guidance risk; cybersecurity after ZS warning.

**Nature of Moves:** Largely fundamental (earnings-driven) and thematic (AI/memory) with a macro overlay (Iran, PCE).

**What Would Change This View:**
- Significant PCE surprise (>0.4% MoM core) would increase rates headwind materially
- U.S.-Iran de-escalation would remove the oil/geopolitical overhang
- DELL earnings beating and raising AI server outlook would supercharge infra names

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Semis are the market's center of gravity** — MU, AMD, AVGO remain in persistent uptrend. Memory mania is real but extended. Watch for DRAM cycle signals.
2. **SNOW's blowout is the software standout** — the $6B AWS commitment validates data cloud demand. Positive read-through for data infrastructure broadly.
3. **Today is two-tiered risk/reward** — PCE data (8:30am) and DELL earnings (after close) are the binary catalysts. Expect elevated intraday volatility.
4. **CRM's guide miss matters** — even with $27B in buybacks, the market is signaling software multiple compression. Agentforce ($1.2B ARR) is real but not yet moving the needle.
5. **Macro creep is the new variable** — Iran tensions, PCE, and the SpaceX IPO timing are creating a risk-on/risk-off tug-of-war that makes every tech trade a macro trade.
