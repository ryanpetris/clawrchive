### Memory Sector Deep Dive

**The $3 Trillion Memory Trio:**
- MU crossed $1T on Tuesday (May 26) after UBS Arcuri tripled target. SK Hynix followed Wednesday reaching ~$1.12T. Samsung first crossed $1T on May 6.
- Combined: approaching $3T market cap for the three HBM producers.
- The HBM conversion is pulling standard DRAM/NAND capacity, creating consumer electronics shortages.
- IDC: smartphone avg price to hit record $523 (+14%); unit sales to fall 12.9%.
- PC makers warning of 15-20% contract price resets.
- Goldman Sachs modeling: HBM to account for ~20% of total global DRAM output in 2026 (up from mid-single digits pre-AI).

**Cycle Risk Flags:**
- Morningstar: all memory stocks trade above intrinsic value; none has durable moat.
- Global DRAM inventory up 18% MoM (potential cycle peak indicator).
- Micron's FCF yield at 4.5% — below S&P 500 average of 5.2%.
- Historical pattern: memory cycles end faster than markets expect.
