## 7. Bottom Line

1. **This is a PCE-first week.** Thursday's inflation data is the most consequential macro event for tech since the Iran oil shock. A hot print resets the rate narrative — expect caution ahead of it, then follow-through into Friday.

2. **Dell earnings are the purest AI infrastructure read-through of the week.** AI server revenue, margins, and FY guidance revision will set the tone for SMCI, HPE, and the broader AI hardware complex. BofA sees beat-and-raise at $280 target.

3. **Microsoft at 25x forward earnings is the most obvious value in mega-cap tech.** Azure growing 39-40%, $37B AI run rate, Build conference next week, and Ackman as a new catalyst. The bull flag technical setup could resolve to $510+.

4. **Semiconductor rotation is the dominant flow.** ARM (+170% YTD), MU (+166%), INTC (+62% in a month), AVGO (+90% over 52 weeks). The AI infrastructure buildout is pulling capital from software and consumer names into chips and hardware. This trend has momentum but is getting extended in names like INTC.

5. **Salesforce earnings are binary and high-conviction either way.** The options market is pricing an 8.7% move — more than double the historical average. Agentforce adoption is the swing factor: if penetration is accelerating, CRM can reclaim $200+; if stalling, the $160 BofA bear case becomes plausible.
