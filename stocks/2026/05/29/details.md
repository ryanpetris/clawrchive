# Tech Stock Briefing - May 29, 2026 - Dell Ignites AI Hardware Rally, Anthropic Hits $965B Valuation

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Dell Technologies (DELL) surged +33% premarket** after reporting Q1 FY27 revenue of $43.8B (+88% YoY), AI orders of $24.4B, and raising its FY27 AI server revenue forecast to $60B, validating that AI server demand remains in rapid expansion mode.
- **Anthropic closed a $65B Series H at a $965B valuation**, overtaking OpenAI ($730B) as the world's most valuable AI startup, with $47B annualized revenue and launching Claude Opus 4.8 with Dynamic Workflows for multi-agent orchestration.
- **US-Iran ceasefire agreement in principle**: 60-day MOU to extend ceasefire, restart nuclear negotiations, and restore Strait of Hormuz shipping access, pending Trump's approval. WTI crude fell toward $88.
- **April PCE data came in mixed**: headline 3.8% YoY, core 3.3% YoY — both elevated but not surprising to the upside, helping ease inflation fears linked to the energy shock.
- **10-year Treasury yield eased to ~4.44%**, lowest in two weeks, as falling oil and softer-than-feared PCE supported bond prices. VIX remains low at ~17.
- **Meta hints at cloud computing entry**: Zuckerberg said competing with AWS/Azure/GCP is "definitely on the table" after 8,000 layoffs and $125-145B in AI capex.
- **Nvidia announced $150B/year Taiwan investment** for new headquarters, doubling down on TSMC proximity and advanced packaging, a move that signals Taiwan remains irreplaceable for AI manufacturing.
- **Market tone: AI-led, risk-on.** S&P 500 closed at its 21st record high of the year on Thursday. Tech and AI hardware names leading, consumer discretionary lagging (Gap -15%, AEO -11%).
- **Top stocks to watch today:** DELL (earnings blowout), SMCI (AI server sympathy), NTAP (data storage), NVDA ($150B Taiwan bet), TSLA (Waymo fleet data and FSD safety report overhang).

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Trading setup:** The market opens at record levels with a clear AI hardware tailwind from Dell's staggering earnings. The Dell report is the single most consequential datapoint for the AI trade this week — $24.4B in AI orders in one quarter, a $60B annual AI server forecast, and raised guidance across the board. This directly validates that the hyperscaler capex cycle is accelerating rather than peaking.

**Main upside catalysts:**
- Dell's AI server numbers provide a fundamental floor under the entire AI hardware trade
- US-Iran ceasefire MOU reducing geopolitical risk and oil prices (tailwind for growth stocks)
- 10Y yield falling to 4.44% eases valuation pressure on high-multiple tech
- PCE not surprising to the upside removes one near-term inflation worry
- Anthropic's $65B raise and Claude Opus 4.8 launch reinforces enterprise AI momentum

**Main downside risks:**
- Sticky inflation (3.8% PCE) keeps Fed on hold; December hike probability lingers
- Consumer discretionary weakness (Gap, AEO) signals demand bifurcation
- TSLA overhang from Waymo data and FSD safety report
- MRVL earnings tonight — any disappointment could dampen AI networking sentiment
- AOC pushing EPA probe into Meta data centers — regulatory risk creeping into AI infra

**Sector strength:** AI hardware (DELL, SMCI, NTAP, HPQ) strongest. Semiconductors broadly supported. Software and cloud mixed but with an AI tilt (MSFT, ORCL, SNOW). Consumer discretionary weakest.

**Moves appear:** Fundamentally driven by Dell's numbers and the AI capex cycle, with a macro tailwind from Iran ceasefire and falling yields. Not positioning-driven or crowded-trade short-covering.

**What would change this view:** A Marvell earnings miss or weak guidance tonight. Renewed US-Iran hostilities if Trump doesn't approve the MOU. Hawkish Fed surprise.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Dell's $60B AI forecast is a single data point:** While powerful, it represents one company's view. Watch for competitive dynamics with HPE, SMCI, and Lenovo.
- **Consumer discretionary weakness:** The Gap/AEO guidance cuts may be canaries for a broader consumer pullback that could eventually weigh on ad-driven tech (META, GOOGL) and consumer hardware (AAPL).
- **Anthropic's $965B valuation vs. expected $400-500B IPO:** Late-stage investors in this round could be underwater at IPO. If this spreads to broader AI sentiment, it could create a "private peak to public discount" narrative.
- **Tesla insider selling:** $21.5M in insider sales over the last 3 months with no insider purchases raises governance questions alongside the FSD safety concerns.
- **ARM production silicon pivot:** The transition from royalty-based IP licensing to balance-sheet-intensive production silicon is a structural risk to margins and ROIC.
- **Intel valuation debate:** Stock is up 190% YTD and 395% over the past year. Consensus analyst target is $85 — well below the current ~$120 price. The rally has outpaced fundamental recovery.
- **China chip policy still unresolved:** NVDA's China data center revenue remains zero in guidance. July tariff review could add further headwinds.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Dell's earnings are the headline of the week** — $24.4B in AI orders, $60B AI server forecast, and raised guidance confirm the AI infrastructure super-cycle is alive and accelerating. SMCI, NTAP, HPQ are direct beneficiaries today.
2. **The macro setup has improved** — falling oil on Iran ceasefire prospects, 10Y yield at 2-week lows, and PCE data that didn't surprise to the upside have removed near-term headwinds for growth/tech.
3. **The AI landscape is consolidating around a few mega-players** — Anthropic's $965B valuation (with strategic participation from Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron, Amazon) shows capital concentration in the AI winners, reinforcing the infrastructure buildout thesis.
4. **Watch Marvell earnings tonight** — as the key AI networking and optical comms chip supplier, MRVL will provide the next critical data point on whether the AI capex cycle is broad-based.
5. **Risk bifurcation is real** — AI hardware names are firing on all cylinders while consumer discretionary (Gap, AEO) and some software (ZS, PDD) are showing cracks. This is not a uniform bull market.
