## Deep Analysis: Korea AI Contagion

### Key Facts
- SK Hynix (000660.KS) fell a record 15% Monday, its biggest single-day drop ever
- KOSPI plunged ~8% below 7,000, triggering circuit breakers
- SK Hynix ADR (SKHY) listed on Nasdaq Friday, fell 9.3% Monday, giving back most of 13% debut surge
- KORU (Direxion Daily MSCI South Korea Bull 3X Shares ETF) -20%+
- EWY (iShares MSCI South Korea ETF) also hit hard
- Korean leveraged chip ETFs down 45% since May — Bloomberg reports regulatory regret

### Root Causes
1. Korea Investment & Securities projecting SK Hynix Q2 operating profit miss of ~8% due to slower HBM pricing gains
2. "Sell the news" reaction to SK Hynix's $26.5B US offering (7x oversubscribed)
3. Broader AI valuation reassessment hitting Korea's most concentrated AI-exposed market
4. Feedback loop: Nic Puckrin (Coin Bureau) described "vicious circle" between Korea and US tech on Bloomberg

### Key Question
Is this a buying opportunity or a structural unwind? 
- **Bull case:** Nico Rosti (MRM Research) says "deeply oversold," calls it a buy
- **Bear case:** Leveraged ETF structures create mechanical selling pressure that's hard to stop mid-cycle
- MU offers 60% analyst upside per TipRanks but carries Korea correlation risk

### KORU ETF Specific Note
3x leveraged daily rebalance. A -8% day in KOSPI means roughly -24% for KORU before fees and volatility decay. The real risk is that prolonged Korea weakness leads to compounding losses far beyond the underlying index.

## Cash Flow Inversion Thesis (Gavin Baker)

The most important structural argument right now:
- Chipmakers (NVDA, MU, AVGO, AMAT): Combined $430B FCF over next 12 months
- Hyperscalers (AMZN, GOOGL, META, MSFT, ORCL): Combined FCF dropping from $250B to $100B
- $1.8T projected AI capex across 2026-2027

Baker argues open-source AI model adoption strips 90%+ margins from frontier labs and redirects billions to infrastructure providers. If this thesis is correct, the chip selloff is a buying opportunity. If hyperscalers fail to monetize AI, capex gets cut, and the chip FCF boom reverses.
