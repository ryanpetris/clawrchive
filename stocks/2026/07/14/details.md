# Tech Stock Briefing - July 14, 2026 - CPI Relief, Korea Contagion, Chip Selloff

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Market crashed Monday** — Nasdaq 100 fell 561 pts (-1.9%) to 29,264, its worst single-day drop in months, driven by a chip-led selloff and oil surge. S&P 500 fell 0.8% to 7,515, Dow -0.3% to 52,498.
- **Korea AI contagion spreads** — SK Hynix Korean shares crashed 15% (record single-day drop), its US ADR (SKHY) fell 9.3%, and the KOSPI plunged ~8%, triggering circuit breakers. South Korea-focused 3x bull ETF KORU tanked >20%.
- **June CPI comes in cooler than expected** — Released this morning at 8:30am ET, CPI rose 3.5% annually vs. 3.8% expected, down sharply from May's 4.2% three-year high. Treasury yields tumbled on the release, with 10Y dropping from ~4.56%.
- **Oil surge complicates Fed outlook** — WTI crude jumped above $80/bbl (+4%) as the US enforced a Strait of Hormuz blockade with a 20% fee on cargo crossings, extending US-Iran hostilities into a third day. The 2Y Treasury yield hit a 16-month high Monday before retreating on the CPI print.
- **Apple sues OpenAI** — Lawsuit filed Friday (July 10) alleging massive trade secret theft. This is sending shockwaves through the AI complex, particularly hammering Oracle (ORCL -6% to 52-week low $132.27) which depends on OpenAI for ~50% of its $638B backlog.
- **TSMC tests $420 support ahead of Thursday earnings** — Record June revenue (NT$442.68B, +68% YoY) failed to lift the stock as investors question AI capex sustainability. Q2 earnings due July 16 are the next big catalyst for the entire semiconductor space.
- **Market tone: Event-driven, defensive rotation with AI-led tech weakness.** Oil, rates, and Korea spillover are driving positioning. Best-performing areas today likely to be defensives, banks (earnings), and any names benefiting from a cooler CPI.
- **Top 5 stocks to watch today:** NVDA (post-CPI relief bounce candidate), MU (Korea memory contagion), TSM (ahead of Thursday earnings, $420 support), ORCL (OpenAI/Apple lawsuit fallout), INTC (Apple chip partnership story, but sold off -6.1% Monday).

Nasdaq futures are +0.2%, suggesting a modest bounce from Monday's -1.9% rout, but Dow futures are -0.3% on oil concerns.

- Tech stocks oversold Monday could see short-covering into CPI relief
- TSMC earnings Thursday creating anticipation-driven positioning
- Bank earnings may show strong results, lifting overall sentiment

**Main Downside Risks:**
- Oil above $81 (WTI) with Strait of Hormuz blockade — further escalation could push Brent to $90+
- Korea AI contagion may not be done — SK Hynix ADR still falling after hours
- Fed Chair Warsh testimony could push back against market optimism
- AI capex concerns remain unresolved — the "show me" moment for hyperscaler ROI hasn't arrived

**Sector Strength:**
- Weakest: Memory chips (MU, SNDK, WDC), AI infrastructure debt-heavy names (ORCL, CRWV)
- Most resilient: Defensive software (NET, SNOW), asset-light AI plays
- Most binary: TSMC (Thursday earnings sets tone for semis)

**Nature of Moves:** Macro-driven (oil, CPI, rates) with fundamental AI valuation reassessment overlay. The Korea spillover adds a positioning/flow dimension.

**What Would Change This View:** A ceasefire de-escalation in the Middle East (bearish oil, bullish tech), dovish Warsh testimony (bullish growth stocks), or a pre-announcement from TSMC raising guidance (bullish semis).

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Korea contagion is the #1 unquantified risk** — If SK Hynix continues falling and KOSPI circuit breakers keep triggering, forced liquidation of leveraged ETF structures could spread further. Bloomberg reports retail-heavy Korean leveraged chip ETFs are down 45% since May.
- **Oracle at 52-week lows but fundamental risks remain** — $42B projected FCF deficit, $40B new financing, S&P BBB- with negative outlook, OpenAI customer concentration. This is a credit story, not just a stock story.
- **SK Hynix ADR still in price discovery** — No analyst ratings yet on SKHY, very limited liquidity relative to Korean-listed shares.
- **SNAP's Perplexity deal may be overhyped** — $400M over 1 year is real, but Snap is unprofitable and the deal doesn't change the long-term revenue trajectory in a fundamental way. +15% move may fade.
- **Oil at $80+ is a regime change if sustained** — $80 WTI before the Strait of Hormuz blockade was already elevated. If the blockade persists, $90+ oil would be a serious macro headwind for tech multiples.
- **CPI at 3.5% is still well above the 2% target** — One month of cooling is not enough to declare victory. The 3.5% print is down from 4.2% but still above the June 2025 level. September rate hike probability remains non-trivial.
- **Intel's 464% Y/Y surge is extreme** — The Apple chip partnership is real but still early stage. Monday's -6.1% drop shows how much the stock can give back on any risk-off move.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **CPI relief gives tech a near-term bounce window**, but the AI valuation reckoning is not over — cooler inflation doesn't answer the question of whether hyperscaler capex generates sufficient returns.
2. **Korea AI contagion is the most underappreciated risk** — SK Hynix's record crash shows what happens when an entire market's AI thesis gets questioned simultaneously. The KORU ETF (-20%+) and leveraged chip ETF blowups add a mechanical flow dimension.
3. **TSMC earnings Thursday are the week's defining event** — The $420 support level, record revenue, and forward guidance will set the tone for the entire semiconductor sector. A miss would accelerate the selloff; a beat with confident AI outlook could reverse it.
4. **Oil is the new macro axis** — The Strait of Hormuz blockade changes the backdrop significantly. Every $5 move in WTI shifts Fed rate expectations and growth stock valuations. Watch oil more than any single tech stock today.
5. **Avoid catching falling knives in Oracle and Korean-exposed names** — The credit story on ORCL is genuinely deteriorating (BBB-, -$42B FCF projected), and the Korea selloff has a structural leveraged-ETF unwind component that may not be finished.
