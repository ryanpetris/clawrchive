## 7. Outlook For Today

- **Trading setup:** Strong open expected. Dip buyers returning after holiday weakness. Nasdaq futures +1.2%, semiconductor-led.
- **Main upside catalysts:** Broadcom-Apple deal, AMD momentum, Palantir AI partnership, cooling macro data supporting rate cuts, dip buying after holiday pullback.
- **Main downside risks:** Nvidia Kyber delay weighing on AI hardware sentiment, hedge fund rotation out of tech hardware, elevated valuations in some AI names (PLTR at 44x revenue).
- **Strongest subsectors:** Semiconductors (especially AMD, AVGO, MU), AI software platforms (PLTR), Apple supply chain.
- **Weakest subsectors:** Pure-play AI hardware exposed to Nvidia product cycle delays.
- **Move character:** News-driven (AVGO deal, NVDA delay, PLTR partnership), positioning-driven (hedge fund rotation), macro-driven (rate cut expectations).
- **What would change this view:** Nvidia issuing official guidance on Kyber timeline, stronger-than-expected CPI data, sudden reversal in hedge fund flows.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Nvidia Kyber delay report is unconfirmed by Nvidia** — SemiAnalysis report, company has not officially commented. Could be denied or clarified.
- **Hedge fund selling of tech hardware** is now in its 4th week — trend worth monitoring through earnings season.
- **PLTR at 44x projected 2026 revenue** — elevated valuation leaves little room for error.
- **Tesla's delivery beat initially sold off** — suggests "buy the rumor, sell the news" pattern. Recovery underway but caution warranted.
- **Meta's AI cloud pivot** is unproven at scale; competing with AWS, Azure, GCP is a multi-year effort.
- **DRAM cycle risk** — if hyperscalers pause CapEx, DRAM stocks could see serious downside according to 24/7 Wall St.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Semiconductor rotation is real** — AMD, AVGO, and MU are outperforming NVDA as investors broaden AI chip exposure. The Kyber delay accelerates this trend.
2. **The Broadcom-Apple deal through 2031** removes a major overhang on AVGO's consumer revenue. One of the clearest catalysts today.
3. **Palantir's Nvidia partnership** and analyst upgrades provide fresh momentum after a 38% drawdown — but valuation remains extreme.
4. **Macro tailwind from cooling labor data** supports growth stocks and tech multiples. Wednesday's Fed minutes and Thursday's CPI are the next big tests.
5. **Earnings season ramps up** — Samsung (this week), Meta (Jul 29), Apple (Jul 30), Tesla (late Jul). Positioning ahead of these reports will be key.
