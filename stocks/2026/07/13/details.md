## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected trading setup:** Risk-off open driven by chip sector weakness and U.S.-Iran tensions. Defensives/energy outperforming. Tech-heavy Nasdaq likely underperforms the Dow. Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) -2.6% early points to broad chip weakness.

**Main upside catalysts:**
- Dip buyers step in on NVDA (forward P/E 19x is historically compelling)
- Tech fund inflows ($9.7B last week) suggest structural demand, not panic selling
- CPI/Warsh testimony this week could shift narrative

**Main downside risks:**
- SK Hynix selloff could extend into Tuesday if Korean market reopens lower
- Oil spike feeds inflation fears → higher rates → growth stock compression
- Momentum trade fully unwinding after SOXX's 83% YTD run
- HBM4 shipment slowdown flag is a real demand signal, not just noise

**Subsector strength/weakness:**
- **Weakest:** Memory semis (MU, WDC, SNDK), Korean exposure (KORU), high-multiple AI names
- **Strongest:** Energy (oil services, integrated), defense, possibly mega-cap platforms (AAPL, MSFT, AMZN) as relative safe havens
- **Event-driven plays:** AMD (pre-Advancing AI), TSM (pre-earnings Thursday)

**Nature of moves:** Semiconductor selloff is largely **positioning-driven** (profit-taking after SK Hynix's massive debut run) combined with **macro-driven** (Iran/oil). The underlying AI demand thesis remains intact — this is a tactical pullback, not a structural breakdown.

**What would change this view:**
- **More bullish:** CPI comes in below consensus Tuesday → yields drop, growth stocks rally
- **More bearish:** Warsh signals hawkish tilt on oil/inflation → further tech multiple compression
- **Event risk:** Escalation in U.S.-Iran conflict → risk-off across all equities

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Today is a chip-led risk-off day** — SK Hynix profit-taking and Iran tensions are the dual drivers. This feels tactical (positioning unwind), not structural (AI demand intact).
2. **Memory names are the epicenter** — MU (-5.1%), WDC (-5.4%), and SNDK are most exposed. NVDA's mild drop (-1.35%) relative to Friday's $5.11T market cap shows the selloff is sector-specific, not tech-wide.

4. **AMD's July 22 Advancing AI event is the next major company-specific catalyst** — Zen 6 Venice and Helios could be significant. Watch for any pre-event positioning.
5. **Tactical opportunity in the dip?** NVDA at 19x forward earnings is cheap relative to its growth trajectory, but the SOXX gap to its June high (-11%) suggests more volatility ahead. Patience may be rewarded.
