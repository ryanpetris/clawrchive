## 7. Outlook For Today

**Trading Setup**: Flat to modestly higher open. The two-day inflation relief rally (CPI Tue, PPI Wed) is providing a tailwind for rate-sensitive mega-cap tech and cybersecurity. However, the violent rotation out of semiconductor hardware and into platform software creates a bifurcated tape.

**Upside Catalysts**:
- Continued rotation into mega-cap platforms after inflation data (AAPL, GOOGL, META, MSFT, AMZN)
- Cybersecurity momentum (CRWD, PANW) benefiting from both IBM read-through and rate expectations
- China AI approval theme — BABA, BIDU strength could spill over to US tech with China exposure

**Downside Risks**:
- Memory/storage chip rout could deepen — SK Hynix ADR continues falling Thursday
- Software names could see follow-through selling from IBM shock (NOW, ADBE, CRM)
- Middle East oil spike could reverse the inflation narrative quickly
- AI hardware profit-taking may not be finished — watch DELL, HPE, SMCI for stabilization

**Sector Strength/Light**: Mega-cap platforms > cybersecurity > semiconductors (ex-memory) > AI hardware > software (ex-ORCL) > memory/storage chips

**What Would Change This View**: A hawkish Fed speaker reversing the rate-cut narrative; TSMC earnings disappointing; Iran ceasefire reducing oil prices; another major tech pre-announcement.

- **IBM -25%**: The magnitude suggests potential forced liquidation or fund redemptions. Could create contagion to other legacy tech names
- **CRWD valuation**: At $210.73, stock is ~12% above analyst average PT of $184.32. Insider selling (978 sales, 0 buys in 6 months) is a caution flag at these levels
- **KORU -9.13%**: The 3x leveraged South Korea ETF getting crushed by SK Hynix exposure. -47% in one month. Leverage decay is amplifying losses
- **AAPL at ATH**: Average analyst PT of $317 is below current price of $327.50. KeyBanc downgrade suggests Street is divided
- **SMCI**: $8.8B debt, Taiwan probe unresolved, guidance range $38.9-40.4B is wide — binary risk remains
- **PayPal/Stripe**: Deal is reported as unwelcome — if it collapses, PYPL could give back gains
- **Oil/Iran**: Any escalation could reverse the entire inflation narrative and reset rate expectations

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **The AI trade is fracturing** — mega-cap platforms and cybersecurity are winning while semiconductor hardware and AI infrastructure names correct sharply. This rotation has room to run.
2. **Inflation is the dominant macro driver** — two consecutive soft prints (CPI, PPI) have pulled rate hike probabilities down dramatically. Every data point matters.
3. **TSMC earnings this week are the key catalyst** — will determine whether memory/semiconductor selloff was an overreaction or the start of a deeper correction.
4. **Apple at an all-time high is the new bellwether** — the China AI approval changes the revenue narrative; July 30 earnings will validate the move.
5. **Position sizing matters on high-beta hardware names** — Dell has gained 219% YTD but dropped 14% in one day. Volatility is not risk-adjusted in these names.
