- **Trading setup:** Cautious open. Nasdaq-100 futures -0.6% as the market digests the SK hynix capital event. Memory names likely to face rotation pressure.
- **Main upside catalysts:** Semiconductor sector momentum from Thursday (+3.06% PHLX) could carry if SKHY debut goes well. META and TSLA momentum may hold.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Thursday's rally on thin volume** — 14.7B shares vs. 22.9B 20-day average. Low-conviction tape.
- **SK hynix IPO pricing** — 7x oversubscribed is strong but doesn't guarantee a successful first day of trading.
- **TSLA SpaceX merger speculation** — unconfirmed. RBC's $500 PT bakes in a 25-30% premium. If merger doesn't materialize, the stock loses that premium.
- **PLTR Burry short rumors** — unconfirmed. Stock already -27% YTD; further downside from rumor alone seems partially priced.
- **SMCI dilution** — 24-28% potential share increase from preferred conversion + ATM is a real per-share value risk regardless of business performance.
- **INTC at 158x forward earnings on negative EPS** — the 226% YTD rally is narrative-driven. Foundry external customer wins are the only catalyst that can sustain it.
- **CPI week is pivotal** — a hot June CPI print ahead of the July 29 FOMC could reset rate expectations sharply higher, hitting growth/tech multiples. Oil dropped 20% in June so headline should moderate, but core remains sticky.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Today is the SK hynix allocation test** — the $26.5B memory IPO is the biggest event in tech this week. How SKHY trades on day one determines near-term direction for the broader semiconductor group.
2. **META is the most compelling setup** — +4.6% Thursday, 22x trailing earnings, 33% revenue growth, $12B quarterly FCF, Iris chip on track, Muse Spark 1.1 live. The AI CapEx is translating to ad revenue.
3. **TSLA is a binary trade ahead of July 22 earnings** — reclaimed $400 on upgrades and speculation, but 200x+ P/E means any July earnings miss (especially on margins) triggers a sharp re-rating.
4. **SMCI dilution overhang is real** — 7.6% bounce is mostly sector beta, not company-specific. The 24-28% equity overhang is a structural headwind until resolved.
5. **Macro takes center stage next week** — CPI, PPI, Warsh testimony, Retail Sales. This week's AI-hype tone could shift abruptly if data runs hot and Warsh sounds more hawkish than expected.
