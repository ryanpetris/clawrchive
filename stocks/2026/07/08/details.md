# Tech Stock Briefing - July 8, 2026 - Iran Escalation, Fed Minutes, Apple-Broadcom $30B Deal

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Market tone: Risk-off / Geopolitically Driven** — Nasdaq futures tumbling sharply premarket after President Trump declared the Iran ceasefire "effectively over," triggering fresh Middle East strikes and a 5-6% oil surge. Energy stocks rallying (XLE +2%+), tech broadly under pressure.
- **Iran ceasefire collapses** — Trump announced the tentative ceasefire with Iran is over, with both countries exchanging fresh strikes. The U.S. Treasury revoked Iran's authorization to sell oil. Oil prices jumped 5-6% (WTI above $72, Brent ~$76). Travel and airline stocks declining on fuel cost concerns.
- **Apple-Broadcom $30B chip deal announced** — Apple and Broadcom confirmed an expanded multi-year agreement expected to exceed $30 billion, extending through 2031. Broadcom will invest $1.5B to upgrade its Fort Collins, CO facility. AVGO surged ~4% on the news, AAPL up modestly. This is Apple's largest U.S. manufacturing commitment.
- **Fed minutes drop at 2:00 PM ET today** — The June 16-17 FOMC minutes carry unusual weight because Chair Kevin Warsh was deliberately opaque in his press conference. The dot plot showed 9 of 18 officials expect at least one rate hike in 2026 — a hawkish reversal from March. Markets pricing rates toward 4% by year-end.
- **Semiconductor sell-off accelerating** — AMD dropped ~6-7%, MU fell ~4-5%, TSM declined ~4%, INTC fell further from recent losses (down ~9.7% on Tuesday). The SOXX semiconductor index broadly lower as profit-taking and Iran risk hit the sector. Broadcom was a notable standout on the Apple deal.
- **PLTR extended 7-day winning streak** — Palantir surged 24% over 6 sessions, adding ~$60B in market cap, driven by the NVIDIA sovereign AI partnership, Army contracts, and the GNP Seguros Mexico insurance expansion deal. Prediction markets peg 76.5% odds PLTR touches $138 in July.
- **SpaceX (SPCX) stumbles in Nasdaq 100 debut** — SPCX fell ~7% on its first day as a Nasdaq 100 member, trading around $150, well below its post-IPO high of $225. Options market pricing ~8% swing by week end.
- **Key stocks to watch today:** AVGO (Apple deal catalyst), NVDA (semiconductor sell-off, DeepSeek AI chip reports), PLTR (momentum vs valuation), TSLA (premarket weakness, earnings July 22), INTC (BofA bubble warning, 18A delay overhang).
- **CPI data July 14 is the next major catalyst** — The June CPI print will be the first major inflation data since the hawkish June FOMC meeting and will heavily influence whether a September rate hike remains on the table.

  - *Impact:* **Bearish** for tech broadly; **Bullish** for energy

### AI and Data Center

- **Apple-Broadcom $30B+ chip deal confirmed** — Apple announced an expanded multi-year agreement with Broadcom valued at over $30 billion, extending through 2031. Broadcom will invest $1.5B to expand its Fort Collins, CO facility for RF components. The deal addresses the long-standing Apple in-sourcing overhang on AVGO shares.
  - *Why it matters:* Cements Broadcom's role as a key Apple supplier. Apple's custom chip ambitions ("Baltra" server processor) coexist with Broadcom supply. Largest U.S. chip manufacturing commitment to date.
  - *Impact:* **Bullish** for AVGO; **Mixed/Positive** for AAPL
  - *Source:* [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/apple-aapl-chip-deal-with-broadcom-avgo-expected-to-exceed-30-billion), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/apple-commits-30-billion-to-broadcom-for-us-chipmaking-push.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/apple-announces-chip-deal-with-broadcom-worth-more-than-30-billion-100000097.html)

- **DeepSeek developing own AI chip — NVDA overhang** — Reports indicate Chinese AI lab DeepSeek is developing its own AI chip, adding competitive pressure to NVIDIA's dominant position. NVDA fell 0.8% Tuesday and was down ~2% in early trading.
  - *Why it matters:* Any credible alternative to NVIDIA's CUDA ecosystem in the Chinese market could threaten NVDA's data center GPU monopoly, though DeepSeek's chip is likely years from production.
  - *Impact:* **Slightly Bearish** for NVDA; low confidence given early stage
  - *Source:* [moomoo](https://www.moomoo.com/community/feed/u-s-market-outlook-nvidia-s-stock-price-fell-following-116878953480198)

**Main Upside Catalysts:**
- Apple-Broadcom $30B deal providing a floor for AVGO and support for AAPL
- Energy sector rotation could limit overall index damage
- If Fed minutes are less hawkish than feared, a midday reversal is possible

**Main Downside Risks:**
- Further Iran escalation (oil above $75+ would accelerate tech selling)
- Hawkish Fed minutes confirming September rate hike trajectory
- Cascading stop-losses in overextended semi names (AMD, MU)
- VIX spike triggering systematic de-risking

**Strongest/Weakest Subsectors:**
- **Strongest:** Energy (XLE), defense tech, select semi names with specific catalysts (AVGO)
- **Weakest:** Semiconductors (broad), consumer tech, high-multiple growth, memory chips

**Nature of Moves:** Today's moves are predominantly **geopolitical/macro-driven**. The semiconductor sell-off also reflects structural profit-taking after massive YTD gains. The Apple-Broadcom deal is a genuine stock-specific catalyst creating divergence.

**What Would Change This View:**
- De-escalation in Iran (unlikely intraday)
- Fed minutes significantly less or more hawkish than priced
- Major company-specific news (M&A, earnings pre-announcement)

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Iran situation is fluid** — ceasefire collapse news is fresh and markets are still pricing initial shock. Further escalation or diplomatic intervention could cause sharp reversals.
- **Fed minutes wildcard** — Warsh's deliberate opacity means the minutes are the only "on-record" committee view. The market may react strongly to details on hawkish dot concentrations.

- **PLTR valuation extreme** — P/E of ~149x with zero room for execution error. Insiders sold heavily at $132-136. The 7-day winning streak could reverse sharply.
- **SPCX options market active** — Options pricing suggests ~8% swing by week end. The Nasdaq 100 addition created unusual options activity. Exercise caution.
- **Inflation expectations rising** — NY Fed survey shows 1-year inflation expectations at 3.7% (multi-year high). CPI on July 14 is the key test.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Iran dominates today:** The ceasefire collapse is the primary market driver. Tech is getting hit broadly as capital rotates into energy. Don't chase semi weakness until the geopolitical picture stabilizes.
2. **Apple-Broadcom is a genuine bright spot:** The $30B+ deal removes the Apple in-sourcing overhang for AVGO and demonstrates Apple's continued U.S. manufacturing commitment. Both names are relative havens in today's environment.
3. **Fed minutes this afternoon:** The 2:00 PM ET release is the day's second key event. Hawkish confirmation would deepen the sell-off; a more balanced tone could trigger a recovery rally.
4. **PLTR momentum is real but fragile:** The 24% run in 6 sessions is impressive, but at 149x earnings, there's no margin for error. Watch for the run to exhaust near $138.
5. **CPI on July 14 looms large:** Inflation data will determine whether the September rate hike stays on the table. This is the next major macro catalyst that will set the tone for tech into Q2 earnings season.
