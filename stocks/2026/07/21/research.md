### TSMC Structural Analysis
- **Break-even at 25% utilization** vs. 90% industry average — unprecedented structural moat
- **$52-56B 2026 capex** (raised to $60-64B on Q2 call) creates per-wafer fixed cost collapse
- **CoWoS sold out through 2026** — the bottleneck is migrating from wafer fab to advanced packaging
- **Pricing power:** 5-10% price increase on 3nm/5nm/7nm absorbed without resistance
- **Citi estimates:** $75B capex in 2027, $80B in 2028 — treating current record as floor
- **Risk:** If capex cycle outpaces demand ramp, cycle math shifts to customers
- **2nm (N2):** GAA transistor structure, risk production late 2025, mass production 2027. Apple confirmed as launch customer for M5/A19 chips
