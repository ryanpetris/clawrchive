Yet the stock fell ~4% on the day — a telling sign of market fatigue with AI capex narratives.

- **Tesla (TSLA) — reports Wednesday July 22 after close.** Expected Q2 revenue ~$27.6B. Key focus: Robotaxi expansion (Miami launched), FSD progress, HW3 upgrade plans, and AI chip development. Options imply ~7% swing.
- **Intel (INTC) — reports Thursday July 23 after close.** Stock is up >160% YTD, trading ~$95. Q2 revenue consensus ~$14.4B, EPS ~$0.21. Foundry yields at 85%, 18A output ahead of schedule. The rally has priced in a lot of good news.
- **Iran conflict escalation weighs on sentiment.** Trump reinstated Strait of Hormuz blockade, oil prices surged. Two U.S. service members killed, Iran called off interim peace deal. The 10Y Treasury yield fell to 4.537% on safe-haven demand. Gold rose to ~$4,005/oz.
- **Macro data this week:** Leading Indicators (Mon), Existing Home Sales (Wed), S&P Global PMI data (Fri). The cooling inflation narrative (CPI fell 0.4% in June, first monthly drop since 2020) provides a tailwind, but the Iran conflict complicates the outlook.
- **Netflix (NFLX) dropped ~8% Friday** after in-line Q2 results and soft Q3 revenue guidance — a warning that even "good enough" may not be enough in this environment.

## 6. Setup For The Next Open and Final Market Day

### Expected Trading Tone at Monday Open

**Risk-off / Cautious** — The semiconductor bear market, Moonshot AI shock, and Iran conflict escalation create a heavy backdrop. However, the cooling inflation data (CPI fell 0.4% in June) provides a macro cushion. The week ahead is earnings-driven, so tone may shift daily.

### Main Upside Catalysts

1. **Alphabet and Tesla earnings beat** — both have strong revision momentum. If they deliver, the AI capex narrative gets a reset.
2. **Intel earnings upside** — foundry progress could surprise positively; 18A yields ahead of schedule is a genuine catalyst.
3. **Dip-buying / positioning** — memory stocks showed signs of a Friday comeback; options activity suggests dip-buying interest in semis.
4. **Cooling inflation narrative** — June CPI and PPI came in softer, keeping the Fed on hold and supporting rate-sensitive tech valuations.
5. **TSMC's underlying demand signal** — 52-78 week CoWoS lead times mean the AI buildout is real, regardless of the market's mood.

### Main Downside Risks

1. **Leverage unwind continues** — Goldman notes record leverage across retail margin, levered ETFs, and short-dated options. Further unwinding could amplify the selloff.
2. **Moonshot K3 opens July 27** — the pending weight release is a sword of Damocles over EDA/design tools. If K3's claims hold up, the repricing of EDA and chip design will accelerate.
3. **Alphabet Gemini 3.5 delay** — if Bloomberg's "months behind" report is accurate, Alphabet's AI narrative takes a hit, and the stock could fall further.
4. **Iran conflict escalation** — oil prices rising, Strait of Hormuz disruption, and potential for a broader Middle East conflict increase stagflation risk.
5. **"Good enough" is not enough** — Netflix's drop on in-line results shows the market is punishing any disappointment in tech.

## 7. Bottom Line

1. **This week is the biggest test of the AI trade since the selloff began.** Alphabet and Tesla earnings directly address whether AI capex is generating returns. If they deliver, the semi bear market could be a buying opportunity. If they disappoint, the correction deepens.

2. **TSMC's record quarter is the fundamental floor under the AI thesis.** $40.2B in revenue, 77% net profit growth, 52-78 week CoWoS lead times — the demand is real. The question is whether the market cares about fundamentals or sentiment right now.

3. **Moonshot K3 is a structural risk that's not fully priced outside of EDA.** The July 27 weight release is the next catalyst. If K3's claims reproduce, the chip-design value chain is disrupted. If not, Friday's EDA selloff was an overreaction. Either way, it's a binary event.

4. **The macro backdrop is two-sided.** Cooling inflation (June CPI fell for first time since 2020) is a tailwind for tech multiples. But the Iran conflict is pushing oil higher and raising stagflation risk. The Fed is on hold through 2026.

5. **Intel's earnings are the sleeper catalyst.** The stock has rallied 160% YTD on foundry turnaround hopes. Thursday's report needs to show tangible progress — 18A yields, customer wins, path to profitability — or the rally is vulnerable to a sharp reversal.
