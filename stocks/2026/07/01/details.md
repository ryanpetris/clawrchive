Meta relied on Gemini for content moderation and fraud detection; now forced to accelerate its own Muse Spark model development. Google signed a $30B deal with SpaceX for compute capacity starting June. **Impact:** bearish for META AI capability in near term, bullish for GOOGL pricing power. ## 8. Risk Notes

- **Unconfirmed claims:** The Rubin Ultra cancellation has not been officially confirmed by Nvidia. SemiAnalysis is a credible semi research firm, but Nvidia may issue a clarification. Consider the cancellation "confirmed by independent research, unconfirmed by Nvidia."
- **Burry shorts:** Michael Burry has a history of early but premature short calls (famously covering his 2024 Nvidia short at a loss before the stock declined). His timing is notoriously unreliable, though his thematic concerns about Korea overinvestment have merit.
- **Intel 250% YTD rally:** Priced on expectations 12-18 months out. Any 18A yield delay could trigger a 25-30% drawdown per analyst estimates. July 23 earnings are a binary catalyst.
- **Meta's situation:** High AI CapEx ($145B) with lagging model performance vs peers. Internal turmoil and talent departures. The employee monitoring controversy could expand into regulatory scrutiny.
- **Q2 rally exhaustion risk:** S&P 500 +15%, Nasdaq +21% in Q2 — best since Q2 2020. Momentum often cools after such strong quarters. July/August historically weaker months for tech.
- **Geopolitical risk:** Iran diplomatic impasse could escalate. Oil prices sensitive (WTI currently ~$69). Growth stocks typically sell off on sustained oil spikes.
- **News that may be stale or already priced in:** Nvidia's earnings beat, big tech capex numbers for 2026, the overall AI narrative — these are well-understood and likely priced into current valuations.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Semis are the place to be, but positioning is getting crowded.** The $1.3T semiconductor TAM, agentic AI CPU renaissance, and massive hyperscaler spending provide fundamental support, but Burry's call and SOXX +111% H1 gains suggest near-term consolidation risk.
2. **AMD vs. NVDA is the defining rivalry.** AMD at $947B market cap with 7.7% Tuesday surge challenges Jensen Huang's dismissal of AMD's prospects. Wells Fargo's $615 PT signals institutional conviction. The Rubin Ultra cancellation narrows NVDA's 2027 competitive moat.
3. **Don't ignore Meta's internal risks.** $145B in AI spending with lagging models, 8,000 layoffs, an employee surveillance scandal, and key AI talent departures is a dangerous combination. The stock needs proof of AI payoff soon.
4. **Intel is pricing perfection.** The 250% YTD rally prices in foundry success that is 12-18 months away. July 23 earnings are make-or-break for the narrative. Until then, caution on chasing at $140.
5. **Today's macro data (ISM/ADP) and Fed Warsh speech set the Q3 tone.** A favorable ISM print + dovish Warsh could extend the AI-led rally. A miss + hawkish tone could trigger July/August weakness.
