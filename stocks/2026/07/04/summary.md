## Top Themes

1. **Semiconductor profit-taking continues** — SOX fell 11% over two days, memory stocks crushed (SanDisk -23% in two sessions). The AI scarcity narrative cracked after Meta signaled it will sell excess compute capacity.
2. **Massive sector rotation underway** — Dow hit a record 52,900 while Nasdaq fell 0.8% on Thursday. Money rotating out of AI chips into financials, healthcare, consumer staples, and value.
3. **Soft jobs data changes rate narrative** — June nonfarm payrolls +57K vs 115K expected. Rate-hike odds for July collapsed from ~29% to below 20%. Fed minutes Wednesday will be the big test.
4. **Apple +4.8% on supply chain news** — Reports Apple is negotiating to buy memory chips from Chinese suppliers CXMT/YMTC to ease component cost pressure. Separately seeking alternative hardware channels to bypass Nvidia pricing.
5. **Meta surging on cloud pivot** — Reports Meta is building a cloud business to sell excess AI compute capacity, re-rating the stock as a potential neo-cloud competitor.
6. **Tesla delivered record Q2 volumes (480,126) but sold off -8%** — classic sell-the-news. US demand fell ~20% after EV credit expiry; Europe +108% did the heavy lifting.
7. **FOMC minutes Wednesday key catalyst** — First meeting under Chair Kevin Warsh. Market watching for views on dropping forward guidance, inflation risks, and rate path signals.
8. **Earnings season kicks off** — PepsiCo (Thu) and Delta (Fri) unofficially start Q2 reporting. Megacap tech earnings (TSLA, GOOGL, META, MSFT, AAPL, AMZN) land July 21-25.
