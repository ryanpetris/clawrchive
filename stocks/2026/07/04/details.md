## 5. Sector Outlook

### Semiconductors
- **Status:** Under severe profit-taking pressure. SOX fell 11% in two days. Memory stocks crushed (SanDisk -14% Thursday, -23% in two sessions; Micron -5.5% Thursday, -10.6% for week; Marvell -10% in a single session).
- **Bull case:** AI capex cycle is still accelerating. Amazon guiding $100B+ capex, Microsoft deploying $2.5B Frontier Company unit. Demand for HBM, networking silicon, and AI accelerators is real and growing. The selloff is positioning-driven, not fundamental.
- **Bear case:** Meta's cloud pivot cracked the AI scarcity narrative — if hyperscalers can sell excess compute, pricing power for chip suppliers weakens. Memory pricing may have peaked. Rising rates punish extended duration trades. Apple seeking Chinese memory chips suggests component costs are too high.
- **Key tickers:** NVDA, AMD, INTC, AVGO, TSM, ASML, ARM, MU, QCOM, MRVL, SNDK
- **Catalysts:** SK Hynix ADR debut Friday; FOMC minutes Wednesday; ISM Services Monday

### AI Infrastructure
- **Status:** Under pressure as part of the broader semi unwind. SMCI, DELL, HPE, ANET all caught in rotation out of AI capex beneficiaries.
- **Bull case:** Enterprise AI deployment is early-stage. Microsoft's $2.5B Frontier Company unit and Amazon's $100B+ capex guidance show demand is accelerating, not peaking.
- **Bear case:** If hyperscalers (META, AMZN, MSFT) start selling compute rather than consuming it all internally, the infrastructure buildout thesis needs recalibrating.
- **Key tickers:** SMCI, DELL, HPE, ANET, VRT

### Mega-Cap Platforms
- **Status:** Mixed. META (+9%) surging on cloud pivot. AAPL (+4.8%) on China supply chain news. AMZN stable. MSFT mixed.
- **Bull case:** Advertising revenue remains strong (META, GOOGL). Cloud growth reaccelerating (AMZN AWS, MSFT Azure). AI monetization becoming visible. Apple's supply chain moves show active margin management.
- **Bear case:** AI capex spending is massive ($100B+ for AMZN alone) and ROI is not yet proven. Rising component costs hit hardware margins. Regulatory and antitrust risks persist.
- **Key tickers:** AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META

### Cloud and Software
- **Status:** Relative outperformer. PLTR upgraded by DA Davidson. Adobe, FICO, and SaaS names gained as capital rotated out of semis into software.
- **Bull case:** Enterprise software is the actual AI monetization layer. AI integration is driving margin expansion (Oracle layoffs, SAP hiring freeze). Palantir's FDE model being adopted by Microsoft.
- **Bear case:** AI displacement fears are real for traditional software. Token-cost economics could eat margins.
- **Key tickers:** ORCL, CRM, ADBE, NOW, SNOW, PLTR

### Cybersecurity
- **Status:** Quiet this week relative to the semi chaos. No major catalyst.
- **Key tickers:** PANW, CRWD, DDOG, NET

### EV and Autonomy
- **Status:** Divergent. Tesla delivered record Q2 volumes (480,126) but sold off -8% on US demand concerns. Rivian jumped 10% after raising production guidance. Lucid restructuring.
- **Bull case:** Global EV adoption is still growing, especially Europe (+108% for TSLA). Rivian's R2 platform could unlock mass-market demand. Energy storage is a growing profit center for TSLA.
- **Bear case:** US EV demand is softening post-credit expiry. Chinese OEMs (BYD, etc.) are exporting affordable EVs globally. TSLA valuation remains high. Lucid is struggling.
- **Key tickers:** TSLA, RIVN, LCID

### Tech ETFs
- **QQQ:** +0.22% in after-hours Friday. Futures suggested a +1.18% NQ bounce. Watch for semi exposure drag.
- **SOXX/SMH:** Under severe pressure. Two-day SOX drawdown of 11% is significant; a stabilization this week would be constructive.
- **XLK:** Technology sector weighting means semi weakness drags, offset by AAPL and MSFT.

- Iran war ceasefire/negotiations are a significant de-escalation that removes a major macro tail risk.

### How This Affects Growth Stocks, Semis, and Mega-Cap Tech
- **Lower rates = good for long-duration growth** (semis, AI infrastructure). The soft jobs report reduced rate-hike risk but the semi unwind still happened — confirming it was positioning-driven, not macro-driven.
- **Lower oil = disinflationary** = supportive for consumer spending and ultimately tech advertising revenue.
- **Weaker dollar = supportive** for mega-cap tech with large international revenue exposure (AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN).
- **Risk:** If the bond market continues to sell off (yields rising), that would punish semis further and could broaden into all long-duration growth assets, including biotech and software.

## 9. Final Weekly Outlook

### Best-Looking Setups
1. **META** — The cloud pivot thesis could be a genuine re-rating catalyst. If the market buys it, META trades less like a cyclical advertiser and more like a long-duration infrastructure play.
2. **AAPL** — Supply chain diversification + price hike pass-through = margin protection story. +4.8% Thursday with follow-through potential if China memory chip approval progresses.
3. **PLTR** — Analyst upgrade + concrete government AI deployment partnership with Nvidia. Enterprise software rotation beneficiary.
4. **Dow/Financials rotation** — The Dow's four-week winning streak may continue as money rotates out of tech into value, financials, and healthcare.

### Biggest Risks
1. **FOMC minutes (Wed)** — A hawkish surprise (hike still on table) would reignite semi selling and could broaden into all growth.
2. **ISM Services miss (Mon)** — While a miss is rate-dovish, a sharp contraction would raise recession concerns and hurt the rotation thesis.
3. **Continued semi unwind** — If the SOX selling doesn't stabilize this week, it becomes a structural correction, not a rotation.
4. **Yield rise persistence** — The bond market's reaction during the selloff (yields up) is the new variable. If yields keep rising, the correction broadens into all long-duration growth.
5. **Credit market crack** — If high-yield spreads widen significantly, "rotation" becomes "risk-off" for real.

### Setup for Monday Open
- Nasdaq 100 futures indicated **+1.18%** on Friday's holiday session, suggesting a potential bounce.
- S&P 500 futures +0.40% also constructive.
- However, the S&P 500 enters the week pinned at 7,483, just 17 points below 7,500. A gap open above 7,500 would be technically bullish; a failure to hold near there would confirm resistance.
- The three-day weekend means accumulated headlines flow in at once. OPEC+ meeting, NATO summit prep, and any geopolitical developments over the holiday will set the tone.
- **Key levels to watch:** QQQ bounce above Friday's close; SOX stabilization vs further decline; NVDA as the AI bellwether.

### Key Data/Events That Could Change the Outlook
- **ISM Services PMI > 55:** Would reduce recession fears but reignite rate-hike concerns — mixed for tech.
- **ISM Services PMI < 48:** Recession signal — bad for all equities including tech.
- **FOMC minutes with explicit dovish tilt:** Major relief rally for semis and tech growth.
- **FOMC minutes with hawkish surprise:** Continued semis pressure, rotation accelerated.
- **SK Hynix ADR strong debut:** Bullish read-through for memory/AI semis.

### 5 Takeaways
1. **The AI/semi unwind is a rotation, not a recession** — bond yields rose during the selloff, confirming positioning and valuation repricing rather than a growth scare. But this needs to stabilize to avoid becoming structural.
2. **This week's events are make-or-break for the rotation narrative** — ISM Services (Mon), FOMC Minutes (Wed), and SK Hynix ADR debut (Fri) will collectively determine whether semis bounce or break further.
3. **META and AAPL are the tech bright spots** — META's cloud pivot, AAPL's supply chain agility; both offer narratives that insulate them from the semi positioning unwind.
4. **Megacap tech earnings (July 21-25) are the real prize** — this week is a setup week. How the market positions into earnings will define Q3.
5. **Watch the bond market** — If yields stabilize, the semi unwind burns out. If yields keep rising, the correction broadens into all long-duration growth. The credit market is the canary.
