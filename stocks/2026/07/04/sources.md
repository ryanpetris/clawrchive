## Source 5: The Week the AI Trade Broke — Investment Gems

**URL:** https://investmentgems.net/2026/07/03/the-week-the-ai-trade-broke-and-why-the-data-says-rotation-not-recession/

**Extract:**
> The entire semiconductor complex, the group that had led everything, fell 10% to 14%. Micron, Sandisk, KLA, Lam, the whole AI-infrastructure trade. KLA broke down 12% one day and 11.5% the next, both on 2× normal volume.
>
> Right as the semis cracked, the bond market flipped. For weeks, yields had been falling (bonds bid), the gentle disinflation backdrop. On the exact two days the AI trade broke, that reversed: yields started rising.
>
> That reframes the entire correction. A rising discount rate hits the most expensive, longest-duration, most crowded trade the hardest. Add the catalyst (Meta signaling it will sell compute, cracking the AI-scarcity story) and you get a textbook positioning-and-valuation unwind.
>
> In a recession trade, terrified money floods into Treasuries and yields fall. They rose. The bond market voted "repricing," not "recession."

**Key insight:** Bond market reaction confirms positioning unwind, not growth scare. Yields rose during semi selloff.
