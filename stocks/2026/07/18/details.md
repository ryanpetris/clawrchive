## 4. Earnings Preview

### Alphabet (GOOGL) — Wednesday, July 22, after close

**The most consequential report of the quarter for tech.**

| Metric | Consensus | Key Focus |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Revenue | ~$89B | Google Services, Cloud growth |
| EPS | ~$2.05 | Margin trajectory |
| AI Capex | Guided $185B for 2026 | **Any moderation = massive sector read-through** |

**What the market is pricing:** The AI capex supercycle is the foundational assumption for semi valuations. Alphabet doubling its AI spend to $185B has been a key driver of the bull case. The Kimi K3 launch and Buffett's "playing a game they don't want to play" comments have shaken confidence in the capex ROI thesis.

**What would be a beat:** Revenue and EPS beats are likely expected. The market needs to hear (a) that AI capex will remain elevated, (b) that Cloud growth is accelerating meaningfully, and (c) that Search and ad revenue remain resilient despite AI competition.

**What would be a miss:** Any signal of AI capex moderation, even if justified by efficiency gains, would be read as demand weakness. Cloud growth deceleration would compound the negative narrative.

**Read-throughs:** Bullish GOOGL → bullish NVDA, AMD, semis broadly. GOOGL bearish → likely another leg down for semis, AI infrastructure plays (SMCI, DELL, VRT, ANET).
