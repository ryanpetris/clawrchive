## 2. Executive Summary

- **Market tone: Rotation continues.** The two-week rotation out of semiconductors into mega-cap platforms and value accelerated through last week. The Dow hit a new all-time high; SOXX fell ~4%. The question is whether this is a healthy broadening or the start of a deeper unwind in the AI trade.
- **Apple is closing in on Nvidia.** AAPL surged ~9% last week on reports of an expanded iPhone lineup (potentially 5 models, including a foldable). AAPL now trails NVDA in market cap by roughly $190B (~4%). Nvidia's next catalyst is late August earnings, leaving it at the mercy of AI sentiment.
- **AMD's Meta-driven selloff was the week's biggest single-story market event.** AMD fell 6.9% in one day (~$65B market cap) after Bloomberg reported Meta plans to sell surplus AI compute. AMD's anchor AI customer with excess capacity is a structurally bearish signal, amplified by a Northland Capital downgrade.
- **June payrolls came in cooler than expected,** easing immediate Fed rate-hike fears. The probability of a July hike fell to ~20% from 30%. The Fed remains at 3.50%–3.75%, with next FOMC on July 28–29.
- **SpaceX joins the Nasdaq-100 on Tuesday, July 7.** JPMorgan estimates $4.3B in passive inflows. SPCX inclusion is the biggest index event of the week and will drive fund-level rebalancing across the Nasdaq 100.
- **Palantir-Nvidia sovereign AI partnership continues to generate headlines.** Palantir CEO Karp's critique of token-based AI billing and the joint air-gapped AI stack for government clients drove PLTR up ~8-9%. This is a structural positive for both PLTR and NVDA in the defense/government AI vertical.
- **FOMC minutes from the June 16-17 meeting release Wednesday, July 8.** Under Chair Warsh's first FOMC and with softer jobs data, the minutes will be parsed closely for any sign of a July cut path. Current market pricing: 20% probability of a hike, no cuts priced.
- **Q2 earnings season begins the following week with bank earnings.** Tech names start reporting in late July (AAPL July 30, MSFT late July, META late July). This week is light on earnings — a breather before the onslaught.

## 6. Setup For The Next Open And Final Market Day

### Expected Trading Tone At Monday's Open

**Cautiously mixed to slightly risk-on.** The holiday-shortened week left positioning unclear. The June payroll miss should support rates-sensitive tech (lower yields = higher growth-stock multiples), but the semi rotation has momentum. Key intraweek variable: ISM Services PMI on Monday.

### Main Upside Catalysts
- **FOMC minutes (Wed):** Any dovish lean from the June meeting — especially with weaker payrolls now in hand — could reignite growth-stock momentum
- **SpaceX Nasdaq-100 inclusion (Tue):** $4.3B passive flows; halo effect on related names (ASTS, VSAT, RIVN)
- **ISM Services above 50:** Would confirm economic resilience, supporting the broadening narrative
- **Mag 7 momentum:** If MSFT, AAPL, GOOGL continue their bounce, it could stabilize the Nasdaq and pull semis higher

### Main Downside Risks
- **ISM Services below 50:** Would be a macro shock, hitting growth names hardest
- **Continued semi rotation:** If semi selling accelerates, NVDA below $190 could trigger stop-loss cascades
- **FOMC minutes hawkish:** If Warsh's committee expressed firm higher-for-longer resolve, it would pressure rate-sensitive tech
- **Meta compute story escalation:** If Meta confirms or expands its surplus capacity narrative, AMD and the broader AI hardware complex could see further selling
- **Middle East headlines / oil:** A geopolitical escalation could shift focus away from tech entirely

### Subsector Strength/Weakness
- **Strongest:** Mega-cap platforms (AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL), defense/sovereign AI (PLTR, NVDA in government verticals), space (SPCX, ASTS)
- **Weakest:** Memory semis (MU, SK Hynix read-through), AMD and AMD-adjacent plays, overbought Financials
- **Neutral:** Cloud/software names ahead of earnings season

### What Would Change This View
- **ISM Services miss (<48)** → broadly bearish, risk-off, tech sold across the board
- **FOMC minutes with explicit rate-cut discussion** → strongly bullish for duration-sensitive tech, semis, and mega-caps
- **Meta earnings (next week) with upward capex revision** → would neutralize the surplus compute narrative and support AMD
- **NVDA AI Factory deal flow acceleration** — would add a new revenue-model thesis and could halt the semi unwind

## 7. Bottom Line

1. **The rotation from semis to mega-cap platforms is the week's central narrative.** It's not yet clear whether this is a healthy broadening or a structural unwind. Monday's ISM Services data is the next major data point.
2. **AMD is the highest-risk name this week.** Meta's surplus compute revelation and the Northland downgrade create a two-front headwind. The MI450 timing and Meta's late-July earnings are the next catalysts.
3. **NVDA at 22x forward earnings is the most glaring value anomaly in the market.** Record revenue, $91B guidance, but sentiment-driven selling. The Burry GPU depreciation critique is the credible bear case; the "healthy pause" thesis is supported by fundamentals.
4. **SpaceX joining Nasdaq-100 on Tuesday is the week's cleanest event-driven trade.** $4.3B in passive inflows is a known quantity, but the rebalancing may create volatility in related names.
5. **FOMC minutes on Wednesday are the macro wild card.** With weaker payrolls in the rearview, the market will be hypersensitive to any dovish signal from Warsh's first FOMC. A hawkish read would compound the semi headwind.
