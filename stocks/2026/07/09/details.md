# Tech Stock Briefing - July 9, 2026 - Semis Bounce, Iran Tensions, Meta Cloud Pivot

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Market tone:** Mixed with a semiconductor-led bounce. Nasdaq futures +0.9%, S&P 500 futures +0.3%, Dow flat. Semis outperform as dip-buyers step in after the $2 trillion chip sell-off.
- **U.S.-Iran tensions escalate but crude flattens.** The U.S. launched fresh strikes on Iran after Tehran attacked commercial shipping. Trump said he called Iran to negotiate, flattening crude futures after the initial spike. Wells Fargo says geopolitical risk premium in oil will persist.
- **Semiconductor sector rebounds.** SMH +3% premarket led by Micron (+5%) and Sandisk (+4%). The SOX index bounced 2% on Wednesday. Analysts characterize the sell-off as a correction within a bull market, with Wedbush's Dan Ives calling AI infrastructure buildout "third inning."
- **AMD +3% premarket** after Goldman Sachs reiterated Buy with a $640 price target. The stock trades above all major moving averages with neutral RSI.
- **Meta's AI cloud pivot is the week's biggest story.** Zuckerberg announced Meta will sell excess AI compute capacity, surging the stock 9% on the news. CoreWeave fell 14% on losing a customer/competitor threat.
- **Broadcom secured $30B+ in Apple AI chip orders**, driving AVGO higher. This reinforces the custom ASIC narrative.
- **DeepSeek building its own AI chip** — Reuters report. NVDA slipped 2% on the news. Unconfirmed but adds to the "hyperscalers building custom silicon" narrative.
- **Stocks most likely to move today:** AMD (analyst upgrade + semi bounce), MU (memory sector rebound leader), CRM (KeyBanc downgrade), META (cloud pivot momentum), AVGO (Apple order news).

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Trading setup:** Mixed open with a semiconductor-led Nasdaq bid. The geopolitical overlay (Iran strikes) creates a risk-off bid in oil and defense but tech is looking through it for now. The bounce in semis is the defining feature of premarket.

**Upside catalysts:**
- Dip-buyers returning to semis after the $2T sell-off
- Mu +5% premarket suggests memory sell-off may have been overdone
- Goldman Sachs AMD upgrade adding momentum to the semi recovery
- META cloud pivot could drive rotation back into mega-cap tech
- China potentially easing H200 restrictions — positive for NVDA

**Downside risks:**
- Iran escalation could worsen intraday, spiking oil and crushing risk appetite
- Salesforce downgrade dragging on software/saas sentiment
- CRM premarket -4.5% could weigh on enterprise software names
- VIX remains elevated — options market pricing in more volatility
- Palantir political risk FT article creating headlines

**Strongest subsectors:** Semiconductors (bouncing), AI infrastructure (META catalyst), memory (reversal trade)

**Weakest subsectors:** Enterprise software (CRM drag), AV/autonomy (NHTSA), consumer discretionary (PEP miss)

**Nature of moves:** The semi bounce is positioning-driven (oversold reversal) and news-driven (H200 China, GS upgrade). The META move is company-specific. The CRM move is analyst-driven.

**What would change this view:** A significant escalation in Iran (missile strikes on shipping, oil above $90) would reverse the risk-on tone. Conversely, a confirmed Iran ceasefire deal would spark a powerful rally in risk assets.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Iran situation is highly fluid.** Trump's messaging has been erratic — "ceasefire is over" one day, calling Iran to negotiate the next. Any headline can swing oil and risk appetite.
- **DeepSeek chip story is unconfirmed.** Reuters report, but details are limited. Treat as speculative.
- **Memory stock bounce may be a dead cat bounce.** The Samsung-driven sell-off had structural concerns about oversupply. One day of +5% does not reverse that.
- **Palantir political risk is real.** The FT piece highlights pushback to Palantir's politics threatening the core business. PLTR at 34x+ sales (est.) leaves little room for error.
- **Crowded long tech/short software trade is unwinding.** CNBC's Todd Gordon notes this rotation is in play. Software could see relative strength.
- **Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) is down 16% from highs.** That's a correction by any definition. The bounce could attract sellers at resistance.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Semis are bouncing but the correction isn't over.** The $2T sell-off hit a make-or-break technical level. MU +5% and AMD +3% premarket are encouraging, but one session doesn't make a bottom. Monitor whether the SOX can reclaim key moving averages.
2. **Meta's AI cloud pivot is the most important company-specific story this week.** Meta selling excess compute capacity changes the competitive dynamics for cloud hyperscalers and neocloud providers. CoreWeave's 14% drop shows the market is paying attention.
3. **Geopolitical risk is the wildcard.** Iran headlines are moving oil and risk appetite. Any escalation kills the rally. Any ceasefire deal sparks a sharp move higher. Position defensively.
4. **AMD looks technically strong ahead of Aug 4 earnings.** Above all major moving averages, GS $640 PT, neutral RSI. The stock is consolidating rather than breaking down, unlike NVDA which is still below its 20/50-day SMAs.
5. **Salesforce downgrade is a reminder that enterprise software faces headwinds.** CRM's 4.5% premarket drop on a KeyBanc downgrade signals that the software rotation out of semis may not be a clean buy signal yet.
