## 8. Risk Notes

- **KOSPI circuit breaker is a serious signal.** This is not just a South Korea problem — KOSPI is now a leading indicator for global AI and semi sentiment. If it fails to recover, U.S. semis will follow.
- **Leverage unwind is in progress.** Goldman noted record retail margin, levered ETF, and short-dated options leverage. The SOXL 3x semi ETF down >50% from its June peak is evidence of forced selling.
- **Yardeni's 12% semi downside target is credible.** The strategist who called the dot-com crash and the 2022 correction has been right about the AI trade rotation.
- **"Mag 7 is dead" from Citi** — if this grouping convention breaks down, the passive flows that have supported these seven names may reallocate. Apple at 40x P/E is the most vulnerable.
- **Kimi K3 weights release on July 27** is a sword of Damocles over EDA/design tools. EDA stocks (CDNS -9.5%, SNPS -7.9% on Friday) could see further selling if the release validates Moonshot's claims.
- **Insider selling at $77.6B in H1 2026** (20% y/y, second-fastest pace in 20 years) is a persistent risk signal for tech stocks.
- **GOOGL EU Android antitrust order** is a headwind not fully priced into earnings expectations.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Wednesday is the most important day of Q2 for tech stocks.** Alphabet's AI capex guidance will set the direction for NVDA, semis, and AI infrastructure through late July. Position accordingly with elevated volatility in mind.

2. **The semi bear market is real, but valuations are resetting.** NVDA at 24x forward earnings with 85%+ growth is historically cheap. KeyBanc's supply-chain checks confirm demand remains strong. The question is whether sentiment or fundamentals drive the near term.

3. **KOSPI's 8% circuit breaker is the canary in the coal mine.** The KOSPI-NSDAQ feedback loop is tighter than ever. Watch Seoul for the first read on U.S. semi direction today.

4. **Iran is an underappreciated macro risk for tech.** Oil above $90 with a Houthi blockade of Saudi Arabia is stagflation territory. Growth stocks hate rising rates and rising oil simultaneously.

5. **Today is a consolidation day — use it to review positioning.** Monday is low-data and futures are positive, making it the calm before the Wednesday earnings storm. The most actionable setups are in GOOGL puts/calls for earnings, AMD for the AI event catalyst, and avoiding overexposure to semis until Alphabet reports.
