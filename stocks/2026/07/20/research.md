## KOSPI Circuit Breaker Analysis

- South Korea's KOSPI fell 8% on Monday, triggering a circuit breaker for the first time since March 2020 (COVID crash) and August 2024 (yen carry trade unwind).
- The plunge was driven by: (1) semi bear market contagion from U.S. SOX, (2) SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics leveraged ETF unwind, (3) Iran/oil spike hitting Korea's energy-import-dependent economy, (4) Seoul tightening margin requirements on single-stock leveraged ETFs.
- Despite the 8% plunge, KOSPI is still up ~62% YTD, making it the best-performing major index in the world in 2026.
- Implication: The KOSPI-NSDAQ feedback loop is now a structural feature of global tech markets, not a one-off. Fund managers in NY, London, and Tokyo now routinely check Seoul before their own markets open.
