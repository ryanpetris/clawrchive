## 6. Setup For The Next Open And Final Market Day

### Expected Trading Tone At Monday's Open
- **Cautiously positive.** Friday's momentum from the SpaceX IPO and US-Iran deal optimism should carry into Monday, but pre-FOMC positioning will cap upside. Futures likely point to a flat to slightly higher open.
- **Key variable:** Whether the US-Iran deal is actually signed over the weekend. A signed deal = clear risk-on catalyst (lower oil, lower inflation expectations, less geopolitical uncertainty). A failure to sign = partial reversal of Friday's gains.

### Main Upside Catalysts
- **US-Iran peace deal signing:** Would be a major de-escalation — lower oil prices, lower bond yields, relief for inflation-sensitive tech multiples.
- **FOMC dovish surprise:** If Warsh signals patience, keeps the dot plot steady, and treats the energy-driven CPI spike as transitory.
- **Dip-buying in semiconductors:** Post-AVGO selloff recovery continuing, with analysts calling the correction overdone.
- **SpaceX momentum:** SPCX could continue its first-day momentum, lifting the entire tech/risk-on trade.

### Main Downside Risks
- **FOMC hawkish surprise:** Warsh delivers hawkish dot plot (2+ hikes in 2026 median), warns of inflation persistence, signals September hike risk. This is the biggest risk of the week.
- **US-Iran deal failure:** Weekend negotiations collapse or Trump issues new threats — oil spikes, risk-off ensues.
- **Anthropic/Amazon fallout spreads:** Regulatory scrutiny widens to other AI companies, impacting the broader AI trade.
- **Triple Witching volatility:** Options expiration on Thursday could amplify moves in either direction, particularly for mega-cap tech and semiconductors.
- **Continued tech rotation:** If money continues flowing out of mega-cap tech into small caps/cyclicals, the Nasdaq could underperform even as the broader market rallies.

### Subsector Strength
- **Strongest:** Small caps/Russell 2000 (momentum from rotation), AI infrastructure (dip-buying opportunity), GOOGL (SpaceX/Anthropic catalyst).
- **Weakest:** Mega-cap tech ex-GOOGL (rotation pressure, FOMC duration risk), space/satellite stocks (SPCX crowding out other space names), AMZN (Anthropic conflict story).

### What Would Change This View?
- **A signed US-Iran deal this weekend:** Increases risk-on conviction going into FOMC. Would reduce the hawkish inflation narrative.
- **Warsh signals no hikes in 2026:** Major relief rally for semis and growth tech; view turns decisively bullish.
- **SpaceX sells off sharply Monday:** Could indicate the IPO frenzy has peaked and risk appetite is fading.
- **New regulatory action against AI labs:** Would change the AI thesis from "unregulated growth" to "increasing oversight."

## 7. Bottom Line

1. **FOMC Wednesday is the week-defining event.** The rate decision is a lock (hold at 3.50%-3.75%), but the dot plot and Kevin Warsh's press conference tone are the market movers. A hawkish dot plot (median showing hikes) is the biggest risk for growth/tech stocks. A steady or dovish dot plot would be a relief.
2. **The Anthropic/Amazon story is the weekend's biggest uncounted risk.** The conflict-of-interest narrative (Amazon as Anthropic's largest investor, cloud host, and government informant) could have broad implications for how big tech's AI investments are perceived and regulated. Watch for AMZN weakness.
3. **SpaceX IPO changes the capital markets landscape.** The largest IPO in history succeeded on a massive scale, and the pipeline (Anthropic, OpenAI) now has a clear precedent. SPCX's inclusion in the Nasdaq 100 would force passive inflows. GOOGL is a liquid derivative play.
4. **Semis look oversold but macro-dependent.** AVGO's selloff appears overdone (20x forward P/E for 143% AI revenue growth), but the sector needs the FOMC to cooperate. AMD's BofA upgrade and July catalysts provide a more specific timeline.
5. **This is a short, compressed week.** Four trading days, capped by Triple Witching on Thursday and Juneteenth on Friday. Volume and liquidity may thin out by Thursday afternoon. Active position management recommended ahead of the holiday weekend.
