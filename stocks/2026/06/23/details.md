# Tech Stock Briefing — June 23, 2026 — Global Tech Rout, Memory-Led Selling

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Global tech rout intensifies:** Nasdaq 100 futures slid 3.1% overnight as a selloff that began Monday accelerated, triggered by South Korea's KOSPI crashing 10% and triggering a circuit breaker — its fourth market-wide halt this year.
- **Memory chips lead the crash:** SK Hynix (-12.5%) and Samsung (-12.3%) collapsed in Seoul on news SK Hynix is slowing advanced AI chip production to boost commodity DRAM capacity. Micron fell ~10% in U.S. premarket ahead of its Wednesday earnings.
- **Alphabet extends slide:** GOOGL fell another 2% premarket after losing 5% Monday on high-profile AI talent defections — Noam Shazeer (Gemini co-lead) to OpenAI and Nobel laureate John Jumper (AlphaFold) to Anthropic.
- **SpaceX rout deepens:** Shares fell 16% Monday on a $20B investment-grade bond offering, extending a three-day slide that erased ~$600B in market cap. The stock is nearing its $135 IPO price.
- **Fed hike fears compound selling:** Markets now price a 68% probability of a September rate hike (up from 29% last week) after the Fed's hawkish dot plot. The 10-year yield sits at ~4.49%.
- **VIX spikes to 20+:** The CBOE Volatility Index jumped above 20 with heavy call buying, signaling options traders expect elevated volatility near-term.
- **Market tone: Risk-off / Tech-led selloff.** The 3-5 stocks most likely to move today: **MU** (premarket -10% ahead of Wednesday earnings), **GOOGL** (AI talent exodus), **AMD/INTC** (semiconductor contagion), **IBM** (bright spot, +4% on JPMorgan upgrade).

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected Trading Setup:** Sharply lower open with high intraday volatility. The global tech rout is broad and severe — this is not a garden-variety pullback. Nasdaq futures at -3.1% signal potential for the worst single day in months if the selloff holds through the cash open. The Dow futures at -0.4% suggest rotation out of growth into value/defensive may persist.

**Main Upside Catalysts:**
- Iran de-escalation and falling oil are positive macro offsets, but the market is ignoring them today.
- Dip buyers may step in if the selloff looks overdone — Morgan Stanley's Slimmon called it "healthy."
- IBM upgrade shows pockets of tech value are being recognized.
- MU earnings catalyst on Wednesday could reverse semi sentiment if results are strong.

**Main Downside Risks:**
- Korea contagion spreading further — if the KOSPI rout was driven by foreign capital flight, selling could intensify.
- Momentum-driven liquidation in crowded AI trades.
- Fed repricing continues — a hot PCE Thursday could compound the selloff into year-end.
- Private credit stress at Apollo could spill into broader risk appetite.
- MW: SpaceX approaching $135 IPO price is a psychological level that could break.

**Subsector Strength/Weakness:**
- **Weakest:** Memory semiconductors (MU, SK Hynix, Samsung), AI mega-caps (GOOGL, AMZN, META), software (PLTR, ORCL).
- **Strongest/Defensive:** IBM (upgrade), Walmart, JNJ (safe havens).
- **Mixed:** AI infrastructure names like SMCI that bucked the trend Monday.

**Move Driver:** News-driven (AI talent defections, Korea production shift), momentum-driven (crowded AI unwind), and macro-driven (Fed repricing). This is a multi-factor selloff.

**What Would Change This View:** A strong MU earnings beat and optimistic guide Wednesday could stabilize semiconductors. A cooler-than-expected PCE Thursday would reduce rate hike fears. Bullish commentary from Fed speakers walking back hawkish dot plots.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Unconfirmed claims:** Qualcomm/Modular acquisition talks are sourced to "people familiar" (Bloomberg) — not officially confirmed.
- **Low-liquidity names:** AMC, smaller memory-related names may see outsized moves.
- **Crowded trades:** AI/semiconductor longs are extremely crowded. The KOSPI was up 95% YTD before today — leverage unwind could be violent.
- **Binary events:** Micron earnings (Wed) and PCE inflation (Thu) are two of the most consequential events for tech sentiment this quarter.
- **Macro risks:** The Fed hiking into an economy with mixed signals (Apollo curbing withdrawals, jobs data, PCE) creates stagflation risk.
- **Stale news:** SpaceX bond offering was Monday news — the -16% move may already reflect most of the damage.
- **News already priced in:** GOOGL AI exits were reported last Wed/Fri — Monday's -5% may have been the initial reaction. Additional selling today may partially be technical/de-risking.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Today is a systemic tech de-risking event, not a sector-specific story.** The combination of AI talent concerns (GOOGL), memory production shifts (SK Hynix), debt-funded capex fears (SpaceX/Big Tech), and Fed hawkish repricing is hitting every subsector simultaneously.
2. **Micron earnings Wednesday is the most important catalyst for semiconductors this week.** The stock is selling off into the print — a strong beat could trigger a sharp reversal. Analysts are overwhelmingly bullish but the bar is extremely high.
3. **The South Korea KOSPI crash (-10%) is the headline story today and bears watching.** If this is foreign capital rotating out of Korean equities broadly (not just semi profit-taking), there could be further contagion.
4. **Defensives and selective value plays (IBM) are working while everything AI-related is under pressure.** The rotation out of growth into value/defensive is well underway.
5. **Thursday's PCE report is the macro wildcard.** A cooler print could derail the September rate hike narrative and provide relief to beaten-down tech. A hot print would confirm the selloff has further to run.
