## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected Trading Setup:** Cautious recovery bounce. Futures indicate a higher open after Tuesday's rout, but the scale of the rebound is likely limited ahead of Micron earnings after the close and PCE data Friday. This is an event-driven session where most participants are waiting for MU's print to gauge whether Tuesday's breadth selling was justified.

**Main Upside Catalysts:**
- Dip buying in high-conviction AI names (NVDA, SMCI, AVGO)
- NVDA shareholder meeting could provide positive Blackwell/Vera Rubin updates
- KOSPI stabilizing (+3%) removes the most acute Asia spillover risk
- Falling oil prices (WTI -5%) ease geopolitical premium and input cost concerns
- Bond yields easing (10Y -5bps to 4.45%) support tech valuations

**Main Downside Risks:**
- MU earnings disappointment could trigger another leg lower in semis
- GOOGL continued weakness dragging mega-cap sentiment
- Fed hike expectations continuing to build (50bps by December)
- PCE Friday could show inflation still stubbornly high
- Continued rotation out of AI/tech into cyclicals

**Strongest subsectors:** AI infrastructure hardware (SMCI, NVDA) showing relative strength vs. software. Semis may bounce ahead of MU but face binary risk after close.

**Weakest subsectors:** Software/hyperscaler layer (GOOGL, PLTR, ORCL) under sustained pressure; memory chips pre-earnings volatility.

**What would change this view:** A MU beat with strong HBM guidance would decisively reset the AI-demand narrative and could trigger an aggressive bounce into Friday. A MU miss on margins or disappointing HBM commentary would validate the selloff and likely extend losses through Friday's PCE release.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Binary event risk:** MU options imply ~14% swing after earnings. Position sizing matters today.
- **Unconfirmed claim:** SK Hynix "slowing HBM4" — widely walked back by analysts as a margin allocation decision, not demand weakness. Still, the initial reflex selling shows how fragile AI sentiment has become.
- **Crowded trades:** The AI trade was described by Baird's Ross Mayfield as "highly concentrated and flow-driven." Tuesday's SOX -8% is a textbook crowded-trade unwind.
- **Low-conviction bounce:** Premarket futures are up but modestly. The scale of Tuesday's damage means dip buyers may not step in aggressively until MU clears the decks.
- **GOOGL narrative break risk:** The $225B wipeout from AI talent departure is unusually specific. If more exits follow, the structural narrative around Google's AI leadership could become a longer-term headwind.
- **Fed repricing is macro-critical:** The shift from expecting rate cuts to pricing 50bps of hikes in just a few weeks is the most important macro development for tech multiples. PCE Friday will reinforce or counter this.
- **SMCI DOJ overhang unresolved:** Independent investigation into export control violations continues. Any negative development would reverse recent gains.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **MU earnings tonight is the single most important catalyst for the sector this week.** A beat with strong HBM guidance resets the AI-demand narrative; a miss or cautious outlook validates Tuesday's -8% SOX rout.
2. **Tuesday's selloff was a cascading event (Korea -> memory -> semis -> tech), not a fundamental AI-demand break.** The SK Hynix HBM story was reinterpreted as a margin allocation decision, and Korea bounced +3% overnight. This supports the "healthy pullback" framing from Wedbush's Dan Ives.
3. **GOOGL is the most structurally challenged mega-cap** — AI talent defections to competitors erode the narrative of AI leadership, and $180-190B in capex is crushing free cash flow (-47% YoY). Avoid catching this knife.
4. **The Fed repricing matters more than any single stock's news.** The shift to pricing 50bps of hikes is a headwind for the entire tech sector's valuation multiples. Friday's PCE print is the next macro waypoint.
5. **Position for elevated volatility into Friday.** MU tonight, NVDA shareholder meeting today, PCE Friday. The rest of the week delivers high-impact catalysts that could swing sector direction.
