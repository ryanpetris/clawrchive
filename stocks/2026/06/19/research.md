### Uncertainty Notes

- **Intel-Apple deal details are extremely sparse.** The announcement came from a Truth Social post, not a formal press release or joint statement. The lack of specificity (which chips, timeline, financials) means the market is trading on narrative, not fundamentals.
- **Iran nuclear talks postponement** is a real risk. The interim peace deal addresses the ceasefire and blockade, but without nuclear deal progress, the long-term risk premium may not fully dissipate.
- **Micron's triple-year-to-date gain** means any disappointment will be punished disproportionately. The risk/reward at current levels is asymmetric to the downside for the print itself, even if fundamentals remain strong.
- **Triple witching volume** may have distorted Thursday's moves. Some of the semiconductor surge could be options-related rather than structural buying.
- **Accenture's warning** about enterprise IT budgets suggests a two-tier market: AI spending is robust, but everything else is under scrutiny. This divergence may widen.
