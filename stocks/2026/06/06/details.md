### Biggest Risks
1. **Hot CPI (Wednesday)** — Headline expected +4.2% Y/Y. If the print surprises to the upside, the rate-hike narrative intensifies ahead of the June 17 FOMC, and growth/tech multiples compress further.

3. **Mag 7 capex skepticism deepening** — AMZN -7.6%, GOOGL -5.5%, MSFT -5.0% in a single week reflects a market increasingly unwilling to pay for promises of future AI revenue. If this sentiment spreads, NVDA and AI infrastructure names could face headwinds despite strong fundamentals.

### Setup For Monday Open
- Futures likely cautious following Friday's tech-led selloff on the strong NFP print.
- **Main upside catalyst:** Apple WWDC delivers a credible AI story that reassures investors about Apple's AI positioning and opens a re-rating.
- **Main downside risk:** Hot CPI (Wed) continues to pressure tech; Friday's NFP-driven selloff carries into Monday.

### 5 Concise Takeaways

1. **Apple WWDC is the week's defining tech catalyst** — Morgan Stanley's bull case ($440) and low expectations entering create asymmetric upside. But the gap between $311 current and $365+ bull case means a lot of good news is already discounted; only a genuinely impressive Siri/AI reveal drives the next leg.
2. **CPI Wednesday is the macro landmine** — Hot print +172k NFP = rate hike expectations intensify. Growth/tech multiples remain vulnerable to the repricing of Fed expectations.
3. **AVGO's -15% drop was a buy-the-dip opportunity** — Record AI revenue (+143% YoY), guidance well above consensus, $30B backlog. The market punished the wrong thing (Hock not raising the full-year target). Watch for recovery.
