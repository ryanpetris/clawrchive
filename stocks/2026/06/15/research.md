## Sector Depth

### Semiconductors — The Dominant Narrative

The semiconductor space is seeing a confluence of powerful tailwinds today:

1. **Macro**: US-Iran ceasefire → oil collapse → lower yields → growth stock re-rating. This benefits semis disproportionately due to their beta to rates.
2. **Fundamental**: AI infrastructure spending continues accelerating. The ByteDance story is a China-specific headwind for NVDA, but hyperscaler capex (Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, Google) remains on a multi-year growth trajectory.
3. **Valuation**: Multiple analyst upgrades this week (AMD, MU) suggest conviction that AI semiconductor demand is broadening beyond NVDA's monopoly.

**Key debate**: Is the semis rally sustainable, or is it crowded positioning driven by momentum? The VIX at 16.73 suggests complacency, but the geopolitical catalyst (Iran deal) is genuinely new information that changes the macro outlook.

## Macro Context

### Oil & Rates Linkage

The Iran deal's impact flows through two channels:
1. **Direct**: Lower oil → lower gasoline prices → consumer spending boost
2. **Indirect**: Lower oil → lower inflation prints → Fed less hawkish → lower rates

The 10-year yield dropping 4 bps to ~4.44% confirms the market is pricing this transmission mechanism. A sustained move below 4.40% would be a strong bullish signal for growth stocks.

### FOMC Preview (Wednesday June 17)

Key questions for the statement and dot plot:
1. Do they acknowledge the Iran deal and potential oil-driven disinflation?
2. Does the dot plot still show 1-2 cuts for late 2026, or has the hot CPI data shifted the median?
3. Does Powell emphasize patience or express concern about inflation persistence?

**If hawkish**: Risk-off, tech underperforms, semis reverse today's gains.
**If dovish**: Risk-on continues, growth stocks extend, yields break below 4.40%.
