# Tech Stock Briefing - June 15, 2026 - US-Iran Ceasefire, Risk-On Rally, SpaceX IPO Momentum

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Nasdaq 100 futures surged 2.2%** after President Trump announced a US-Iran ceasefire deal late Sunday, with talks to finalize the peace deal expected within 60 days. Oil prices cratered (WTI -5%+, Brent -4.7%), driving a broad risk-on rotation into tech and growth stocks.
- **SpaceX (SPCX) enters its second trading day** after the largest IPO in history (+19% debut Friday, market cap >$2T). Premarket indicated +5-7%. Options launch expected Tuesday; early MSCI inclusion possible within 10 trading days.
- **Fox announced a $22B acquisition of Roku** at $160/share ($96 cash + 0.9693 Fox shares). FOX shares -10% premarket; ROKU +1.4% building on Friday's 20% gain. Deal expected to close 1H CY2027 with $400M in cost synergies.
- **AI chip stocks lead the rebound**: Micron (MU) +8%+ premarket on multiple analyst PT hikes (TD Cowen $1,500, RBC $1,200, Aletheia $1,600) ahead of June 24 earnings. NVDA +2-3%, AMD +4.7% on Citi upgrade to Buy ($575 PT).
- **ByteDance reportedly weighing local Chinese AI chip suppliers** — a bearish signal for NVDA's China exposure (now near zero). Reuters report raises questions about whether Chinese hyperscalers are diversifying away from US chips.
- **Fed FOMC decision Wednesday** — 98%+ probability of no rate change per CME FedWatch. Hot inflation (April CPI +4.2% YoY) keeps rate-hike debate alive. The statement and Powell's tone will be key.
- **Market tone: Risk-on, AI-led, semiconductor-led.** The VIX collapsed to ~16.73 (-5.4%), the 10-year yield eased to ~4.44%, and Asian tech stocks surged overnight (SoftBank +12%, Tokyo Electron +9%, SK Hynix +6.4%).
- **Stocks most likely to move today**: MU (earnings catalyst + analyst upgrades), SPCX (second-day IPO dynamics + options launch), AMD (Citi upgrade), FOX/ROKU (M&A), and NVDA (macro tailwind vs. China headwind).

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Trading Setup:** Strongly risk-on open expected. The US-Iran ceasefire is the dominant narrative, driving a rotation out of energy and into tech/growth. The lower oil price provides a dual tailwind: reduced inflationary pressure (supporting lower yields) and improved cost structure for transport-dependent and consumer-facing tech.

**Main Upside Catalysts:**
- US-Iran ceasefire momentum driving broad risk appetite
- AI chip analyst upgrades (AMD, MU) providing sector-specific fuel
- SpaceX IPO momentum continues into second trading day with options catalyst imminent
- Lower Treasury yields (~4.44%) supporting growth stock valuations

**Main Downside Risks:**
- FOMC Wednesday — hawkish surprise or hawkish language could reverse risk-on positioning
- NVDA China overhang — ByteDance exploring Chinese alternatives is a genuine structural concern
- Fox/Roku deal execution risk — FOX taking on significant debt ($12B bridge loan)
- Profit-taking risk after Friday's strong rally (+0.5-0.7% across major indices)

**Subsector Strength/Lightness:**
- **Strongest:** Memory chips (MU, STX, WDC), AI infrastructure (AMD, SMCI), broad semis (SOXX +4.1%)
- **Weakest:** Energy stocks (oil price collapse), traditional media (FOX down on deal concerns)

**Character of Moves:** News-driven with a clear macro/geopolitical catalyst. The Iran deal is a genuine exogenous shock that has fundamentally changed the near-term outlook for oil prices, inflation expectations, and geopolitical risk premium. Moves in semis and tech appear fundamental (lower rates = higher growth stock valuations), while energy moves are positioning-driven.

**What Would Change This View:** A hawkish surprise from the Fed Wednesday, a breakdown in Iran ceasefire talks, or a hot inflation print that pushes yields back above 4.60%.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **ByteDance/NVDA China story** is from Reuters and carries weight, but the timeline for any actual shift is uncertain. NVDA's China business is already near zero, so incremental downside may be limited. However, the narrative risk is real.
- **SpaceX valuation** at $2T+ with $4.94B in losses — extreme valuation that depends on Musk's $1T revenue target for 2030. Limited float (only ~4% of shares public) amplifies volatility. Morningstar values it near $780B.
- **Micron earnings on June 24** — expectations are extremely high after 250% YTD rally. The bar for a beat-and-raise is elevated. Any disappointment could trigger sharp profit-taking.
- **FOX/Roku deal** still requires shareholder and regulatory approvals, and close is not expected until H1 2027 — a long execution timeline.
- **FOMC blackout period** means no Fed guidance ahead of Wednesday's decision. The market is pricing a hold, but the statement/dot plot could still surprise.
- **Crowded trades**: AI semis, especially NVDA and MU, are extremely crowded longs. Any macro shock could trigger rapid unwinding.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Today is all about the Iran ceasefire.** It's a legitimately positive macro shock for tech — lower oil = lower inflation pressure = lower rates = higher growth stock valuations. The risk-on signal is unambiguous.
2. **AI chips are the place to be within tech today.** MU leads with analyst upgrades and earnings preview, AMD has fresh Citi conviction, and NVDA gets the macro tailwind despite the China overhang.
3. **Watch the FOMC Wednesday.** The market is pricing a hold, but the path forward on rates matters more for tech multiples than any single stock catalyst this week.
4. **SpaceX is the wild card.** Second-day IPO dynamics + options launch Tuesday + possible MSCI inclusion creates a volatile setup. Not a fundamental trade — pure momentum and market structure.
5. **The week is short** (closed Friday for Juneteenth), which could amplify moves as traders adjust positions ahead of the long weekend.
