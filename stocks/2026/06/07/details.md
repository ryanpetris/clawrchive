The PHLX Semiconductor Index dropped 10.3%, its largest one-day fall since March 2020 ([Source](https://au.marketscreener.com/news/hot-jobs-report-rising-rates-send-wall-street-s-tech-favorites-sprawling-ce7f5dd2d889f522)).

The stock is still up 11% YTD ([Source](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/bank-of-america-resets-broadcom-stock-price-target-after-earnings)).
- The 10-year Treasury yield surged to 4.54% and the 2-year hit 4.16% (highest since Feb 2025), creating a rates-driven equity sell-off with no flight-to-safety bid in bonds ([Source](https://mottcapitalmanagement.com/the-semiconductor-squeeze-cracked-and-the-two-year-treasury-wouldnt-rally/)).
- Apple's WWDC kicks off Monday at 10am PT. The headline event is the long-delayed AI Siri overhaul powered by Google Gemini — Apple's most consequential software event in years. Morgan Stanley raised AAPL to $440 target ([Source](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/wwdc-2026-what-to-expect/)).
- SpaceX is set to price its $75B IPO on Thursday (June 11) and begin trading Friday (June 12) on Nasdaq under ticker SPCX. Demand has reached ~$150B, double the supply ([Source](https://sophiccapital.com/june-06-2026-worst-week-in-2026-for-sp-500-and-nasdaq/)).
- The Korean won weakened to its lowest level versus the dollar since the 1998 Asian currency crisis — a risk factor for semiconductor stocks if Korean investors repatriate US equity holdings ([Source](https://mottcapitalmanagement.com/the-semiconductor-squeeze-cracked-and-the-two-year-treasury-wouldnt-rally/)).
- The ECB is expected to hike rates on Thursday (June 11), and the BOJ is ~80% priced for a hike on June 16, adding global tightening pressure alongside the Fed ([Source](https://mottcapitalmanagement.com/the-semiconductor-squeeze-cracked-and-the-two-year-treasury-wouldnt-rally/)).

## 6. Setup For The Next Open And Final Market Day

### Expected Trading Tone At Monday Open
- Futures are expected to show a bounce attempt after Friday's brutal sell-off. The S&P 500 -2.6% and Nasdaq -4.2% moves were statistically extreme.
- VIX closed at ~21.5, with 1-day VIX at ~28.7 — giving room for a volatility reset or a gap lower.
- Overnight Asian markets and early pre-market futures will be the key tell. South Korea's KOSPI (already down 4.5% on Friday) is the most important Asian read-through for semiconductor sentiment.
- **Baseline:** Cautious bounce attempt, but follow-through selling is equally likely if rate fears persist. The market needs to decide whether Friday was a positioning washout (bullish for a bounce) or a fundamental repricing (bearish continuation).

### Main Upside Catalysts
- **Apple WWDC on Monday** could redirect attention to a positive catalyst and stabilize tech. A well-received AI Siri demo could boost AAPL and lift the entire mega-cap complex.
- **Buy-the-dip appetite remains strong** after nine consecutive weeks of gains. Wells Fargo's Ohsung Kwon called it "not the end" for the semiconductor bull run.
- **Nvidia's $80B buyback** provides a structural floor. The company is the largest authorized buyer of its own stock in the market.
- **CPI could surprise to the downside** on Wednesday, reversing the rate narrative. The Cleveland Fed nowcast shows CPI potentially cooling.

### Main Downside Risks
- **CPI hot print** on Wednesday would confirm the jobs report narrative and drive yields higher, compounding tech multiple compression.
- **SpaceX IPO liquidity drain** — retail investors may continue selling existing tech positions to fund the IPO allocation, a dynamic that was already visible in Friday's sell-off (MU was the most-bought stock by retail and fell hardest).
- **Korean won crisis escalation** — further won weakening could force Korean investors (who hold massive US equity positions) to repatriate, adding concentrated selling in semi names.
- **ECB/BOJ hikes** add global tightening pressure, reinforcing the "no rate cuts" narrative.
- **Meta or Google follow-on dilution** — if META announces a large equity offering for AI infra, it would confirm the concern that drove Friday's 5.5% drop.

### Subsector Outlook
- **Strongest:** Value/defensives (healthcare, financials, energy) are seeing rotation inflows. Eli Lilly and Wells Fargo gained last week.
- **Weakest:** Semiconductors (SOXX -10.3%), high-multiple AI names, and software names with unproven AI monetization.
- **Most event risk:** Apple (WWDC), Oracle/Adobe (earnings), SpaceX-linked names (TSLA, broader retail flow).

### What Would Change This View?
- **A hot CPI print (>0.3% MoM)** would confirm the rates-driven sell-off has further to run and likely trigger another leg down in tech.
- **A strong Apple WWDC with a live Siri demo** that impresses markets could stabilize and refocus sentiment on AI-driven product cycles rather than macro.
- **A surprise dovish pivot from the ECB** (less hawkish than expected) could relieve some global rate pressure.
- **A Korean won stabilization intervention** by the Bank of Korea would reduce repatriation risk.

## 7. Bottom Line

1. **Friday's sell-off was positioning-driven, not fundamentally broken.** The semiconductor bull case (AI-driven demand growth >180% YoY for Broadcom, Blackwell ramp for NVDA) remains intact. But positioning reset could take days, not hours — expect elevated volatility through Wednesday's CPI.
2. **Apple WWDC (Monday) is the highest-impact single-company event this week.** A well-received AI Siri launch could be the catalyst that stabilizes mega-cap tech. A disappointment compounds the negative narrative.
3. **Wednesday's CPI is the macro fulcrum.** The jobs report set a hawkish tone, but CPI is what confirms or refutes it. If core CPI comes in cooler than feared, the rate narrative resets and tech rallies hard. If hot, expect more downside.
4. **SpaceX IPO is a two-sided event.** It's a landmark for tech and growth markets, but the $75B+ demand absorption may continue to drain liquidity from existing tech names through Thursday-Friday.
5. **Defensive positioning makes sense early in the week.** Semis are oversold but the catalyst calendar (CPI, ECB, SpaceX) argues for caution until Wednesday at least. Watch for a dip-buying entry point on NVDA if it holds above $200.
