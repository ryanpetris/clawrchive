# Research Notes - June 7, 2026

## Market Structure Notes

### The Dispersion-Correlation Squeeze
- Per Mott Capital: Semiconductor call-froth drove single-stock implied vol higher; three-month correlations back to July 2024 low; dispersion at crisis-only extremes.
- Broadcom earnings were the catalyst flagged in advance. Post-earnings move: ~12.5% drop Thursday + ~8% Friday.
- The squeeze unwind ran through the names that had built it.

### The Absent Flight To Safety
- Normally a 4%+ Nasdaq drop triggers a bid in Treasuries. This time: 2Y yields ROSE and held. 10Y rose 6bps.
- The curve modestly inverted on Friday — typically a recession signal, but in this context indicates the market is pricing possible rate HIKES.
- This is not a growth-fear sell-off. This is a rates-driven equity sell-off.

### Korean Won Repatriation Risk
- Won closed at weakest vs USD since 1998 Asian currency crisis.
- ~3% weekly decline, +1.7% on Friday alone.
- Korea's cumulative US-equity holdings now close to Japan's. Growth rate in those holdings has run ahead of S&P 500 appreciation — a flow signature.
- If won keeps weakening, repatriation pressure becomes a legitimate question for the names that have been bid hardest (semiconductors).
