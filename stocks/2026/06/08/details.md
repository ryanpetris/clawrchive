## 1. Executive Summary

- **Nasdaq 100 futures +0.5-0.75%, S&P 500 futures +0.2-0.4%, Dow futures flat to slightly negative** — the market attempts a recovery after Friday's brutal semiconductor-led selloff, though Iran-Israel tensions and rising rate-hike expectations limit conviction.

- **Asian markets crushed:** Korea's KOSPI -8.3% (circuit breakers triggered), Samsung Elec -10.2%, SK Hynix -7.7%. Japan's Nikkei -4.6%, Taiwan TAIEX -3.2%.
- **Orbit-shaping week ahead:** May CPI (Wed), PPI (Thu), Oracle Q4 earnings (Wed after close), ECB decision (Thu), and final data before new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's first FOMC decision on June 16-17.

**Market tone:** Cautious recovery / event-driven / semiconductor-led, with a major macro cross-current from inflation data and geopolitical risk.

**Stocks to watch today:** NVDA (Huang's Korea deals & "cheap" call vs. $5T valuation test), AAPL (WWDC catalyst), MRVL (S&P 500 addition + 16% Friday drop bounce), MU (memory shortage narrative), SMCI (premarket +7.7% relief bounce).

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected Trading Setup:** Cautious recovery attempt. Semis lead the bounce, but the macro cross-currents (Iran-Israel, rate-hike repricing, Asia circuit breakers) keep the tone fragile. The opening is likely to be positive for Nasdaq/QQQ but choppy for the Dow.

**Main Upside Catalysts:**
- Jensen Huang's "very cheap" / "memory shortage multi-year" framing provides a strong narrative for dip-buying in semis
- Apple WWDC keynotes often generate positive sentiment for tech broadly, and this year's AI-heavy agenda is the most consequential in years
- Positioning unwind from Friday may be overdone; Danske Bank and Wells Fargo both described it as a normal correction in a bull market
- MRVL S&P 500 addition is a structural demand catalyst

**Main Downside Risks:**
- Iran-Israel escalation kept oil elevated; further escalation would pressure risk assets
- KOSPI circuit breaker is a real stress signal — if U.S. futures fade, Monday could see follow-through selling
- CPI Wednesday is the dominant concern; a hot print cements the rate-hike narrative through Warsh's FOMC
- Meta/Google equity raise narrative may spread to other Mag 7 names, pressuring sentiment
- Any disappointment from Apple WWDC (Siri demo fails to impress, Extensions pricing unclear) would compound tech weakness

**Subsector Strength/Weakness:** Semis look to lead the recovery day (MRVL, MU, SMCI as strongest). Mega-cap platforms are mixed — AAPL as WWDC catalyst, META under dilution overhang. Cloud/software is a watch-and-see ahead of ORCL earnings.

**Fundamental vs. Positioning vs. Macro:** Friday's selloff was primarily positioning-driven (overbought semis, crowded AI trade). The macro catalyst (jobs data → rate-hike repricing) and geopolitical catalyst (Iran) were secondary triggers. The fundamentals of AI infrastructure spending remain intact per multiple sell-side analysts.

**What Would Change This View:**
- A hot CPI print Wednesday would confirm the rate-hike narrative and likely trigger another leg lower
- Clear de-escalation in Middle East (Iran statement that operation ended is a positive step but fragile)
- Apple WWDC delivers convincing Siri 2.0 demo with clear AI differentiation
- Oracle earnings beat (Wed after close) with strong AI cloud growth signals

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Unconfirmed claims:** Meta equity raise is an FT report; company called it "pure speculation." The stock already repriced — if Meta uses debt instead, the overhang lifts.
- **Low-liquidity names:** SMCI, MRVL will be highly volatile today given their premarket moves and Friday drops. SNDK also elevated.
- **Crowded trades:** The AI/semiconductor trade was extremely crowded — positioning unwind may not be complete. Friday's SOX -8.2% was the biggest since March 2020.
- **Binary events:** CPI Wednesday, ORCL earnings, ECB Thursday, FOMC next week. Today is a pre-positioning day.
- **Macro risks:** 10Y yield at 4.55%, Brent >$97, DXY firm — the trifecta that historically compresses tech multiples.
- **Stale news:** The Broadcom earnings disappointment (June 3) is now 5 days old. The negative reaction is largely priced in, but follow-through selling is possible if AI guidance concerns spread to ORCL.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Today is a recovery test for semis, not a confirmed reversal.** Friday was a positioning-driven unwind, not a fundamental break. But the macro setup (rates, oil, Fed) is deteriorating and bears watching.
2. **Apple WWDC is the single biggest catalyst today.** A compelling Siri 2.0 / Apple Intelligence demo can shift the AI narrative away from "capex without returns" toward "product differentiation." Watch the market reaction closely.
3. **CPI Wednesday is the week's dominant risk.** A hot print validates the BNP Paribas rate-hike forecast and pressures growth multiples into Warsh's first FOMC. Any tech rally this week should be carefully sized ahead of that number.
4. **The AI infrastructure capex story is becoming a double-edged sword.** Meta's possible equity raise and Google's $85B offering introduce dilution concern. ORCL earnings Wednesday is the next real test of whether AI spending is translating into revenue growth.
5. **Memory names (MU, SNDK) have the strongest fundamental catalyst this week** — Huang's multi-year shortage call adds credibility to their extended visibility. If you're positioned in semis, these offer the cleanest thesis.
