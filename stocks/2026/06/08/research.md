# Research Notes - June 8, 2026

## Friday's Selloff: Deeper Context

### The Scale
- Philadelphia Semiconductor Index: largest one-day decline since March 2020 (COVID crash)
- S&P 500 lost 200.57 points (-2.64%) to 7,383.74
- Nasdaq Composite lost 1,121.53 points (-4.18%) to 25,709.43
- Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 695.15 points (-1.35%) to 50,866.78
- S&P 500's first negative week in 10 weeks — ended its nine-week Friday-to-Friday winning streak
- Tech sector was the worst S&P 500 sector, down 5.8%; consumer staples led gainers

### The Catalysts (Ranked by importance per sell-side analysis)
1. **Positioning unwind** — consensus view (Danske Bank, Wells Fargo): semis were up ~50% in 2 months, positioning far exceeded long-term allocation. The selloff was an overbought correction.
2. **Broadcom disappointment** — June 3 earnings: declined to raise AI chip revenue projections, triggering initial weakness that snowballed into a sector-wide unwind by Friday
3. **May jobs report** — stronger than expected: raised rate hike expectations, provided the macro trigger for profit-taking
4. **Iran/Israel** — NOT a factor on Friday (oil was down Friday, headlines came after the selloff per Danske)

### Key Quote — Danske Bank:
> "The sell-off was extremely concentrated: US semiconductors were down 8.2%, and semis account for roughly 15% of the S&P 500."
> "This had nothing to do with Iran."
> "The real reason is that global tech had risen around 50% in just about two months. After that kind of move, setbacks like Friday's are entirely normal."
> "Even if the Fed were to hike, or if oil were to rise 3%, that does not change the outlook for the AI build-out."

### Key Quote — Wells Fargo (Ohsung Kwon):
> "The market reaction today was more driven by positioning rather than fundamentals. The semiconductor sector was way overbought. That's why we're seeing the selloff. I don't think it's the end of the semi bull market."

### Key Quote — Carson Group (Ryan Detrick):
> "After the record run we've seen the last nine weeks in equities, specifically tech and semiconductors, the dam just broke today."
