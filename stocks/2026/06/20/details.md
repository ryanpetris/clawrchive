## 4. Earnings Preview

### ⭐ Micron Technology (MU) — Wednesday June 24 after close

| Metric | Consensus | Year Ago | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| EPS | **$20.05** | $1.91 | +950% |
| Revenue | **$35.0B** | $9.3B | +276% |

**What matters:**
- **HBM (High-Bandwidth Memory) revenue trajectory** — the AI-driven growth engine. Investors want to hear about HBM3E ramp and HBM4 timeline.
- **DRAM pricing outlook** — memory pricing cycle is the core driver of earnings momentum. Any sign of peaking would hit the stock hard.
- **Guidance for Q4** — the stock has rallied 298% YTD and trades at ~$1,134. Guidance needs to justify the multiple.
- **Apple memory demand read-through** — Apple CEO flagged device price increases due to rising memory costs, which supports MU's pricing power.
- **SK Hynix capacity expansion risk** — SK Hynix announced plans to significantly increase production capacity over 5 years, raising oversupply concerns.

**Wall Street view:** Wedbush (Outperform, PT $1,300→$1,300), Stifel (Buy, PT $550→$1,500). Both cite accelerating AI-driven memory demand. The AI trend still has "a lot of juice" per Burney Company. However, SK Hynix's expansion plans and Micron's 298% YTD gain leave limited room for disappointment.

**Read-throughs:** Bullish MU → bullish DRAM (Samsung, SK Hynix), bullish HBM supply chain, bullish for SOXX/SMH. Bearish MU surprise → risk-off for entire semiconductor sector.
