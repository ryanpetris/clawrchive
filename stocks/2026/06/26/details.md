# Tech Stock Briefing - June 26, 2026 - AI Trade Selloff Deepens; Apple/MSFT Price Hikes, OpenAI IPO Delay

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Nasdaq futures were down ~1.2% premarket Friday**, extending a four-day losing streak as the AI trade selloff deepened amid fresh uncertainty on AI spending returns, inflation, and component cost shocks.
- **Apple (AAPL) plunged ~6% Thursday** after raising MacBook, iPad, and other device prices by 15–25%, citing "unprecedented" memory cost inflation driven by AI data center buildout. Tim Cook called it a "hundred-year flood" in component costs.
- **Microsoft (MSFT) fell ~3.5%** after raising Xbox prices $100–150 and warning memory costs could double again in 12 months. MSFT is now down ~18% YTD.
- **OpenAI may delay its IPO to 2027** per NYT report, hammering SoftBank (-13% overseas), Oracle (-1.6% premarket), and CoreWeave (-2.4%). The Stargate infrastructure partners face a longer road to monetization.
- **Micron (MU) surged ~16% Thursday** after reporting record 84.9% gross margins and Q3 revenue of $41.46B (+346% YoY), but gave back ~5% premarket Friday as the broader AI spending narrative came under pressure.
- **Major Asian tech rout:** South Korea's KOSPI fell ~8%, Japan's Nikkei dropped 4.5% Friday, led by SK Hynix and Samsung on AI demand sustainability fears.
- **May PCE inflation hit 4.1% YoY** — first reading above 4% since April 2023 — keeping a Fed rate hike on the table for September (CME FedWatch shows ~80% probability).
- **Market tone: Risk-off / AI-led selloff / rates-driven.** The Magnificent Seven lost ~$1T in market cap over the past three sessions.

**Stocks most likely to move today:** AAPL (continued fallout from price hikes), MU (Micron volatility post-surge + premarket pullback), NVDA (AI bellwether testing $200 support), PLTR (hitting 52-week lows), QCOM (upgraded non-mobile revenue outlook).

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected trading setup:** Risk-off open. Nasdaq futures pointing to a 1%+ gap lower. The AI trade is under its most sustained pressure since April 2025. Expect continued rotation out of mega-cap tech into industrials, healthcare, and defensive sectors — the Dow held up Thursday and may continue to outperform.

**Main upside catalysts:**
- NVDA holding $200 support could trigger dip-buying from algorithmic/long-term investors
- Micron's fundamental demand signals remain intact despite premarket pullback
- IBM's sub-1nm chip announcement provides a positive narrative in semis
- Oil prices falling on peace deal could ease some inflation pressure

**Main downside risks:**
- OpenAI IPO delay narrative expanding — could drag down all AI infrastructure names
- Apple/MSFT price hikes signaling broader consumer tech inflation that the market hasn't fully priced
- Fed rate hike in September becoming more likely (80% priced)
- Asian market contagion — KOSPI -8% could spill into US session
- More tech companies potentially following Apple/MSFT with price hikes, amplifying demand destruction fears

**Subsector bias:** Semis mixed (MU strong, ON/AMD/INTC weak); mega-cap platforms weak; cybersecurity/software under continued pressure (PLTR at lows); AI infrastructure mixed with bearish tilt.

**Moves appear:** News-driven (Apple/MSFT price hikes, OpenAI delay, PCE data) and positioning-driven (tech unwind, sector rotation). Some multiple compression from rising rates.

**What would change this view:**
- A stabilization in NVDA above $200
- Fed commentary signaling a more dovish tilt
- Positive pre-announcements from tech companies
- Any indication Apple/MSFT price hikes are manageable for demand

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Unconfirmed claims:** The OpenAI IPO delay is per NYT sources — not official. Could be walked back.
- **Low-liquidity names:** SMCI, CRWV, and small-cap AI infrastructure names may see exaggerated moves.
- **Crowded trades:** The AI / Magnificent Seven trade remains extremely crowded. The unwind could continue.
- **Binary events:** Fed September rate decision (~80% probability of hike). July CPI print will be critical.
- **Macro risks:** PCE at 4.1% with services inflation sticky; Iran peace deal may reduce oil prices but services inflation is structurally higher.
- **Stale/priced-in risk:** Micron's blowout earnings may already be priced in after the 16% Thursday surge. The -5% premarket suggests profit-taking.
- **Regulatory:** DOJ investigation into SMCI export violations remains open. Palantir facing UK/France government contract reviews.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **The AI trade is in its most significant correction since 2024** — but the narrative is about multiple compression and return-on-capex scrutiny, not demand destruction. NVDA at 21x forward earnings with 85%+ revenue growth is historically cheap.
2. **Memory cost inflation is the single most important cross-sector theme today** — it's the primary driver of Apple and Microsoft price hikes, Micron's record margins, and the demand destruction fear spreading across consumer tech.
3. **OpenAI's potential IPO delay and SpaceX's post-IPO slide signal a maturing sentiment cycle for private AI giants** — the Stargate partners (ORCL, SFTBY, CRWV) face a longer path to monetization.
4. **Watch for sector rotation to persist** — the Dow at or near record highs while the Nasdaq bleeds suggests capital flowing to value/defensive/industrial names.
5. **The Fed remains the wildcard** — 4.1% PCE and an 80% September rate hike probability mean rate-sensitive tech multiples will struggle to re-rate higher without a shift in Fed communication.
