## Market Context & Depth

### Nasdaq Four-Day Losing Streak
The Nasdaq Composite has fallen for four consecutive sessions through Thursday (June 25). - The Dow testing record highs while Nasdaq bleeds is the structural story

### Conflicting Signals On AI Demand
Bull case for AI semis remains intact at the order-book level:
- NVDA data center revenue grew 68% YoY to $193.7B in FY2026
- AVGO AI semi revenue $10.8B in Q2 (+143% YoY); guiding Q3 to $16B (+200%)
- MSFT AI run rate $37B (+123% YoY) with $627B commercial RPO (+99%)
- MU secured 16 long-term take-or-pay contracts worth $100B+
- SMCI: $39B order backlog

But the market is now pricing in:
- Concern that hyperscaler capex is circular (NVDA invests in customers → customers buy NVDA GPUs)
- Demand for proof of ROI on AI spending
- Rising rates compressing growth stock multiples
- Fear that consumer device demand destruction will hit semi orders

### SpaceX & The IPO Sentiment Cycle
- SpaceX raised $86B in its IPO on June 12, then immediately pursued $25B in bonds
- Allianz CIO called this a "prime example" of bubble territory
- SpaceX stock fell from $225 to $152, -24% in 6 trading days, erasing $777B in market cap
- OpenAI citing SpaceX's decline as a reason to delay its IPO — the sentiment contagion is real
- Anthropic also filed confidentially — both AI giants watching market conditions

### Circular AI Financing Risk
- NVDA takes stakes in AI startups → those startups commit to buying NVDA GPUs → revenue is real but demand independence is questionable
- The same hyperscalers building AI models are the ones renting compute from cloud providers they invested in
- This circularity means the unwind, when it comes, could be fast
- For now, demand keeps exceeding supply — but the market is starting to ask harder questions
