# Tech Stock Briefing - June 5, 2026 - Broadcom Earnings Rattles AI Semis, Jobs Data in Focus

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Broadcom's earnings miss triggers second day of AI-chip selling.** AVGO fell ~12% Thursday after revenue and AI-chip guidance fell short of elevated expectations; premarket pressure continues Friday with semis broadly lower.
- **May nonfarm payrolls report drops at 8:30 AM ET** — consensus ranging 80K–105K vs. 115K prior. A miss could reignite rate-cut hopes; a strong print may compound tech selling.
- **Nasdaq futures down ~0.9–1.2%, S&P 500 futures off ~0.4–0.5%** as tech rotation deepens. Dow futures modestly positive (+0.1–0.2%), extending Thursday's record close.
- **Dow surged 875 points to a record 51,561.93** on strength in financials, healthcare, and energy — the flip side of the tech/rotation trade.
- **South Korea's KOSPI crashed 7%** overnight, led by SK Hynix (-10%) and Samsung (-6%) as AI enthusiasm cools. Jensen Huang is in Seoul and said robotics will be Korea's next big industry.
- **Alphabet seeking $85B in equity, stock facing 4-week losing streak.** GOOGL briefly surpassed NVDA by market cap last month; now faces dilution concerns and underwhelming Google I/O sentiment.
- **Apple WWDC next Monday (June 8)** — Morgan Stanley sees Siri 2.0 as a potential catalyst for multiple expansion; UBS disagrees, calling it a neutral event.
- **Palantir continues to slide** after Michael Burry's "sand castle" warning; PLTR down ~7% despite 85% revenue growth — pure valuation compression.
- **Market tone: semiconductor-led risk-off, rotation into cyclicals.** Most likely to move today: AVGO, NVDA, AMD, MU, GOOGL, AAPL (WWDC anticipation).

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected trading setup:** Mixed-to-negative open skewed toward tech underperformance. The Dow should remain bid on rotation into cyclicals, financials, and energy. The key variable is payrolls at 8:30 AM ET — this will dictate whether the day becomes a broad selloff (hot number = higher for longer = tech compression) or a relief rally into the close (cold number = rate cut hopes = tech bounce).

**Main upside catalysts:**
- Weak payrolls number → rate-cut hopes → tech mean-reversion
- Apple WWDC anticipation supporting AAPL
- NVDA dip buying (Computex newsflow still positive)

**Main downside risks:**
- Hot payrolls → hawkish Fed repricing
- Broadcom contagion continuing through semis
- GOOGL dilution narrative weighing on mega-cap sentiment
- Systemic AI-trade pause after 10 straight S&P weekly gains

**Strongest subsectors:** Dow/cyclicals (banks, healthcare, energy). **Weakest subsectors:** Semis, AI infrastructure, high-multiple software.

**Nature of moves:** Largely news-driven (payrolls, Broadcom earnings after-effects) and rotation. Fundamental demand for AI infrastructure remains intact — this is positioning-driven and sentiment-driven weakness, not a demand shock.

**What would change this view:** A payrolls number below 70K or above 150K. A positive Broadcom analyst day. Apple WWDC leaks that pre-empt the keynote.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Unconfirmed:** Details of Huang's meetings in South Korea are still emerging; specific supply agreements with Samsung/SK Hynix are not fully quantified.
- **Crowded trade:** The AI semi trade was extremely crowded entering this week. The Broadcom disappointment is a de-risking trigger. Flows could persist into next week.
- **Binary event:** Payrolls at 8:30 AM ET — avoid oversized positioning into the print.
- **Dilution risk:** GOOGL's $85B equity raise is the largest ever; secondary effects on other mega-cap names raising debt/equity for AI capex.
- **Macro risk:** 10Y at 4.47% is not alarming, but any move above 4.6% would pressure growth stocks. Watch yields after payrolls.
- **Stale news:** Computex announcements from Monday-Wednesday are largely priced in; NVIDIA's Vera Rubin production news is now stale.
- **Low liquidity:** Watch LULU for consumer discretionary read-through after 12% premarket plunge on lowered guidance.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **AI semi rotation is the dominant theme** — Broadcom's "high bar" miss triggered a sector-wide de-risking that may take days, not hours, to wash through. The demand story is intact; valuations got ahead of fundamentals at the margin.
2. **Payrolls are the binary event today.** A soft print could reverse tech weakness quickly. A hot print extends the pain. Position accordingly.
3. **GOOGL's equity raise is a sentiment drag** but likely a long-term opportunity — Morningstar maintains $433 fair value. The Cloud growth story is real.
4. **Apple WWDC on Monday is the next major catalyst** for mega-cap tech. Low expectations could make AAPL a relative safe haven today.
5. **The Dow/tech divergence is the market's message:** rotation, not panic. The S&P 500's 10-week winning streak is at risk, but a pause would be healthy after a historic run.
