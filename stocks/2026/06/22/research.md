## Key Themes

### 1. The Intel Re-Rating Trade
Intel is the most debated name in the sector. The Apple foundry rumor (amplified by Trump social media post) has pushed INTC to a 52-week high and near $700B market cap. But the fundamental debate is extreme:
- **Bull case:** Foundry turnaround real. Apple deal, Nvidia collaboration, improving 18A yields. Washington backing. TSMC capacity constrained.
- **Bear case:** 1.4% revenue growth, -9.4% operating margins, losing CPU share to AMD, no competitive AI GPU, 87x 2027 EPS. Seeking Alpha calls it "levitating on AI hype."
- **Best framing:** Both can be right near-term; the stock has strong momentum on narrative alone, but the fundamental gap makes it a high-risk position.

### 2. Micron's Moment
Wednesday's MU print is the most consequential tech earnings this week. The numbers expected are staggering:
- Consensus EPS ~$20 (+960% YoY), revenue ~$35B (+277% YoY)
- Gross margin ~81% guided
- 12 straight quarters of beats
- But expectations are so high that the bar for disappointment is low
- Key tension: $25B+ capex for FY26 vs. sustainability of memory supercycle
- DRAM pricing up 93-98% QoQ in Q1, another 58-63% in Q2
- HBM expected to be a $100B market by 2028

### 3. The "AI Tax" Across the Supply Chain
Multiple data points confirm memory scarcity is creating cascading price effects:
- AMD reportedly raising GPU prices 10-15% due to GDDR6 costs (tripled since late 2025)
- TSMC not ruling out price increases despite already-surged demand
- NVIDIA RTX 5090 selling at 100%+ above MSRP
- KORU's wild ride (+2,108% over one year, then -42% in one day) reflects the extreme positioning in Korean memory names
- This is the structural scarcity vs. demand sustainability debate in real time

### 4. US-Iran: The Macro Wild Card
The negotiations failure creates uncertainty on multiple vectors:
- Oil prices: Brent under $80, WTI ~$74 - relatively contained but could spike on escalation
- Rate expectations: CME shows Dec rate hike most likely outcome. New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh seen as hawkish.
- Market had rallied on peace deal hopes (Nasdaq +2.4% Thursday); disappointment reversing some of that
- 60-day ceasefire keeps diplomatic channel open but no breakthrough

### 5. Amazon's Prime Day Catalyst
Prime Day (June 23-26) is a significant near-term catalyst for AMZN:
- First July-to-June move since 2021
- Expected $15.68B US sales (+7.1%)
- 55% of US consumers plan to participate (up from 45%)
- Stock at $244, down 11% from $278.56 ATH on May 5
- AWS growth at 28% fastest in 15 quarters, but $200B capex creates tension
- Advertising at $70B trailing revenue - larger than AWS was in 2018
