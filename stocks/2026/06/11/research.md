## Super Micro (SMCI) Deep Dive

### $7B Financing Package
- **$5B underwritten offering:** $1.25B common stock + $3.75B depositary shares (fractional mandatory convertible preferred)
- **$2B ATM program:** Begins no earlier than Q3 2026; JPM, Goldman Sachs, Citi as managers
- **$39B AI server orders** from 20+ customers in "recent weeks"
- **Market cap pre-announcement:** ~$45B → $7B raise = ~15% dilution

### Stock Action
- Tuesday after-hours: -9% initially
- Wednesday close: -27.98% ($29.27) on 191.35M shares
- Premarket Thursday: ~$35.04 (-13.78% from Wednesday close, but roughly -50% from pre-announcement)
- Co-founder indicted March 2026 over Nvidia chip smuggling to China
- Memory costs more than tripled (CEO Liang on May earnings call)

### Analysis
- The $39B backlog roughly equals trailing twelve-month revenue
- This is NOT a distressed raise — it's a company scrambling to fund confirmed orders
- BUT dilution math is brutal: $7B on a ~$45-48B market cap means significant per-share value transfer
- Institutional investors "sell first, ask questions later" on equity offerings of this magnitude
- Key near-term catalyst: pricing of the underwritten offering. Tight pricing = institutional confidence. Steep discount = risk appetite deteriorated
