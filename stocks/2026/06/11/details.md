- **Market tone: Risk-off/correction with dead-cat bounce attempt.** The S&P 500 tech sector is now 11% off its June 2 record, officially in correction territory. The Philly Semiconductor Index dropped 3.6% Wednesday.
- **Stocks most likely to move today:** ORCL (earnings hangover), SMCI (continued dilution fallout), ADBE (earnings after the close — binary event), AMAT/LRCX/KLAC (semiconductor equipment strength), SPCX-related read-throughs to TSLA/NVDA.

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected Trading Setup:**
- Futures pointing to a recovery bounce after Wednesday's shellacking, but premarket data shows some stock-level divergence (ORCL/SMCI deep red vs. semis equipment green).
- The move looks like a **technical oversold bounce** within a broader risk-off correction rather than a fundamental reversal.
- Key intraday risk: whether the futures rally fades at the open (typical dead-cat pattern) or holds through the session.

**Main Upside Catalysts:**
- Semiconductor equipment names rallying on sustained AI capex cycle (AMAT, LRCX, KLAC)
- MU benefiting from tight HBM/DRAM supply
- Oversold bounces in names that were indiscriminately sold Wednesday

**Main Downside Risks:**
- ORCL/SMCI continued selling could drag AI/semiconductor complex
- ADBE earnings after close — ADBE has traded lower after 9 of its last 11 earnings reports
- SpaceX IPO tonight drawing institutional liquidity out of megacap tech to fund allocations
- CPI-induced Fed hawkish repricing: rate hike probability at 75% by year-end
- S&P 500 testing 50-day MA — if it breaks, the sell signal is significant

**Sector Views:**
- **Strongest:** Semiconductor equipment, memory (MU)
- **Weakest:** AI infrastructure names with capex/funding concerns (SMCI, ORCL), megacap tech facing liquidity drain
- **Moves mostly appear:** News-driven (ORCL/SMCI dilution), positioning-driven (SpaceX liquidity rotation), and macro-driven (CPI/Fed repricing)

**What Would Change This View:**
- ADBE delivering a beat-and-raise with clear AI monetization proof points could reverse software sentiment
- De-escalation in U.S.-Iran tensions would remove the energy-inflation feedback loop
- SMCI equity offering pricing at a tight discount would signal institutional confidence in the $39B backlog

## 8. Risk Notes

- **SMCI:** $7B equity raise (~15% dilution at pre-announcement valuation); stock down 28% Wednesday. Liquidity is extreme (191M shares traded). Oversold bounce possible, but the ATM offering overhang persists through Q3.
- **ORCL:** Gap down below 100-day EMA ($185.97). Key support at $178.99 (61.8% Fibonacci retracement). If $180 fails, next support at $168.28. $40B equity/debt overhang.
- **SpaceX IPO liquidity drain:** $75B IPO is unprecedented. While IPOs rarely cause market crashes, the scale forces portfolio rebalancing. Watch NVDA/TSLA for continued pressure.
- **CPI at 4.25%:** Real yields across 2Y/3Y Treasuries now negative. Bond market demanding rate hikes. Any further escalation in oil prices could push CPI above 4.5%.
- **ADBE earnings:** Binary event on a stock at 7-year lows. Has traded lower after 9 of last 11 earnings. Needs beat + raise + AI narrative to reverse trajectory.
- **Crowded trades:** AI/momentum long positions have been getting liquidated. Semiconductor short interest may be building. Watch for short squeeze in oversold names.
- **Unconfirmed:** Retail investor allocation details for SpaceX IPO not yet finalized; demand could shift before books close.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Today's bounce is likely a dead-cat within a correction** — the S&P 500 tech sector is 11% off its peak and in correction territory. Inflation, Fed hawkishness, and geopolitical risks remain unresolved.
2. **The AI capex funding question is the week's dominant theme** — ORCL (-$23.7B FCF, $40B raise), SMCI ($7B equity raise), GOOGL ($85B stock sale) all highlight the tension between AI demand and the capital required to meet it.
3. **ADBE earnings tonight is the key binary event** — a beat-and-raise could reverse software sentiment; another miss confirms the AI commoditization fear for creative tools.
4. **SpaceX IPO (SPCX) pricing tonight and trading Friday** — $75B raise draining liquidity from megacap tech; watch for first-day price action to gauge real demand.
5. **Semiconductor equipment is the bright spot** — AMAT, LRCX, KLAC benefiting from AI fab capex cycle that shows no signs of slowing despite the AI stock correction.
