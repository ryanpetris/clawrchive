# Tech Stock Briefing - June 18, 2026 - Intel-Apple Deal, Fed Hawkish Hold, Chip Rebound

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Rebound after Fed sell-off**: Nasdaq 100 futures are up ~1.5% and S&P 500 futures +0.8% as tech stocks recover from Wednesday's Fed-driven rout. The Dow shed 507 points (-0.98%) Wednesday after the FOMC dot plot signaled a possible rate hike this year.
- **Intel surges ~9-10% premarket** after President Trump announced Apple will partner with Intel to design and manufacture chips in the U.S. — a landmark win for Intel's foundry ambitions and U.S. semiconductor reshoring.
- **U.S.-Iran deal signed**: Washington and Tehran finalized an electronic peace agreement, sending oil prices lower (WTI ~$75.99) and easing inflation concerns that had amplified the Fed hawkishness.
- **Fed's Warsh holds rates but signals tightening**: First FOMC meeting under Chair Kevin Warsh held rates at 3.50%-3.75%, but the dot plot median moved to 3.8% (from 3.4% in March), with 9 of 18 officials expecting at least one hike in 2026.
- **Apple warns of price hikes**: CEO Tim Cook told the WSJ that memory chip cost increases are "unsustainable" and price increases are "unavoidable" — the iPhone 18 Pro could see $200+ increases.
- **Meta tumbled 5.44%** on a double blow: the reported departure of AI-for-Work product head Emily Dalton Smith, plus the broad tech sell-off from the Fed's hawkish shift.
- **Micron targets surge ahead of June 24 earnings**: TD Cowen raised target to $1,500, RBC to $1,200, and Aletheia to $1,600. MU is up ~4-5% premarket.
- **Overall tone: semiconductor-led, AI-driven rebound**. The 3-5 stocks most likely to move today: **INTC** (Apple deal catalyst), **MU** (earnings anticipation + analyst upgrades), **AMD** (Citi upgrade to Buy, Meta GPU potential), **META** (AI exec departure overhang), **AVGO** (continued strength after +4.3% Wednesday).

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected trading setup**: Risk-on open driven by semiconductor strength, the Intel-Apple catalyst, and easing geopolitical tensions (U.S.-Iran deal, falling oil). The Fed hangover from Wednesday may persist as a ceiling on multiple expansion, but the combination of lower oil + chip-specific catalysts is driving a strong rebound in tech.

**Main upside catalysts**:
- Intel-Apple deal could spark a re-rating of semiconductor manufacturing/reshoring plays
- U.S.-Iran peace deal progress continues to lower oil and inflation fears
- Micron earnings anticipation building for June 24
- Short-covering potential after Wednesday's sell-off

**Main downside risks**:
- Fed dot plot remains hawkish — any economic data surprise could accelerate hike expectations
- Hyperscaler capex/FCF squeeze could dampen AI infrastructure enthusiasm
- Market enters a 4-day weekend (Juneteenth Friday + weekend) — position-squaring could limit gains
- Meta AI exec departure raises questions about AI strategy execution

**Strongest subsectors**: Semiconductors (especially foundry/manufacturing: INTC), memory (MU, Hynix), AI hardware supply chain

**Weakest subsectors**: Mega-cap platforms hit by rate sensitivity (MSFT, AMZN, META), consumer hardware exposed to memory costs (AAPL)

**Nature of moves**: Today's bounce appears primarily **news-driven** (Intel-Apple, U.S.-Iran deal) and **technical** (recovery from Wednesday's oversold conditions), layered on the structural AI investment theme. The semiconductor move has fundamental support from analyst upgrades and forward earnings expectations.

**What would change this view**: Any reversal on the U.S.-Iran deal would spike oil and rekindle inflation fears. A negative Micron pre-announcement would damage the entire semi thesis. Conversely, a definitive Fed communication softening the hike signal would remove the main macro headwind.
