## Intel-Apple Partnership Analysis

**What was announced**:
- Trump on Truth Social: Apple agreed to work with Intel to design and manufacture chips in U.S.
- Intel's 18A-P process node entered risk production on June 16
- Intel to manufacture at Oregon, Arizona, and Ohio fabs
- Part of broader administration push including Nvidia and Elon Musk's TerraFab
- U.S. government holds ~10% stake in Intel worth ~$50B+ (originally ~$8.9B/$10B investment)

**Key caveat**: Neither Apple nor Intel has officially confirmed the deal. Trump's announcement follows preliminary agreement reports from WSJ in May.

**What it means for Intel**:
- Foundry validation: Apple as anchor customer would transform Intel's foundry narrative
- Revenue scale for IFS segment (Q1 rev $5.4B, -$2.4B operating loss)
- Stock up 200%+ YTD, market cap >$600B
- CEO Lip-Bu Tan expects multiple foundry commitments in H2 2026
- 18A-P: 9% higher perf at same power, 18% lower power at same perf

**What it means for Apple**:
- Supply chain diversification from near-total TSMC reliance
- TSMC still expected to handle majority of Apple's chip manufacturing
- Timing: Testing throughout 2026, production/delivery starting 2027 per Kuo
- Helps Apple manage chip supply amid AI-driven capacity crunch at TSMC

**What it means for TSMC**:
- Potential loss of some Apple business medium-term
- But TSMC capacity fully utilized by AI chip demand (NVDA, AMD)
- TSMC would not rule out price increases per recent BBC interview
- Competition could push TSMC to offer better terms
