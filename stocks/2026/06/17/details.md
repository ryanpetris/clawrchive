## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected Trading Setup:** Cautiously optimistic open with Nasdaq futures leading. The market is in a "wait for the Fed" holding pattern. The Dow's record close Tuesday (above 52,000 intraday) provides a positive anchor, but the semi rout and tech selloff signal profit-taking rotation.

**Main Upside Catalysts:**
- Oil below $80 on U.S.-Iran deal reduces inflation fears, giving the Fed room to sound less hawkish.
- Semi sector bounce: AMD, INTC, MU leading premarket, suggesting yesterday's SOX -5.7% was an overreaction.
- Retail sales beat could reinforce economic strength without spurring immediate rate-hike panic.

**Main Downside Risks:**
- Fed hawkish surprise: if Warsh signals balance sheet reduction speed-up or sounds more concerned about inflation, growth stocks could sell off again.
- Retail sales miss + hawkish Fed = worst case for tech multiples.
- The SOX correction could resume if the bounce fails and rate-hike expectations rise further.

**Subsector Strength:** AI infrastructure (AMD, WDC, SMCI), storage (WDC), foundry alternatives (INTC, Samsung)
**Subsector Weakness:** High-multiple software (PLTR), proprietary AI vendors exposed to export controls

**Nature of Moves:** News-driven (Fed, Iran), positioning-driven (semi oversold bounce), and event-driven (Warsh press conference). Not fundamental rotation yet.

**What Would Change This View:** A Warsh press conference that explicitly discusses AI as disinflationary (bullish for tech); a signal that balance sheet reduction will accelerate (bearish for liquidity-sensitive names); any mention of rate hike timing (bearish).

- **Macro overhang:** The 80% probability of a December rate hike caps multiple expansion for the entire tech sector, especially semis and high-growth software.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Fed is the fulcrum.** Warsh's first FOMC meeting sets the tone for the rest of 2026. The market has already priced out cuts and is now pricing hikes. A neutral-to-dovish surprise would be the biggest upside catalyst for tech.
2. **The semi rout looks like a healthy pullback in a bull trend.** Tuesday's -5.7% SOX was violent but followed a sharp Monday rally. Premarket bounce suggests dip-buyers are active. AMD, AVGO, and NVDA are the key names to watch for confirmation.
3. **The AI buildout funding story is shifting.** NVDA's $25B bond sale, ORCL's $55.7B capex, and GOOGL's $84.75B equity raise all point to a single reality: the AI infrastructure buildout requires enormous outside capital. Bond/equity markets are absorbing it, but equity holders are starting to ask questions.
4. **SpaceX is the canary in the risk-appetite coal mine.** The SPCX mania shows extreme retail enthusiasm and limited float dynamics. A SPCX reversal would likely spill over into other high-momentum tech names.
5. **Watch Thursday (last trading day before Juneteenth) for position-squaring.** With Friday's holiday closure and the Fed decision out of the way, Thursday could see amplified moves as traders adjust into the long weekend.
