## 1. Market Closed Today And Next Week Open

- **The market is closed today**, Saturday, June 27, 2026.
- **First trading day of the next week:** Monday, June 29, 2026.
- This is a **holiday-shortened week** — markets are closed Friday, July 3 for the Independence Day observance (July 4 falls on Saturday). Only four trading days: Monday through Thursday.
- The week ended Friday, June 26 with the Nasdaq suffering its worst weekly decline since April 2025 (Nasdaq Composite −4.6% for the week, S&P 500 −~2%). The Dow outperformed (+0.6%), underscoring a dramatic rotation out of tech into cyclicals and value.
- The defining event of the past week was a globally synchronized AI/semiconductor correction — the most serious test of the AI trade this year.

## 2. Week Ahead Summary

- **Expected market tone:** Event-driven, data-dependent, with elevated risk in semiconductors and AI-exposed names. The selloff was sharp and positioning-driven, not fundamentally triggered, which leaves room for both continued rotation and tactical rebounds.
- **Highest-conviction catalysts this week:** (1) **June Non-Farm Payrolls** on Thursday, July 2 (moved forward one day for the holiday), (2) Fed Chair **Jerome Powell speaks** on Wednesday, July 1, (3) **Oracle (ORCL)** earnings after close Thursday, July 2, (4) month-end and quarter-end portfolio rebalancing, (5) holiday-thinned liquidity Friday compounding residual risk.
- The AI/semiconductor selloff was driven by a convergence of valuation fatigue, rising input costs (memory), doubts about AI capex ROI, and the OpenAI IPO delay story — not a collapse in underlying AI demand. Most institutional holders view the AI infrastructure thesis as intact.
- **Key narrative tension:** The "AI winners vs. AI payers" trade has bifurcated. Memory producers (Micron etc.) reported strong results, but downstream consumers (Apple, Microsoft) are paying the price through higher component costs. This dynamic may persist.
- Software stocks (Salesforce, Microsoft, IBM) bucked the trend Friday and could provide a safe haven within tech this week.

### 5 Concise Takeaways
1. **AI trade faces its first serious test.** The selloff was sharp and globally synchronized, driven by positioning fatigue, not broken fundamentals. But the "AI winners vs. AI payers" divide is real and may persist.
2. **Software is the relative safe haven.** MSFT +5.71%, CRM +~5%, IGV ETF +4% on a day semis plunged. Rotation within tech is under way.
3. **NFP + Powell = the week's binary event.** A hot print + hawkish Fed = more tech pain. A soft print = relief rally in growth. Thursday's data is the most important single catalyst.
4. **Holiday-shortened week = thin liquidity + exaggerated moves.** With Friday closed, positioning risk is elevated. Don't chase Friday's late moves into Monday.
5. **Micron's $22B in supply agreements is the bull case anchor for semis.** Despite the selloff, AI memory demand visibility is stronger than ever. But the commoditization debate (HBM-free architectures from Qualcomm, Cerebras, Nvidia) is the growing bear case.
