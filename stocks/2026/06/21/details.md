## 7. Bottom Line

1. **This week is all about Micron (Wednesday).** The memory earnings print is the single most important catalyst for the AI trade this week. With the stock up 300% YTD and already past many analyst targets, the risk/reward is asymmetric to the downside unless Micron delivers a beat-and-raise and strong forward guidance. Watch HBM commentary and Q4 revenue guidance most closely.

2. **The macro setup has improved significantly.** The U.S.-Iran MoU and falling oil prices are the biggest macro tailwind for tech multiples since the war began in February. Lower energy = lower inflation = less Fed pressure. This is a genuine positive, but some of the relief may already be priced in after Thursday's rally.

3. **The cash-flow squeeze is real and will matter eventually.** Epoch AI's analysis that hyperscaler FCF crosses zero around Q3 2026 is the most important medium-term bear argument for AI infrastructure. It doesn't break the bull case this week, but it limits the upside for names reliant on ever-increasing customer capex.

4. **Intel's Trump-fueled rally is unconfirmed hope-trading.** The stock has tripled YTD. The Apple foundry deal would be transformational, but neither company has confirmed it. Treat the 10.6% Thursday move as speculative until Apple or Intel files a statement.

5. **Positioning is crowded in AI memory and semiconductors.** MU and Sandisk have run past analyst price targets. NVDA is just below its 50-day moving average. The equal-weight Nasdaq (QQQE) is outperforming the cap-weighted QQQ in June, suggesting breadth is improving but the Mag 7 concentration risk is being actively hedged. Favour exposure through diversified vehicles (QQQ, SMH) or best-in-class names (NVDA, GOOGL) rather than high-beta single-name bets before Micron's print.
