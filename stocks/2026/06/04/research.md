## Broadcom (AVGO) — Deeper Dive

**Q2 FY2026 Results (filed 6/3 after close):**
- Revenue: $22.187B vs $22.716B consensus (miss of ~2.3%)
- YoY growth: +48% (still impressive, but market wanted more)
- GAAP net profit: $9.310B, +88% YoY
- AI semiconductor revenue: ~$10.8B (record, doubled YoY)
- Infrastructure software: $7.18B vs $7.32B consensus
- Q3 guidance: ~$32.4B revenue vs $29.768B consensus (above consensus but below whisper numbers)
- Full-year AI chip target: $56B maintained, $100B long-range target unchanged

**Key Call Quote:** CEO Hock Tan said the growing AI semiconductor business is "diluting gross margin" — a structural concern for custom chip profitability. Six core AI custom chip customers: Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI + two unnamed.

**Valuation Context:** AVGO was trading at ~93.5x P/E heading into earnings. The market was pricing in several years of hypergrowth. A 2.3% revenue miss at that multiple triggers disproportionate downside. The ~15% premarket drop brings P/E closer to 80x, still elevated.

**The Bull Case:** AI rev doubled. Q3 guidance beat consensus. The $100B long-term target was maintained. This is a high-quality AI franchise that just failed to clear a very high bar. Long-term holders may view the dip as an entry point.

**The Bear Case:** The infrastructure software miss is a concern — it shows not every segment is growing at AI pace. AI margin dilution is a structural headwind. Hock Tan didn't raise the long-range target — if anything, the unchanged guidance suggests the current trajectory is on track, not accelerating.
