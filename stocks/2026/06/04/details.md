# Tech Stock Briefing - June 4, 2026 - Broadcom Selloff, Semiconductor Contagion

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Broadcom (AVGO) -15% premarket:** Q2 FY2026 revenue of $22.19B missed the $22.72B consensus by ~2.3%. AI semiconductor revenue doubled to $10.8B, a record, but CEO Hock Tan did not raise the full-year $100B AI chip sales target. Infrastructure software revenue of $7.18B also missed estimates ($7.32B). The mixed message — record AI sales alongside a revenue miss and unchanged long-range guidance — disappointed investors who had bid AVGO to a 93.5x P/E.
- **CrowdStrike (CRWD) -11% premarket:** Beat Q1 FY2027 EPS and revenue estimates, but in-line guidance and rising expenses weighed on a stock that had rallied 60% YTD. The market priced in a bigger beat given the cybersecurity demand environment in the age of advanced AI.
- **SMCI -7% premarket:** Profit-taking after a 145% breakout rally. A $2B AI infrastructure deal with Gorilla Technology for Yotta Data Services (India) was overshadowed by GRRR's shelf registration filing. SMCI also unveiled new AI rack-scale platforms aligned with NVDA Vera Rubin, AMD Helios, and Intel Xeon 6+.
- **Broader semiconductor selloff:** AMD, Intel, Arm Holdings, Marvell Technology all fell more than 3% premarket on Broadcom contagion. SOXX/semiconductor ETFs leading the decline.
- **Geopolitical risk elevated:** Oil prices near $100/bbl as U.S.-Iran tensions escalate following missile launches at Kuwait and Bahrain. Brent crude near $95. The OECD cut its global growth outlook citing Middle East conflict damage. Markets are pricing an 85% probability of a Fed rate hike by year-end (up from 60% last week).
- **10Y Treasury yield at 4.49%** — easing slightly from recent highs but supported by oil-driven inflation concerns and hawkish Fed repricing.
- **Nvidia (NVDA) holding relatively better** as Jensen Huang's Computex keynote pushed the AI PC vision, Vera Rubin data center alignment, and a unified agentic AI stack with Microsoft. Nvidia is up 49% since Jan 2025, one of only two Mag-7 names beating the S&P 500 (along with GOOGL +88%).
- **Apple WWDC 2026** begins Monday June 8. AI-powered software upgrades expected across iOS, macOS, and visionOS. Key event for AAPL sentiment.
- **SpaceX IPO roadshow begins today:** $135/share, targeting a record $75B raise at a ~$1.75T valuation. Debut on Nasdaq on June 12. Morningstar values SpaceX at just $780B — less than half the IPO target.

**Market tone:** Risk-off, semiconductor-led, event-driven.

**Stocks most likely to move today:** AVGO (premarket -15%), CRWD (-11%), SMCI (-7%), AMD/INTC/ARM/MRVL (-3%+), with NVDA and AAPL likely relative strength.

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected trading setup:** Risk-off open with Nasdaq futures leading declines. The semiconductor sector is the primary drag, driven by Broadcom's earnings disappointment and cascading through the entire chip complex. The AVGO/CRWD earnings reactions will set the tone for the first hour.

**Main upside catalysts:**
- Dip-buying into AVGO and CRWD on the view that fundamental AI demand remains strong (AVGO AI rev doubled, CRWD beat EPS)
- NVDA relative strength from Computex momentum may support the broader AI infrastructure theme
- SpaceX IPO roadshow could drive positive sentiment for tech IPO comps
- Potential for pre-payrolls positioning to stabilize markets

**Main downside risks:**
- Broadcom contagion spreading beyond semiconductors into the broader tech complex
- Iran/oil headlines escalating further, pushing oil above $100
- Fed rate hike repricing continuing to pressure growth stocks
- SMCI profit-taking extending
- May payrolls miss on Friday adding macro uncertainty

**Subsector strength/weakness:**
- Weakest: Semiconductors (SOXX), cybersecurity (CRWD pulling down sector)
- Most resilient: Large-cap AI platforms (NVDA, GOOGL, MSFT), Apple (WWDC catalyst ahead)

**Nature of moves:** The AVGO/CRWD selloffs are news-driven earnings reactions. The broader semiconductor weakness is contagion/positioning-driven. Oil/Iran moves are macro/geopolitical. The Fed repricing is rates-driven.

**What would change this view:** A strong payrolls number Friday that signals a soft-landing economy (would ease recession fears). De-escalation headlines on Iran (would lower oil and rate hike expectations). Dip-buying on AVGO that recovers most of the premarket loss.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **AVGO at 93.5x P/E was priced for perfection.** The 2.3% revenue miss is modest, but the multiple amplifies the downside. The ~15% premarket drop may overcorrect if dip-buyers step in, but margin concerns (AI mix diluting gross margins) may persist.
- **CRWD was also in high-expectation territory** at +60% YTD. The stock beating estimates but falling 11% is a classic "sell the news" reaction. Fundamentals remain strong but the buying-the-dip thesis needs a clear catalyst.
- **SMCI's 145% rally** from lows makes it vulnerable to extended profit-taking. The $2B India deal is real but GRRR's shelf registration is a financing overhang for the partnership.
- **Oil near $100** is a stagflation risk for growth stocks. The 85% probability of a Fed hike by year-end is a rapid repricing that could accelerate if oil keeps rising.
- **SpaceX IPO at a $1.75T valuation** is ambitious. Morningstar's $780B valuation is a sharp warning for IPO investors. The gap between IPO price and fundamental valuation is unusually wide.
- **Friday's payrolls** is a binary macro event. If payrolls are too hot (fueling rate hike fears) or too cold (recession signal), it could compound today's weakness.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Today is about earnings hangover and geopolitics.** AVGO and CRWD earnings reactions dominate the tape, with semiconductor contagion spreading to AMD, INTC, ARM, and MRVL. Oil/Iran is the macro backdrop that amplifies the move.
2. **Broadcom's selloff is a high-multiple correction, not a fundamental breakdown.** AI revenue doubled, guidance was raised, and the long-term pipeline ($100B target) was reaffirmed. But at 93.5x P/E, the market demanded perfection.
3. **NVDA and GOOGL remain the leaders** for a reason. NVDA has Computex momentum and Vera Rubin alignment. GOOGL is the YTD Mag-7 outperformer with Ark buying $95.6M. Both are likely relative strength plays today.
4. **Watch Friday's payrolls as the next major catalyst.** If nonfarm payrolls are solid but not too hot, it could stabilize risk sentiment going into next week's Apple WWDC.
5. **SpaceX IPO (June 12) is the next big narrative event** for tech. The roadshow starting today will test institutional appetite for a record $75B raise at a $1.75T valuation.
