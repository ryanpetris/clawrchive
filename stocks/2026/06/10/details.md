# Tech Stock Briefing - June 10, 2026 - Tech Bloodbath, SpaceX IPO Frenzy, CPI Day

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Market tone:** Risk-off, AI-led selloff, liquidity-squeeze driven. The Nasdaq Composite suffered its worst single-day decline of 2026 on Friday (roughly -4%), and selling has continued into Wednesday.
- The broad tech rout is being attributed to a combination of factors: SpaceX's record IPO ($250B demand, 4x oversubscribed) creating a liquidity vacuum, a disappointing Broadcom AI chip outlook, strong May jobs data reviving rate-hike expectations, escalating U.S.-Iran tensions, and growing AI valuation concerns.
- **3-5 stocks most likely to move today:** **SMCI** (down 12% premarket on $7B financing), **ORCL** (reports FQ4 earnings after close), and **AAPL** (post-WWDC slide continues), **MU** (semi rout continues, CPO concerns from SemiAnalysis), **AVGO** (AI XPV platform announcement with Apollo/Blackstone).
- **Key macro event:** May CPI at 8:30am ET — expected headline +4.2% y/y, core +2.9% y/y. A hot print could accelerate the rotation out of tech.
- U.S. launched self-defense strikes against Iran after an American helicopter was downed, escalating Middle East tensions and pushing oil prices higher.
- Institutional selling of NVDA continues: 13F filings show 436M fewer shares held by institutions QoQ as of March 31.
- TSMC reported record May revenue of NT$417B (+30.1% YoY) but ADRs fell premarket on broad sector weakness.
- SoftBank's $6B OpenAI margin loan discussions have stalled, and SK Hynix is reportedly eyeing a $14B US listing as soon as August.

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Trading setup:** Risk-off open expected with Nasdaq futures down ~1.4%. CPI at 8:30am ET is the key binary event. If CPI prints in-line or below, expect a partial snap-back as dip buyers step in. If CPI prints hot (headline above +4.2%), brace for the selloff to accelerate into the close.

**Main upside catalysts:**
- A "soft" CPI print could trigger short covering and a relief rally in semis and mega-cap tech.
- Oracle earnings after close: a strong AI cloud revenue beat could provide a positive counter-narrative.
- Dip buyers may view the SpaceX-liquidity-squeeze thesis as transient and buy the rotation.

**Main downside risks:**
- Hot CPI accelerates rate-hike expectations, hitting long-duration tech multiples hard.
- SpaceX IPO pricing Thursday could keep liquidity tight through the week.
- U.S.-Iran escalation could push oil higher, feeding inflation fears.
- SMCI's $7B financing sets a bearish precedent for other AI-infra names needing capital.

**Subsector strength:** Gaming stocks (DKNG +11.3%, FLUT +6.1%) are bucking the trend — consumer/entertainment plays benefiting from rotation out of AI.

**Subsector weakness:** Semis (SOX), AI infrastructure, cloud software.

**What would change this view:** A materially soft CPI print (headline <4.0%) that reverses the rate narrative. Or a SpaceX IPO surprise that brings liquidity back into the market faster than expected.

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Unconfirmed claims:** The "SpaceX liquidity squeeze" theory is widely cited but difficult to verify. Some of the selling is genuine AI sector repositioning.
- **Low-liquidity names:** Avoid small-cap AI names during this environment — liquidity is being absorbed by mega-cap and IPO demand.
- **Crowded trades:** Long AI/semis has been one of the most crowded trades on Wall Street. The unwind has been violent but arguably overdue.
- **Binary events:** CPI today (8:30am ET), ORCL earnings tonight, SpaceX pricing tomorrow, FOMC June 16-17.
- **Macro risks:** U.S.-Iran escalation, oil price spike, renewed inflation, rate hike repricing.
- **Stale data:** 13F data is as of March 31 — selling may have accelerated or reversed since then. Cathie Wood buying NVDA suggests the selling narrative is not universal.
- **SemiAnalysis CPO report:** Co-Packaged Optics production timeline concerns could have longer-lasting implications if major capex plans get pushed out.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **Today is all about CPI.** The 8:30am ET print will dictate whether we see a relief rally or deeper losses. Tech is oversold short-term, but the macro setup (strong jobs, sticky inflation, Iran tensions) is hostile to growth multiples.
2. **The SpaceX IPO is a genuine liquidity event.** $250B in demand doesn't disappear — some of it will rotate back. But pricing Thursday means the squeeze persists today.
3. **SMCI's $7B financing is a warning for AI infrastructure names.** If market leaders need to dilute to fund demand, the AI capex ROI narrative gets harder. Watch how other names (DELL, HPE) trade on this read-through.
4. **Software is getting re-rated lower on AI disruption fears — CRM is the canary.** If Salesforce can't monetize Agentforce convincingly, the whole software sector's AI premium is at risk.
5. **Semis face a two-front war:** valuation compression from rates and supply concerns (CPO). But TSMC's record revenue and SK Hynix's $1T+ market cap are reality checks that AI demand is real — the question is timing and price.
