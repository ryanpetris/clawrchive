## Micron Earnings Deep Dive

**Key Metrics:**
- Q3 (ended May 28, 2026): Revenue $41.46B vs $35.7B consensus ($23.86B prior Q, $9.30B year-ago)
- YoY growth: +346% (fifth consecutive quarterly record)
- Non-GAAP EPS: $25.11 vs $20.49 consensus
- GAAP net income: $28.9B
- Gross margin: 84.9% (GAAP); guided ~86% for Q4
- DRAM revenue: $31.3B (+343% YoY) — record
- NAND revenue: $9.9B (+361% YoY)
- Cloud memory business unit: rev $13.77B (from $3.39B year ago), op margin 78%
- Q4 guide: Revenue ~$50B (±$1B), EPS ~$31 (±$1), gross margin ~86%

**Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs):**
- 16 signed, most covering 2026-2030
- ~20% of DRAM volume, ~1/3 of NAND volume
- Floor price ensures "very robust" gross margins "well above peak quarterly margins in any past cycle"
- Ceiling price protects customers
- $22B expected deposits/commitments ($18B cash deposits)
- ~$100B in minimum-price remaining performance obligations
- Customers pay up front — helps fund fab expansion
- Will account for ~40% of Micron revenue

**CEO Commentary:**
- "No line of sight" to AI memory supply catching up with demand — shortages expected past 2027
- Expects tight conditions "to persist beyond calendar 2027"
- Conventional server growth expected in mid-teens during CY2026
- "Modest reduction" in average server DRAM content growth as customers focus on maximizing unit shipments
- Humanoid robots expected to require 10x more memory — potential multi-decade demand cycle starting late 2020s
- DRAM output in CY2026 to grow "low- to mid-20s percentage range"

**Capex:**

- FY2026: ~$27B
- FY2027: quarterly capex running above Q4 FY2026 levels
