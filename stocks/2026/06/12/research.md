## Uncertainty Notes / Items to Monitor

1. **SpaceX IPO price discovery:** At $1.77T, the valuation is unprecedented for a newly-public company. Limited float + massive retail demand = unpredictable first-day trading.
2. **Fed meeting next week:** June 17-18 FOMC. Dot plot will be critical. No rate change expected, but the dots could shift hawkish after CPI 4.2% and PPI 6.5%.
3. **Intel sustainability:** The 463% one-year rally and BofA upgrade are compelling, but institutional ownership at 16% is a double-edged sword — room to buy in, but also implies most active managers have been skeptical for a reason.
4. **AI software monetization gap:** Oracle and Adobe are demonstrating that AI-related spending (capex) is visible and rewarded in hardware, but AI-driven revenue in software is not yet material enough to satisfy investors.
5. **Memory cycle peak timing:** Micron's June 24 earnings will test whether the HBM-driven memory supercycle is still in mid-cycle expansion or approaching peak.
6. **U.S.-Iran deal execution risk:** The market is pricing a deal. If negotiations collapse or drag out, oil rebounds and the inflation / rate-hike narrative reasserts itself.
