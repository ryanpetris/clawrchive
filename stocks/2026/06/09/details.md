## 1. Executive Summary

- **Semiconductor recovery extends into a second day:** Nasdaq 100 futures +0.76%, S&P 500 futures +0.44%, Dow futures +0.24% as the chip bounce continues and Middle East tensions ease. Oil prices retreated on reports of an Israel-Iran ceasefire holding.

The company is valued at $852B privately, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading. No timing confirmed yet. Follows Anthropic's confidential filing and precedes SpaceX's June 12 debut.
- **Analyst upgrades for semis are pouring in:** Wells Fargo more than doubled MU target to $1,220. Cantor Fitzgerald raised to $1,500. Morgan Stanley to $1,050. JP Morgan raised QCOM target to $265, and praised by Jensen Huang in Seoul.
- **Oracle earnings tomorrow after close is the week's dominant company catalyst.** $19.1B revenue (+20% YoY), EPS $1.96 consensus. The $553B RPO backlog and OCI growth rate are the key metrics. Implied options move: 11.22%, or ~$71B in market cap swing.
- **CPI data tomorrow at 8:30am ET is the dominant macro risk.** Headline CPI expected +0.3% m/m (4.2% y/y). A hot print would cement rate-hike expectations into new Fed Chair Warsh's first FOMC next week.
- **May CPI Wednesday, followed by PPI Thursday, Oracle earnings Wednesday, ECB Thursday, SpaceX IPO Friday** — this is the most consequential midweek of macro and tech catalysts in months.

**Market tone:** Semiconductor-led recovery / pre-CPI caution / AI-led / rates-driven

## 7. Outlook For Today

**Expected Trading Setup:** Constructive continuation of Monday's semiconductor-led recovery. Premarket futures indicate a second day of buying in semis (MU +6.56%, AMD +3.59%, AVGO +3.41%, INTC +3.06%, NVDA +2.53%). However, the dominant sentiment driver is pre-positioning ahead of tomorrow's CPI print and Oracle earnings. Expect lower conviction and potentially choppy action as traders size positions for binary events.

**Main Upside Catalysts:**
- The analyst upgrade cycle for MU is extraordinary — three firms more than doubled their targets. This creates a powerful narrative for memory/semi dip-buying
- Jensen Huang's continued Korea tour provides positive AI news flow
- Middle East ceasefire seems to be holding, driving oil lower (removing an inflation input)
- OpenAI's S-1 filing adds to the positive AI sentiment cycle
- SpaceX IPO on Friday sets up a "big IPO" risk-on appetite signal

**Main Downside Risks:**
- **CPI tomorrow is the dominant risk.** If headline CPI prints above 4.2% y/y, the rate-hike narrative becomes the consensus view going into Warsh's FOMC. This could trigger another leg lower in tech multiples.
- Apple WWDC disappointment may weigh on AAPL and have moderate spillover to tech sentiment broadly
- Friday's selloff was the deepest in years — positioning unwind may not be complete. The VIX at ~20 still suggests elevated uncertainty.
- 10Y yield at 4.55% and DXY firm continue to pressure growth stock valuations
- Broadcom's failure to raise AI guidance (from June 3) is still a subtle overhang for the semis recovery

**Subsector Strength/Weakness:** Memory semis (MU, SNDK) are the strongest subsector today. AI infrastructure (NVDA, SMCI) are constructive. Software/cloud is cautious ahead of ORCL Wednesday and ADBE Thursday. Mega-cap platforms are mixed — AAPL weak post-WWDC, GOOGL benefiting from Gemini validation, META under dilution overhang.

**Fundamental vs. Positioning vs. Macro:** This remains primarily a positioning-driven recovery from an overbought unwind. The fundamental AI thesis (spending, HBM demand, data center buildout) is intact, but the macro backdrop (rates, inflation, CPI risk) is deteriorating. Today is a "buy the dip/recovery" day positioned within a "sell the CPI" risk framework.

**What Would Change This View:**
- A hot CPI print tomorrow confirms rate-hike risk → likely renewed tech selling
- A clear break above Friday's close on NVDA ($205 → $220+) would signal positioning unwind is over
- Any escalation in Iran-Israel (ceasefire collapse) reintroduces geopolitical risk-off
- Oracle earnings beats with strong OCI/AI backlog growth → bullish read-through for AI infrastructure

## 8. Risk Notes

- **Binary events in 48 hours:** CPI Wednesday 8:30am ET, ORCL earnings after close Wednesday, PPI Thursday, ADBE earnings Thursday, ECB Thursday, SpaceX IPO Friday. Today is a pre-positioning day with elevated event risk.
- **Unconfirmed claims:** Meta equity raise is still an FT report; company called "pure speculation." Intel as Google/Nvidia backup chipmaker from The Information report — unconfirmed by any company.

- **Crowded trades:** The AI/semiconductor trade remains one of the most crowded in the market. This limits upside in any recovery rally because there's no sidelined capital to deploy.
- **Macro risks:** 10Y at 4.55%, DXY firm, rate-hike odds at 70%. This trifecta historically compresses tech multiples, particularly for high-duration names like NVDA, PLTR, and ARM.
- **Apple post-WWDC drift:** AAPL's $215 bear case exists if the Gemini-Siri deal draws antitrust scrutiny or if the Siri experience in public beta underperforms expectations. The binary range ($215-$400) makes AAPL one of the highest-variance mega-cap names.
- **Stale news:** The Broadcom earnings disappointment is now nearly a week old. Most negative repricing has occurred. However, if Oracle earnings show a similar pattern (strong results, no guidance raise), the "AI spending without returns" concern will resurface.

## 9. Bottom Line

1. **The semi recovery is extending into day two, but CPI is the real test.** MU's analyst upgrade cascade provides fundamental cover for the bounce, but tomorrow's inflation data will determine whether this was a bear-market rally or a genuine reset. Position accordingly.

2. **Three historic tech IPOs are converging.** OpenAI's confidential S-1 (confirmed Monday) joins Anthropic's and SpaceX's filings for what could be the three largest IPOs on record. The first to trade — SpaceX this Friday — serves as a sentiment barometer for the AI risk appetite cycle.

3. **Apple's WWDC verdict is in: evolutionary, not revolutionary.** AAPL at $300 with a $215-$400 analyst range is the most asymmetric mega-cap bet in the market. The next catalyst is July's public beta reviews and the late-July earnings call where AI-tier attach rates must be quantified.

4. **Memory is the strongest subsector in semis right now.** MU and SNDK have the clearest fundamental catalyst (HBM sold out through 2026, Huang's multi-year shortage call, multiple massive analyst upgrades). Memory stocks are the cleanest thesis in the AI trade.

5. **Tomorrow (Wednesday, June 10) is the most consequential day of the week.** CPI at 8:30am ET sets the macro tone, then Oracle earnings after close tests the AI infrastructure demand thesis. Any tech gains today should be treated as pre-positioning, not conviction buying.
