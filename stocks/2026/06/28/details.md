### 5 Takeaways

1. **Last week's selloff was a rotation, not a crash.** The Dow was flat while Nasdaq dropped 4.6%. Money moved into healthcare, utilities, small caps — not out of equities altogether. The AI supercycle thesis remains intact for most long-term holders.

2. **The memory cycle is this quarter's defining tech narrative.** Micron's record margins are a double-edged sword: great for MU, painful for AAPL, MSFT, and consumer tech. Watch for margin commentary from downstream companies.

3. **Macro is moving in the right direction for tech.** Oil below $72, Hormuz normalization, easing yields, and a softening labor market all reduce pressure on growth stocks. The jobs report Thursday is the hinge point.

4. **Valuations have reset attractively in spots.** META at 8.5x, AMZN at 10x, NVDA at 12x forward sales, MSFT at 13x forward cash flow. These are not bubble multiples. The selloff was about positioning, not fundamentals.

5. **This week is a transition week.** Light earnings calendar, holiday compression (Mon-Thu only), quarter-end flows, and binary payroll risk suggest patience. Aggressive positioning should wait for Thursday's data. The second-half setup looks more positive than most expect.
