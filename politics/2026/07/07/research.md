### 3. Supreme Court Term Analysis (Concluded June/July 2026)

**Term Assessment:**
- Unitary executive theory won out broadly
- Trump v. Slaughter: President can fire independent agency heads (except Fed)
- Trump v. Cook: Fed governors protected — independence preserved
- Emergency tariffs: struck down (February ruling)
- Birthright citizenship: executive order struck down as unconstitutional
- Shadow docket: 63 orders in 2024-25 term — more than merits decisions (56)
- Voting Rights Act: nearly impossible to use to challenge racial gerrymandering
- Campaign spending: unlimited coordinated party spending allowed
- Court also curbed Congress' power on damages, allowed continued $4B foreign aid freeze

**Institutional Dynamics:**
- Roberts wrote all three major rulings against Trump
- Constitutional crisis talk receded — no defiance of court orders
- But court is increasingly willing to cast off constraints and claim power for itself
- Georgetown's Vladeck: "Supreme Court rules for itself, 6-3"
