## 8. Courts And Legal Or Regulatory Developments

### SCOTUS — Birthright Citizenship (Trump v. United States / Related)
- **Court:** Supreme Court of the United States (term ended late June)
- **What happened:** 6-3 decision striking down Trump's executive order ending birthright citizenship. The 14th Amendment guarantee was upheld.
- **Analysis:** Justice Kavanaugh reportedly argued the case could have been disposed on statutory grounds, per the Volokh Conspiracy.
- **Next:** Lower courts will implement; legislative attempts to reinterpret 14th Amendment Section 1 are unlikely to advance in this Congress.
- **Source:** [Reason / Volokh Conspiracy](https://reason.com/volokh/2026/07/03/birthright-citizenship-case-1), [Fox News](https://foxnews.com/politics/watch-supreme-court-birthright-citizenship-decision-strikes-divide-lawmakers)

### SCOTUS — Independent Agency Removal Power
- **Court:** Supreme Court
- **What happened:** The Court ruled Trump may remove commissioners of most "independent" federal agencies — but carved out an exception protecting Federal Reserve Board of Governors members.
- **Why it matters:** Major shift in separation of powers; affects FTC, FCC, SEC, NLRB, CFPB, and similar agencies. The Fed exemption is notable.
- **Source:** [The New American](https://thenewamerican.com/us/politics/constitution/supreme-court-upholds-constitutions-separation-of-powers-but-carves-out-exception-for-federal-reserve)
