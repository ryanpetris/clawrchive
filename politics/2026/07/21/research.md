## Arizona Primary — Additional Context

### Governor Race
- The Arizona primary used to be in August; lawmakers moved it to July to give election officials time to meet recount deadlines before November.
- Gov. Katie Hobbs (D) defeated Kari Lake by less than 1% in 2022. She is unopposed for renomination.
- The GOP primary has four candidates: Rep. Andy Biggs (Trump-endorsed, former House Freedom Caucus chair), Rep. David Schweikert, Ken Miceli. Biggs is the frontrunner in polling and prediction markets.
- Biggs was among Republicans who sought to block certification of the 2020 election.
- The general election is expected to be highly competitive — Hobbs is one of only two Democratic governors seeking reelection in states Trump carried in 2024.

## House Legislative Agenda — Deeper Context

### Johnson's CR Strategy
The conventional reading: Johnson may be setting himself up to win in September by losing in July. A failed CR vote would give him political cover to argue that Republicans exhausted the normal appropriations path before resorting to reconciliation for government funding (Reconciliation 3.0 Plus). Conservatives have been pushing to use reconciliation for appropriations, but appropriators like Rep. Valadao (R-Calif.) call it a "bad idea."

### Reconciliation 3.0 Details
- The budget resolution instructs four committees to draft legislation: Armed Services, Intelligence, Agriculture, and Administration.
- The resolution does not require offsets — a major sticking point for fiscal conservatives.
- $60B is for Iran war funding; the Pentagon warns it's running out of money.
- The SAVE Act provisions ($10B) would incentivize states to adopt voter ID and proof-of-citizenship registration requirements.
- The Senate needs to adopt the same budget resolution to unlock reconciliation. Senate Republicans are skeptical — Thune called it "a much easier proposition in the House."

### NDAA Procedural History
- In 2023, a similar failed rule on a defense spending bill paved the way for Kevin McCarthy's removal as Speaker.
- The current House rule (H.Res. 1398) would have merged the NDAA with SAVE Act after House passage. Failed 198-224 on June 30 with 14 GOP defectors.
- Senate version (S. 4784) cleared committee 18-9 on June 10 but cloture failed 50-46 on July 14 — all but one Republican voted for cloture.
