# Daily Political Outlook — July 21, 2026

**Tone of the day:** Legislative — the House is in its final week before August recess, with a packed agenda. Arizona holds its primary election today.

**Top developments:**

- **Arizona primary elections today:** Voters head to the polls for governor, US House, and state races. The Republican primary for governor is a showdown between Reps. Andy Biggs (Trump-endorsed) and David Schweikert. Gov. Katie Hobbs (D) is unopposed for renomination. Two competitive House seats (AZ-01 and AZ-06) are key to November control of the House.

- **House faces a jam-packed legislative week:** Speaker Johnson plans votes on the Reconciliation 3.0 budget resolution ($95B for Iran war funding, farm aid, and SAVE Act provisions), a clean CR to fund government through Dec. 4, a second try at the NDAA, and a stock trading ban.

- **SAVE America Act maneuvers continue:** Trump's Thursday primetime speech failed to unlock Senate votes. He's now pushing House Republicans to unite behind the budget resolution that would bypass the Senate filibuster. Several GOP House members are opposed over lack of offsets.

- **Iran war casualties:** Two US soldiers killed in an Iranian ballistic missile/drone attack in Jordan on Friday have been identified: 1st Lt. Tyler James Feehan, 25, and Pvt. Isabella Gonzales, 19. A third service member is missing with unidentified remains recovered. US death toll in the war now at 17.

- **Trump orders review of climate science guidance for federal judges:** He ordered federal suspension and debarment officials to review the National Academy of Sciences' conduct over climate change manuals that had been used by federal judges.

- **FDA cyclospora false positive:** The FDA announced a lettuce sample from Taylor Farms that tested positive for cyclospora was a false positive, but the epidemiological investigation and recall continue.
