## 3. Congress: Senate

### Lindsey Graham (R-SC) — Death and Succession

- **What happened:** Graham died Saturday evening, July 12, at age 71. Preliminary medical examiner findings show cause of death as a rupture in his aorta.
- **Why it matters:** Graham chaired the Senate Budget Committee and was a key Trump ally on defense, judiciary, and foreign policy. He was central to negotiations on the third budget reconciliation bill for defense spending and was an outspoken advocate for Iran war funding.
- **Replacement process:** South Carolina Gov. Henry McMaster (R) will appoint an interim replacement. Per state law, a special election will follow. Multiple reports indicate an August Republican primary is expected for the full six-year term.
- **FBI involvement:** FBI Director Kash Patel confirmed the FBI is assisting in the investigation, though preliminary findings suggest natural causes. [Source](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/5964793-fbi-assists-in-graham-death)
- **Committee impact:** Graham's death creates vacancies on Budget (chair), Judiciary, Appropriations, and Armed Services. A GOP committee leadership reshuffle is expected this week.
- **Sources:** [Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/07/13/this-week-do-over-time) | [CNN](https://cnn.com/2026/07/12/politics/lindsey-graham-replacement-senate) | [POLITICO](https://politico.com/news/2026/07/12/lindsey-graham-death-replacement-process-00994113) | [KCRA](https://kcra.com/article/lindsey-graham-dead-71/71908465)

### Mitch McConnell (R-KY) — Health Update

- **What happened:** McConnell broke weeks of silence Sunday with a statement revealing that a fall led to his hospitalization. He was "briefly unconscious," developed "a mild case of pneumonia," and has undergone a battery of tests.
- **Confirmed:** No broken bones, concussion, heart attack, stroke, tumors, or hemorrhages.
- **Return timeline:** McConnell said he will not return "quite yet."
- **Why it matters:** His continued absence leaves Senate Republicans down two members total, complicating Majority Leader John Thune's ability to advance party-line priorities.
- **Sources:** [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/mitch-mcconnell-hospitalized-fall-health-senate-d708e9a1f18763fbb961fd3879227ce3) | [Washington Post](https://washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/07/12/mitch-mcconnell-says-he-was-hospitalized-due-fall-contracted-pneumonia/) | [BBC](https://bbc.com/news/articles/cvge2nd611zo)

### Sen. Adam Schiff (D-CA) — Iran War Powers Resolution

- **What happened:** Schiff announced he will introduce another war powers resolution to force Republicans to vote on ending hostilities with Iran.
- **Why it matters:** Previous similar resolutions have failed but forced politically difficult votes for Republicans. As the Iran conflict escalates, these votes become more potent.
- **Expected next step:** Resolution introduced this week; vote timing uncertain.
- **Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/5965316-conflict-iran-schiff-resolution)
