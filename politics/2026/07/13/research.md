## Lindsey Graham Succession Details

- **Cause of death:** Ruptured aorta (aortic dissection/aneurysm). FBI assisting per Kash Patel, though preliminary exam suggests natural causes.
- **Senate seat replacement:** South Carolina law gives Gov. Henry McMaster (R) authority to appoint an interim replacement. McMaster is expected to act quickly (within days). The replacement will serve until a special election.
- **Special election:** Per POLITICO, an August primary is expected for the full six-year term. This creates a compressed, high-intensity campaign cycle.
- **Committee impact:** Graham chaired the Budget Committee. His death also affects Judiciary, Appropriations, and Armed Services committee assignments. A reshuffle of GOP committee leadership is expected.
- **Budget reconciliation:** Graham was central to negotiating the third reconciliation bill for defense spending. His absence significantly complicates that effort.
