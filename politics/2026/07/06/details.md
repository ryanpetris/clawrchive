**What happened:** President Trump delivered the keynote address at the "Salute to America 250" event on the National Mall Saturday evening, marking the 250th anniversary of U.S. independence. Severe weather forced a two-hour evacuation of the Mall before his speech. The address blended patriotic themes with campaign-style attacks on political opponents.

## 3. Congress: Senate

### SAVE America Act — Status

**What happened:** The Senate has twice rejected the SAVE America Act — once in April and again in June. Both times it fell well short of the 60 votes needed for passage, with four Republicans joining all Democrats in opposition. The House has passed the bill three times.

**Why it matters:** Senate Majority Leader John Thune (R-SD) has said publicly and privately that passing the SAVE Act through reconciliation is unworkable. He has little interest in a reconciliation bill, according to Punchbowl News.

**What to watch:** Whether Thune and Senate GOP leadership can resist Trump's pressure to nuke the filibuster or find a compromise path.

## 4. Congress: House Of Representatives

### Floor Crisis — Anna Paulina Luna Blockade

**What happened:** Rep. Anna Paulina Luna (R-FL) and 13 GOP hardliners took down a procedural rule vote that would have attached the SAVE Act to the $1.15 trillion NDAA. Luna's coalition has pledged to vote against any procedural rule until the Senate passes the SAVE Act. This forced Speaker Johnson to cancel votes and send members home early for the July 4 recess — the second straight week of paralysis.

**Why it matters:** Luna's blockade prevents the House from processing the NDAA, FY2027 spending bills, and any other legislation requiring a rule. If the blockade continues, the House may enter NDAA conference negotiations without its own bill, putting it at a significant disadvantage.

**What to watch:** Whether Johnson can negotiate a deal with Luna and the hardliners to restore floor access.

**Sources:** [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/save-act-mike-johnson-housing-bill-f9af93810930ad282ebb96934cbe1955), [Punchbowl News](https://punchbowl.news/article/house/recess-stage), [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/4636639/mike-johson-house-save-america-act-reconciliation-bill/)

### Johnson's Reconciliation Plan — Multiple Tripwires

**What happened:** Johnson announced on Fox News Sunday that he will attach SAVE Act provisions to a budget reconciliation bill, arguing this is the path to bypass the Senate filibuster. The plan has at least three major obstacles:

1. **Senate parliamentarian:** The parliamentarian ruled earlier this year that SAVE Act provisions violate the "Byrd rule" against extraneous legislation in reconciliation. Johnson plans a scaled-back version with $4 billion in state incentives for voter ID and citizenship verification, but this may also be rejected.
2. **Senate leadership resistance:** Thune opposes the reconciliation approach and has said so publicly and to donors.
3. **Hardliner demands:** Hardline Republicans want tens of billions in spending cuts alongside Pentagon funding increases — a non-starter for moderates and Democrats.

**Why it matters:** With only 18 real legislative days before the midterms, the window for any legislative accomplishment is closing fast.

**Sources:** [Punchbowl News](https://punchbowl.news/article/house/recess-stage), [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/4636639/mike-johson-house-save-america-act-reconciliation-bill/)

### Johnson Considers Birthright Citizenship Legislation

**What happened:** In the same July 5 Fox News interview, Johnson said Congress should take up legislation to curtail birthright citizenship in the wake of the Supreme Court striking down Trump's executive order on the issue. Justice Kavanaugh's concurring opinion suggested Congress could establish exceptions for children of people in the country illegally.

**Why it matters:** Amending the 14th Amendment requires a supermajority that doesn't exist. Legislation (as opposed to a constitutional amendment) would face immediate legal challenges and almost certain defeat in the Senate.

**Source:** [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/07/05/mike-johnson-birthright-citizenship-bill/90814782007/)

### Democrats Plan Investigations If They Retake House

**What happened:** House Democrats are laying out investigation priorities targeting Trump and his family if they win control of the House in November.

**Why it matters:** Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries (D-NY) is in line to become Speaker if Democrats flip the chamber. A Democratic majority would likely launch aggressive oversight investigations into the Trump administration.

**Source:** [Washington Examiner](https://washingtonexaminer.com/news/campaigns/congressional/4634789/democrats-investigation-priorities-retake-house)

### Highway Bill — August Recess Target

## 7. Federal Agencies: News And Reported Developments

### ICE Veterans Enforcement — Confirmed

**What is being reported:** ICE appears to be showing little consideration of noncitizens' U.S. military service when pursuing enforcement actions. Prior ICE guidance treating military service as a "significant mitigating factor" is no longer being followed.

**Confirmation status:** Confirmed by multiple sources including veterans' advocates and DHS statement. DHS told Houston Public Media: "U.S. military service alone does not automatically grant lawful immigration status, or exempt aliens from the consequences of violating U.S. immigration laws."

**Why it matters:** Veterans and active service members who are noncitizens are being caught in the enforcement net. These individuals can obtain citizenship after one year of service, but must apply. Spouses of service members may also be affected.

**Source:** [Houston Public Media](https://www.houstonpublicmedia.org/articles/news/politics/immigration/2026/07/06/556163/ice-trump-immigration-military-service-veteran-noncitizen/)

### DHS Funding Standoff — Partially Confirmed

**Why it matters:** DHS funding is essential for border security, immigration enforcement, and disaster response.

**Source:** [Pithy Productions](https://pithyproductions.com/article/dhs-shutdown-drama-house-gop-vs-senate-on-immigration-funding-explained)
