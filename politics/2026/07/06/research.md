# Research Notes - July 6, 2026

## Deeper Analysis

### The Luna Blockade: Tactical Analysis
Rep. Anna Paulina Luna (R-FL) and her 13-member coalition have effectively shut down the House floor by voting against the "previous question" (the procedural rule that governs debate and amendment). This is an unusually aggressive tactic — typically, even rebellious members vote for the rule to keep the chamber functioning. The coalition's demand that the Senate pass the SAVE Act before they'll allow floor business creates a catch-22: the Senate can't pass it, so the blockade continues indefinitely.

**What makes this different from the McCarthy/McHenry era:** Under McCarthy, hardliners used the motion to vacate the chair as leverage. Under Johnson, they're using the procedural rule vote — a lower-stakes but equally effective tool that doesn't require as many members to coordinate.

### Johnson's Reconciliation Gambit — Why It's Unlikely To Work
1. **Byrd rule issues:** The Senate parliamentarian already ruled that SAVE Act provisions are "extraneous" under the Byrd rule. Johnson's scaled-back plan ($4 billion in state incentives) may also be ruled extraneous because the policy change (voter ID requirements) doesn't primarily affect the budget.
2. **Thune opposition:** Senate Majority Leader Thune opposes the reconciliation approach both publicly and privately. Without Thune's support, Johnson's reconciliation bill dies in the Senate.
3. **Time constraints:** Reconciliation requires both chambers to pass identical budget resolutions first — a process that takes weeks. With 18 real legislative days, there's simply not enough time.

### The "Zombie Congress" Phenomenon
The Wall Street Journal reported that some GOP lawmakers are privately calling this the "zombie Congress" — a majority that's technically alive but unable to function. Key characteristics:
- Members have little incentive to come to work or toe the party line
- Leadership cannot punish defectors because the majority is too thin
- Lame-duck members (retiring or defeated) have no reason to cooperate
- The calendar is working against leadership
