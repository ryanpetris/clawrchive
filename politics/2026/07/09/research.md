## Air Force One Security Details

- The new Qatari-gifted Boeing 747-800 cost $400 million to retrofit.
- Images show it lacks some missile detection and countermeasure systems present on the older VC-25As.
- The older jets were built near the end of the Cold War, hardened against nuclear blast effects, and include anti-missile countermeasures, an onboard operating room, and air-to-air refueling capability.
- Permanent replacement Boeing jets are delayed until 2028.
- The bridge aircraft was intentionally delivered without "several highly complex engineering modifications."

## SAVE Act

- Would require documents like birth certificate or passport to register to vote.
- Brennan Center estimates 21 million+ citizens lack ready access to such documents.
- Previous version failed before a Senate vote.
- If enacted, would be first federal law in U.S. history restricting access to voting.
