## 2. White House And President

### Trump Declares Iran Ceasefire "Over" at NATO Summit

**What happened:** President Trump, at the NATO summit in Ankara, Turkey, declared the U.S.-Iran ceasefire agreement "over" after Iran attacked three tankers in the Strait of Hormuz. The U.S. launched new retaliatory strikes hitting targets including Kharg Island, Qeshm Island, and the port cities of Sirik and Bandar Abbas. Trump revoked a license allowing Iran to sell oil. He called Iranian leaders "scum" and "sick people," saying "I don't want to deal with them."

**Why it matters:** The collapse of the ceasefire means a return to open hostilities between the U.S. and Iran. The Strait of Hormuz, through which one-fifth of the world's oil passes, remains contested. Oil prices are spiking globally. NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte defended the strikes as "absolutely necessary."

**What to watch next:** Whether further strikes will follow, how Iran's "level three" leadership responds, and whether oil price increases affect domestic politics ahead of the midterms.

**Source:** [The Independent](https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/americas/us-politics/trump-iran-assassination-threat-b3011422.html), [The Independent - MTG takedown](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/us-politics/marjorie-taylor-greene-trump-iran-b3011116.html), [NBC News](https://nbcnews.com/world/iran/live-blog/live-updates-iran-attacks-gulf-us-strikes-tehran-ships-hormuz-oil-rcna353439)

### Trump Flies Home on Older Air Force One

**What happened:** President Trump flew partway home from Turkey on an older Boeing VC-25A (the "baby blue" Air Force One) instead of the new Qatari-gifted jet. The new jet was sent ahead to RAF Mildenhall in the U.K. Trump claimed it was "for old time's sake" and to show troops the aircraft. The older plane's transponder was turned off after takeoff, a security measure typically used in war zones. Reporters were asked to keep window blinds closed during the flight from Turkey to the U.K.

**Why it matters:** The swap raises questions about whether the new $400 million retrofitted jet lacks adequate countermeasure systems for flying near Iran, which shares a border with Turkey and has missiles with sufficient range to reach Ankara. The Air Force admitted "several highly complex engineering modifications" were excluded from the bridge aircraft.

**What to watch next:** Whether the White House provides more detail on security concerns, and whether the Qatari-gifted jet is used only for domestic travel going forward.

**Source:** [NPR/AP](https://npr.org/2026/07/09/nx-s1-5887064/trump-air-force-one), [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/5959432-president-returns-from-turkey)

### White House Denies Security Concerns Behind Air Force One Swap

**What happened:** White House spokesman Steven Cheung said the new Qatari-gifted jet "has been fitted with high-level security protocols," and said the administration uses "every tool at our disposal — including distraction and misdirection — to address those threats."

**Why it matters:** The statement implicitly acknowledges the administration is using security measures to address threats from Iran, while the Air Force declined to answer questions about missing countermeasure systems.

**Source:** [NPR/AP](https://npr.org/2026/07/09/nx-s1-5887064/trump-air-force-one)

### Trump Claims He Is Iran's "No. 1 Target" for Assassination

**What happened:** During the NATO summit press conference, Trump said: "I may be gone too, because I'm their No. 1 target." He referenced an Iranian-led assassination plot for which Asif Merchant was convicted in federal court in March. Trump has been the target of three assassination attempts since 2024.

**Why it matters:** The president's open discussion of assassination threats is unusual. It came as he described Iran's current leadership as "level three" — after the U.S. and Israel killed previous leadership tiers in the February 28 Operation Epic Fury strikes.

**Source:** [The Independent](https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/americas/us-politics/trump-iran-assassination-threat-b3011422.html)

### Trump to Ask Supreme Court to Rehear Birthright Citizenship Case

**What happened:** Trump announced on Truth Social on July 8 that he will ask the Supreme Court to rehear Trump v. Barbara, the birthright citizenship case. The Court ruled 6-3 on June 30 to strike down his executive order (EO 14160), with Chief Justice Roberts writing that "a child born on American soil and subject to American law was made an American citizen."

**Why it matters:** The Supreme Court rarely grants rehearing petitions. The last time it did so was in 1956 in Reid v. Covert, which resulted in the only instance of the Court reversing itself. Parties have 25 days to file a petition after a decision. Trump's claim of birth tourism rings has been supported by some federal prosecutions but the administration's estimates remain disputed.

**What to watch next:** Whether the administration files the petition within the deadline and how the Court responds.

**Source:** [The Epoch Times](https://www.theepochtimes.com/us/trump-to-ask-supreme-court-to-rehear-birthright-citizenship-case-6059212)

## 3. Congress: Senate

### Graham Platner Suspends Senate Campaign (Maine)

**What happened:** Democratic Senate candidate Graham Platner suspended his campaign on July 8 after a woman accused him of rape. Platner denies the allegation, calling it "categorically false." The Maine Democratic Party voted to hold a convention to select a replacement. The deadline to select a new nominee is July 27.

**Why it matters:** This is a critical Senate race — Sen. Susan Collins (R-ME) is one of the most vulnerable Republican incumbents. Platner's campaign had galvanized progressive volunteers and defeated establishment-backed Gov. Janet Mills in the primary. The collapse creates deep uncertainty about Democratic chances in a must-win seat.

**Potential replacements:** Nirav Shah (former Maine CDC director), Troy Jackson (former state Senate president, filed FEC exploratory committee paperwork), Jordan Wood (former congressional staffer), Shenna Bellows (Maine secretary of state), Dan Kleban (Maine Beer Co. founder), Hannah Pingree (Democratic gubernatorial nominee).

**Source:** [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/maine-senate-graham-platner-48d472ac4a043792032f3e3f5a33ef1b), [CNN](https://cnn.com/2026/07/08/politics/graham-platner-replacements-maine-senate), [Politico](https://politico.com/news/2026/07/08/graham-platner-potential-replacements-maine-00991341)

### Mitch McConnell Remains Hospitalized, Beshear Requests Update

**What happened:** Sen. Mitch McConnell (R-KY) has been hospitalized since June 14 with minimal public updates. Kentucky Gov. Andy Beshear (D) formally wrote to McConnell's office requesting a public update on his condition. McConnell's wife released a statement, and Senate Republican leadership maintains he will return to work. Former McConnell adviser Scott Jennings said he spoke with McConnell and that he remains engaged.

**Why it matters:** McConnell is not seeking reelection, but his health and potential vacancy raise questions about who would appoint his successor. Kentucky law gives the governor appointment power, and Beshear is a Democrat. If McConnell cannot serve, Beshear would appoint a replacement — potentially flipping a Senate seat to Democratic control.

**What to watch next:** Whether McConnell's office provides a substantive update and whether the situation escalates into a political crisis over succession.

**Source:** [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/mcconnell-health-beshear-kentucky-hospital-letter-condition-efbfc2179c8d43f6b43d9024b8d73506), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/gov-beshear-urges-mcconnell-transparent-give-update-health-rcna353477), [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jul/08/republicans-mitch-mcconnell-health)

## 4. Congress: House of Representatives

### House Passes New Version of SAVE Act

**What happened:** The House passed a new version of the SAVE Act (S. 1383), which would require American citizens to show documents like a birth certificate or passport to register to vote. The Brennan Center for Justice estimates over 21 million U.S. citizens of voting age lack ready access to these documents.

**Why it matters:** If enacted, it would be the first federal law in U.S. history restricting access to voting. The previous version failed before a Senate vote due to public opposition. The bill now moves to the Senate.

**What to watch next:** Whether the Senate takes up the bill and whether it can overcome a filibuster.

**Source:** [Brennan Center for Justice](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/analysis-opinion/house-passes-new-version-save-act-brennan-center-responds)

### Anti-Incumbent Sentiment Sweeps House Races
