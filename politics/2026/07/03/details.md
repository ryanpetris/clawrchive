### Housing Bill — Pocket Veto Legal Question
- **Legal status**: Under the Constitution, the president has 10 days (excluding Sundays) to sign or return a bill. If Congress is adjourned when that 10-day period ends, the unsigned bill dies via pocket veto. There are legal questions about whether this applies during a congressional recess (temporary) or only at end-of-session adjournment.
- **What to watch**: The bill was sent to Trump on June 29. The 10-day clock (excluding Sundays) would expire around July 10. If Congress is still in recess and the bill hasn't been signed, the legal question of whether it becomes law or dies is unresolved and could head to court.
- **Source**: [Mediaite](https://mediaite.com/media/tv/house-republican-urges-trump-to-sign-housing-bill-agrees-its-a-mistake-not-to)
