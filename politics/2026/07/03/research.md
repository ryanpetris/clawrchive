## 2. Housing Bill / SAVE America Act Stalemate

### Current Status
- Bipartisan housing bill (largest in 36 years) sent to Trump's desk by Speaker Johnson on June 29
- Trump canceled signing ceremony, called the bill "a yawn" on June 29
- Rep. Mike Lawler (R-NY), who helped write the bill, said July 2 it's a "mistake" for Trump not to sign
- Congress leaves for 10-day recess starting July 3
- **Pocket veto risk**: Under the Constitution, the president has 10 days excluding Sundays to sign or return a bill. If Congress is adjourned when that 10-day period ends, the unsigned bill dies via pocket veto. Legal questions remain about whether a recess (vs. end-of-session adjournment) triggers this.
- If Trump does nothing and Congress is in recess, the bill would die around July 10

### SAVE America Act
- Sen. Thom Tillis (R-NC) declared the SAVE Act "dead" — there is not enough time to change election laws before the midterms
- Even if it got 60 votes (unlikely), implementation takes 6+ months
- GOP holdouts (14 Republicans) blocked Johnson's plan to merge SAVE Act with the NDAA, triggering early House recess
- House not expected back until ~July 13
- Republicans fear Trump will use the SAVE Act to blame them if they lose in November
