## Extra Context and Deeper Analysis

- The strikes targeted more than 80 sites including "more than 60 IRGC small boats" — indicating the U.S. is directly targeting Iran's ability to harass shipping in the Strait of Hormuz

- Kuwaiti air defenses responded to hostile missile and drone threats

### DOJ Election Letters — Legal Analysis
- The DOJ has lost 11 lawsuits seeking unredacted voter rolls — a remarkable losing streak
- The Sixth Circuit became the first appellate court to affirm a loss, siding with Michigan
- The letters to all 50 states appear to be an attempt to achieve through intimidation what DOJ has been unable to win in court
- The legal basis for the DOJ's position is contested: federal law requires states to maintain clean voter rolls, but states argue they already comply
- The Brennan Center's 2017 study finding 0.0001% noncitizen voting in 2016 remains the most comprehensive data on the issue
- ABC News reported that one election official found the letters "threatening"

### Speaker Johnson's Court Elimination Threat
- Johnson specifically referenced Congress' authority under Article III, which gives Congress the power to "ordain and establish" lower federal courts
- Historical precedent: Congress abolished the Commerce Court in 1913 and restructured the Court of Claims and Court of Customs and Patent Appeals in 1982
- However, eliminating an entire district court to punish it for rulings against the administration would be unprecedented in modern times
- Sen. Hawley's concern about "massive backlogs" is well-founded — district courts handle the vast majority of federal cases
- Rep. Dave Joyce (R-OH), who chairs the appropriations subcommittee funding the courts, is seen as a key obstacle — he's a former prosecutor and moderate
- Johnson's "middle path" — the Issa bill barring nationwide injunctions — is less dramatic than eliminating courts but still a significant constraint on judicial power

### Drug Pricing Bill — Industry Fight
- The bill would expand Medicare negotiation from 20 to 75 drugs annually
- CBO scores $340 billion in savings over a decade
- PhRMA has committed $200M+ in lobbying spending
- The international reference pricing mechanism (pegging U.S. prices to Canada, Germany, France, UK, Japan averages) is the most controversial provision
- XBI biotech ETF declined 8.3% since introduction
- Venture investment in early-stage drug development dropped 14% in Q2 2026 vs Q2 2025
- HHS Secretary RFK Jr. has publicly backed the framework but signaled the international reference pricing may be revised
- The bill creates unusual alliances: insurers (AHIP) support it; drug makers oppose; hospitals are divided
