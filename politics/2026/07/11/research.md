## EAC Firings — Background

- The Election Assistance Commission was created by the Help America Vote Act of 2002
- It has four commissioners (two Democrats, two Republicans by statute)
- Recent Supreme Court rulings apparently expanded presidential removal power over independent agencies
- Without commissioners: cannot distribute HAVA funds, cannot certify voting systems, cannot issue guidance
- Potential lawsuits would likely argue statutory restrictions on commissioner removal
- This may be a test case for the new SCOTUS executive power framework
