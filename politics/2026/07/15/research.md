## 2. SENATE NDAA BLOCKED (50-46)

### The Vote
Senate failed to invoke cloture on S. 4784 (FY2027 NDAA). Vote: 50-46, far short of 60-vote threshold. Party-line vote. Four senators absent: Fetterman, Justice, McConnell, Padilla. Thune switched to "no" procedurally to allow reconsideration.

### Why Democrats Blocked
- **Iran War**: Schumer called NDAA a "permission slip" for Trump's war. "Donald Trump does not get to drag the American people deeper into a war he cannot explain and does not know how to end."
- **Budget**: Pentagon spending requested at $1.5 trillion, up from ~$900 billion last year. No bipartisan topline deal.
- **Israel provisions**: Bill would deepen US-Israel military/intelligence integration, including shared weapons production and "data fusion" targeting systems. Van Hollen, Sanders, Warren, Markey, Merkley, Welch circulated anti-NDAA letter.
- Duckworth demanded amendment to prohibit funds for Iran war.

### What's in the Bill
- $1.15 trillion defense budget authorization
- Pay raise for troops
- Would block Secretary Hegseth's travel funds unless he provides reports on Iran school strike
- $67 billion supplemental for Operation Epic Fury (Iran war costs)

### Path Forward
Thune preserved right to bring bill back. House may take up its own version next week. Hegseth meeting with House GOP on reconciliation $ — WH wants $350B, House GOP signaling ~$87B.

### Broader Context
Congress has voted 10+ times on war powers resolutions to halt hostilities. None succeeded. Most Republicans back Trump.
