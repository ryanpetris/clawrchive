## 2. White House and President

### Graham's Death — Trump Response

- **What happened**: Trump posted on Truth Social early Sunday: "Senator Lindsey Graham, one of the greatest people and Senators I have ever known, is dead!" He called Graham "a true American Patriot" and said he would be "greatly missed."
- **Why it matters**: Graham was Trump's closest Senate ally on foreign policy, a frequent golf partner, and a key player on budget and judicial strategy.
- **What to watch**: Whether Trump attends memorial services; whether he weighs in on Graham's successor.

### US Strikes on Iran — Third Round This Week

- **What happened**: Trump ordered CENTCOM to strike ~140 Iranian targets after the IRGC attacked the MV GFS Galaxy, a Cyprus-flagged container ship in the Strait of Hormuz. One crew member missing, ship disabled. Iran retaliated by attacking US bases in Jordan, Kuwait, Qatar, and Oman, and struck a second vessel.
- **Why it matters**: The June 17 ceasefire is in danger of collapse. Trump declared it "over" three days ago. Iran's closure of the Strait threatens ~20% of global oil supply.
- **Source**: [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/iran-usa-united-arab-emirates-attack-0764d17c09370a8c5cf1e8197a8878ab), [ABC News Australia](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-07-12/iran-revolutionary-guard-closes-strait-hormuz-centcom-us-strikes/106906666)

### Housing Bill Becomes Law Without Signature

- **What happened**: Trump allowed the 10-day constitutional window to expire without signing the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, which passed Congress with veto-proof majorities (358-32 House, 85-5 Senate). The bill became law at 12:01 a.m. Saturday.
- **Why it matters**: Trump framed his refusal to sign as protest over the SAVE America Act (voter ID/citizenship proof bill). Democrats are using the episode to argue Trump is indifferent to housing costs. Home prices hit record highs last month per NAR.
- **Source**: [LA Times Now](https://latimesnow.com/2026/07/12/trump-wont-sign-housing-bill-in-save-america-act-protest/)

### DOJ Subpoenas NYT Journalists

- **What happened**: The Justice Department, through US Attorney Jay Clayton, subpoenaed four NYT journalists (Julian E. Barnes, Eric Lipton, Tyler Pager, Eric Schmitt) to testify before a federal grand jury regarding their reporting on security concerns about the new Qatari-gifted Air Force One. Federal agents delivered some subpoenas to reporters' homes.
- **Why it matters**: The subpoenas represent an extraordinary escalation of Trump's campaign against press freedom. The Times reported that the FBI asked the paper to hold the story and disclose sources before publication, which the Times refused.
- **What to watch**: Clayton's ODNI confirmation hearing Wednesday before the Senate Intelligence Committee. The Reporters Committee called on the committee to hold Clayton accountable.
- **Source**: [Reuters via Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/global-trends/trumps-qatari-air-force-one-report-triggers-grand-jury-summons-for-new-york-times-journalists/articleshow/132339773.cms)

## 3. Congress: Senate

### Lindsey Graham (R-SC) — Death

- **What happened**: Graham died at 71 after a "brief and sudden illness." He was the Senate Budget Committee chairman and was set to chair the Judiciary Committee in January 2027.
- **Why it matters**: Graham's death reduces the GOP Senate majority from 53-47 to 52-47 until a replacement is appointed. His Budget chairmanship — which controls the reconciliation process — is now vacant. The Judiciary chairmanship was set to shift from Sen. Chuck Grassley (R-IA) to Graham in 2027.
- **Succession**: SC Gov. Henry McMaster (R) will appoint a temporary replacement. SC law requires a special primary within weeks. Graham had won 57% in the GOP primary against Democrat Annie Andrews.
- **Source**: [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/lindsey-graham-dies-south-carolina-bfa556e170f2df22ce9ffc7165da3dfa)

### Russia Sanctions Bill — Status Uncertain After Graham's Death

- **What happened**: On Friday, Graham (R-SC), Blumenthal (D-CT), Shaheen (D-NH), and Wicker (R-MS) announced a deal with the White House on the Sanctioning Russia Act, which would impose 500% tariffs on countries purchasing Russian oil, gas, and uranium.
- **Why it matters**: Graham was the bill's primary GOP sponsor and driving force. His death creates uncertainty about whether the bill will advance. The bill had veto-proof support in the Senate but was frozen by Majority Leader Thune (R-SD) to give Trump negotiating space.
- **What to watch**: Whether Blumenthal and Shaheen can find a new GOP lead sponsor. Whether Thune now schedules floor time.
- **Source**: [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/5963080-senators-trump-russia-sanctions-bill/)

### Senate Schedule — Week Ahead

- Senate not in session on Sundays (pro forma sessions possible)
- SAVE America Act status: stalled in Senate; lacks 60 votes to overcome filibuster

- **Court**: US District Court for DC (Judge Timothy Kelly, Trump appointee)
