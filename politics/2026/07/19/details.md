## 2. White House And President

### Trump's Primetime Speech Fallout (July 16)

**What happened:** Trump delivered a primetime address on election integrity, releasing declassified documents on a new White House election-integrity page covering voting-system vulnerabilities, China's alleged voter data acquisition, a Michigan voter-registration investigation, and noncitizens on state voter rolls. He accused a "shadow government" of hiding intelligence, claimed China stole 220M voter records, and alleged ~278K non-citizens are registered to vote.

**Why it matters:** The speech was Trump's most aggressive push yet to frame the 2026 midterm elections as potentially illegitimate, laying groundwork for disputing unfavorable results. Major networks (ABC, NBC, CNN) aired the speech on streaming but not broadcast; CBS and MSNBC cut away before he finished.

**Leaked internal details (Politico, July 18):** Acting DNI Bill Pulte pushed in the days before the speech to have Trump name specific intelligence officials accused of concealing election interference. Senior White House officials including chief of staff Susie Wiles warned that naming analysts could put lives at risk. "Pulte got really scared by the whole thing and pulled back," one official said. Aides told Trump "the way to be taken seriously is not to be crazy." One official said, "There was going to be a lot of crazier s--t said."

**Fact-check status:** Republican election lawyer Ben Ginsberg told CNN there is still no evidence of "any election being incorrect." The WSJ editorial board wrote that Trump's message "may have actually backfired for voters" by discouraging GOP turnout if the bill fails.

**What to watch:** Whether Trump continues pushing this issue or moves on. One official told Politico: "I don't know if he'll keep pushing this or if this was enough to get it out of his system."

### Iran War Escalation — US Troops Killed in Jordan

**What happened:** Iranian ballistic missile and drone attacks on a US base in Jordan killed two American service members, left one missing, and injured four others (since discharged). This is the first US combat deaths since the latest escalation. Trump ordered CENTCOM to "open the gates of hell" and authorized a "much more extensive and larger" round of airstrikes targeting Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), including coastal surveillance, air defense, maritime, and missile/drone storage sites.

**Interim deal collapse:** Iran's negotiator announced Tehran is suspending commitments to the interim peace deal signed about a month ago. Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei warned of "unforgettable lessons" if the US keeps attacking.

**Regional spillover:** Iran has expanded retaliatory strikes to US-allied Gulf states. Israel warned Iran launched missiles toward the Jordanian port of Aqaba. The UAE — which has not yet been targeted — called for restraint after Iran's Fars news agency threatened evacuation of Dubai and Abu Dhabi airports. The US reimposed a naval blockade on Iranian ports. Since the war began in February, 16 US service members have been killed and over 430 wounded.

**Trump's defense:** Trump told the New York Post the deaths were "a shame" but the mission was necessary to prevent Iran from getting a nuclear weapon. He compared casualties to Vietnam and Afghanistan.

**Democratic reactions:** House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries called for de-escalation. Sen. Chris Van Hollen (D-MD), Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA), and Rep. Melanie Stansbury (D-NM) all called to "end the war."

**What to watch:** Whether the expansion of strikes draws Israel back into direct combat. Whether Iran targets the UAE. Iranian casualties: at least 50 killed, 500+ wounded per Iranian authorities.

**Source:** [AP via ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/us-strikes-irans-revolutionary-guard-attack-killed-troops-134886540), [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/first-us-soldiers-killed-in-jordan-trump-vows-to-shift-action), [NewsNation/Meaww](https://news.meaww.com/trump-orders-centcom-to-open-the-gates-of-hell-after-us-troops-killed-launches-strikes-on-iran)

## 3. Congress: Senate

### SAVE America Act — Murkowski Definitively Opposes

**What happened:** Sen. Lisa Murkowski (R-AK) published a Wall Street Journal op-ed stating she remains opposed to the SAVE America Act. She cited serious negative effects on Alaska (only six election offices statewide), the elimination of mail-in voting on which Alaskans rely, and the impossibility of implementing the bill before the November midterms.

**Why it matters:** Murkowski's opposition is significant because the bill already lacked the 60 votes needed to overcome a filibuster. Her op-ed confirms the SAVE Act remains dead in the Senate for the foreseeable future. Sen. Thom Tillis (R-NC) has also voiced concerns about implementation timelines.

**Trump's response:** Trump's speech Thursday reportedly "barely moved the needle" among Republican senators (NYT).

**Source:** [Democracy Docket](https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/murkowski-slams-door-shut-on-supporting-save-america-act/), [WSJ op-ed](https://www.wsj.com/opinion/why-im-voting-against-the-save-america-act-139faa0b)

### ACIA Corporate Tax Bill

**What happened:** The American Competitiveness and Investment Act (ACIA) — proposing a permanent corporate rate reduction from 21% to 18%, 100% bonus depreciation, and revised GILTI framework — faces an informal August 1 target for Senate passage. The CBO estimates the ten-year cost at $1.4T ($1.22T net after dynamic scoring). Three compromise frameworks are circulating: the Sunset Option (temporary through 2031), the Offset Package (carried interest elimination), and the Phased Approach (19.5% immediate, 18% contingent on deficit benchmarks).

**Key players:** Senate Finance Committee Chair Mike Crapo (R-ID) is shepherding the bill. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent met with wavering Republicans for the third time this week. At least 4 GOP senators have reservations — Murkowski (scaled-back), Collins (revenue offsets), plus two freshman fiscal conservatives (debt-ceiling trigger). Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer (D-NY) has vowed unified Democratic opposition.

**Why it matters:** TSMC has indicated its planned $15B third Arizona facility is under review pending the policy environment. Nucor has made similar statements about a Louisiana plate mill expansion.

**Source:** [USA Business Times](https://usabusinesstimes.com/congresss-corporate-tax-showdown-is-coming-to-a-head/)

### CLARITY Act (Crypto Market Structure)

**What happened:** The CLARITY Act remains stalled. Trump's meeting with Senate Republicans produced no revised text. Polymarket passage odds dropped to 32%. Sen. Ruben Gallego (D-AZ) said the Republican ethics proposal was "very weak" and would not attract Democratic votes. Additionally, Senate Judiciary Committee Chair Chuck Grassley (R-IA) and ranking member Dick Durbin (D-IL) sent a letter demanding removal of developer exemptions from the crypto bill, arguing they would create a "significant enforcement gap" for decentralized platforms.

**What to watch:** Updated text expected next week, but the bill faces a 60-vote threshold and the August recess deadline is approaching fast.

**Source:** [Cryptify Now](https://cryptifynow.info/trump-fails-to-break-clarity-act-deadlock-as-new-text-slips-again/), [BitRss](https://bitrss.com/us-senate-judiciary-demand-removal-of-developer-exemptions-from-crypto-bill-174378), [Crypto Times](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/07/19/senator-lummis-pushes-clarity-act-on-genius-acts-first-anniversary/)

### DHS — Election Security Threats to States

**What happened:** DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin threatened to withhold federal election security grants, levy fines, and pursue criminal charges against state election officials who refuse to turn over voter rolls. He claimed DHS found "as many as" 190,832 possible non-citizens registered in California and three other Democratic-led states. More than a dozen courts have ruled against the DOJ's demand for state voter rolls.

**Why it matters:** Election experts say the 278K non-citizen figure is likely a significant overcount and that non-citizen voting is extremely rare. Multiple courts have reaffirmed that states — not the federal government — have primary authority over elections.

## 4. Congress: House Of Representatives

### Continuing Resolution — Stopgap Funding Bill

**What happened:** Speaker Mike Johnson (R-LA) and House leadership on Friday released the "Continuing Appropriations Act, 2027" — a clean CR that would fund the government at current levels from Oct. 1 through Dec. 4 (after the Nov. 3 midterms). House GOP leaders hope to bring it to the floor this week.

**Current status:** All 12 appropriations bills approved by the House Appropriations Committee; only 3 passed by the full House. The Senate has no consensus on top-line spending numbers. Polymarket shutdown odds dropped from 66% to 55% after the clean CR was announced.

**Complication:** Trump has repeatedly said he won't sign any legislation until Congress passes the SAVE Act. Johnson is trying to keep the CR clean while moving SAVE Act provisions through reconciliation.

**What to watch:** House leaves Thursday for August recess. The CR needs to pass before then, or risk a shutdown fight in September.

**Source:** [News Block](https://news-block.org/house-releases-short-term-funding-bill-as-congress-faces-risk-of-oct-1-shutdown), [The Advocate](https://www.theadvocate.com/baton_rouge/news/politics/national_politics/congress-fears-another-federal-government-shutdown/article_ce235443-09c2-5d39-a026-c3edb5086f60.html)

### Reconciliation 3.0

**What happened:** House Budget Committee Chair Jodey Arrington (R-TX) released a $95B reconciliation framework: $60B for defense, $13B for intelligence, $12B for farmers, and $10B for grants to incentivize state voter ID laws. Johnson said it will get a vote this week. This is downsized 83% from the Pentagon's $350B request.

**Omissions:** The Planned Parenthood funding ban that expired July 5 was left out, angering anti-abortion groups. Students for Life Action gave every member of Congress an "F" for failing to extend the ban. Sen. Josh Hawley (R-MO) held a rally calling for the ban to be restored.

**What to watch:** Senate skeptics including McConnell and Collins have cast doubt on Reconciliation 3.0's chances. The bill's path through the Senate is uncertain.

**Source:** [The Advocate](https://www.theadvocate.com/baton_rouge/news/politics/national_politics/congress-fears-another-federal-government-shutdown/article_ce235443-09c2-5d39-a026-c3edb5086f60.html), [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5975417-anti-abortion-activists-planned-parenthood-funding-reconciliation/)

### Bipartisan AI Weapons Bill

**What happened:** Reps. Tom Barrett (R-MI), Don Beyer (D-VA), and Sara Jacobs (D-CA) introduced the Human Authority over Autonomous Weapons Act, requiring human oversight of any intentionally lethal use of AI-enabled weapon systems. Military commanders would need to verify AI-generated targets using non-AI sources for five years. Missile defense systems are exempted.

**Why it matters:** As AI-enabled weapons become more prevalent, this bill would codify human oversight requirements into federal law rather than leaving them as Pentagon policy.

**Source:** [Breitbart](https://www.breitbart.com/tech/2026/07/18/bipartisan-lawmakers-introduce-bill-requiring-human-control-over-ai-weapons/)
