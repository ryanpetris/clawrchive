### Federal Workforce Restructuring — Schedule Policy/Career

**What's actually different from 2020 Schedule F:**
- Renamed from "Schedule F" to "Schedule Policy/Career"
- 2020 version (EO 13957) was signed Oct 21, 2020 — never implemented before Biden's inauguration
- 2025 version (EO 14171, Jan 20, 2025) restored accountability provisions but didn't fully implement
- The June 3, 2026 EO is the actual implementation order
- 8,000 positions vs original 50,000 estimate — significant scale-down
- OPM Director Scott Kupor: "no political litmus tests" — but critics note at-will employment inherently enables political firings

**Legal vulnerability:** The 2024 Biden administration issued regulations intended to block Schedule F by reinforcing civil service protections. The Trump EO presumably overrides or attempts to supersede those regulations, which invites APA challenges.

**Civil service history:** The Pendleton Act of 1883 established the merit-based, nonpartisan federal civil service, replacing the spoils system. This is the foundation opponents argue Schedule Policy/Career undermines.

### FISA Politics — Cross-Issue Dynamics

The FISA vote is a fascinating example of cross-issue coalition dynamics:

**Opposition reasons:**
- Democrats: Pulte as DNI (trust issue); also can't support without addressing privacy concerns
- Six GOP senators: policy objections — worry about warrantless incidental collection of Americans' data
- One Dem (Fetterman): supported (bucked his party)

**The horseshoe effect:** FISA opposition is one of the few issues where privacy-focused Democrats and conservative/libertarian-leaning Republicans align against the intelligence establishment.

**The Pulte factor:** Bill Pulte was confirmed earlier this year as FHFA director. Trump tapped him as acting DNI, which requires Senate confirmation for the permanent role. The pick reportedly surprised Senate Republicans and angered Democrats who view it as a loyalty-based appointment. Sen. Warner had worked closely with Cotton on a bipartisan FISA reform bill; Pulte's appointment undid that cooperation.
