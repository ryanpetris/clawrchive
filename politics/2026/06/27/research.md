## SAVE Act / House GOP Dynamics

**The procedural mechanism:**
- Luna's bloc is blocking the "rule" (the resolution that sets terms for floor debate). Without a rule, most legislation cannot reach the floor in the House.
- This is one of the most disruptive procedural tactics available to a small minority within the majority party.
- The narrow 218-212 margin means Johnson can lose only 2 GOP votes on party-line votes.

**What's being blocked:**
- Government funding bills (DHS has been unfunded since Feb 14 in one version of the funding fight)
- FISA Section 702 renewal (surveillance program lapsed)
- NDAA work
- Reconciliation package

**The SAVE America Act specifics:**
- Mandates photo ID for federal elections
- Requires proof of citizenship for voter registration
- Compels states to share voter rolls with federal government
- Trump's version adds: mail-in voting restrictions, ban on men in women's sports, ban on child sex change procedures
- House passed its version in February; Senate has failed five times since March

## Housing Bill

**Formal title:** 21st Century Road to Housing Act

**Key provisions (compiled from multiple sources):**
- 47+ housing supply provisions
- Streamlines environmental reviews for construction
- Requires HUD guidance on zoning/land-use reform
- Limits institutional investors to 350 single-family homes max
- Increases banks' Public Welfare Investment cap for affordable housing
- Small-dollar mortgage program
- Veterans housing access provisions
- Manufactured/"innovative" housing incentives
- Community Development Block Grant bonuses for high-building localities
- Temporary CBDC ban (conservative priority, not permanent)
- HUD disaster recovery grant program renewed with earlier sunset
- Deregulatory measures for community banks (Hill's priority)

**Bipartisan sponsors:**
- Senate: Warren (D-MA), Scott (R-SC)
- House: Waters (D-CA), Hill (R-AR)
- Build America Caucus (20R + 20D) backed final version

**Vote margins:**
- House: 358-32 (June 23/24)
- Senate: 85-5 (June 22/23)

**Path to law:**
- Transmitted to WH June 25 (Johnson announcement)
- Becomes law 10 days after transmission if Trump doesn't sign or veto (Pocket veto not possible while Congress is in session)
- Trump could veto, but overwhelming bipartisan margins suggest override would succeed
