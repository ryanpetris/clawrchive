## AI Accountability Act — Bill Details

- Official name/clarity: The reporting calls it the "AI Accountability Act"
- Vote: 68-29 in Senate
- Key provisions:
  - Federal agencies/vendors must document high-risk automated systems
  - Report serious incidents within 72 hours to agency CIOs and NIST
  - Civil penalties for algorithmic discrimination in housing, credit, employment
  - Incident triggers: wrongful denial of benefits, erroneous law enforcement alert, data breach affecting 10,000+ individuals
  - Private lawsuits limited: only where contractor failed to maintain documentation or ignored stop-use order
  - Union representatives can participate in workplace AI impact reviews at federal facilities
  - Real-time facial recognition ban replaced with agency-by-agency authorization
- House referral: to Energy & Commerce and Judiciary simultaneously
- CBO cost estimates still under White House review
