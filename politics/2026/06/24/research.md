### Vote Mechanics
- **Senate vote:** 50-48 in favor of War Powers Resolution (concurrent resolution, not subject to presidential veto)
- **House passed** earlier this month: 215-208 (4 Republicans joined all Democrats)
- **This was the 10th time** Senate Democrats forced an Iran war powers vote; first time it passed
- **4 Republican defectors:** Collins (ME), Murkowski (AK), Paul (KY), Cassidy (LA)
- **1 Democrat defector:** Fetterman (PA) voted with Trump

## Supreme Court — June 23 Opinions (Deeper)

### Cisco Systems v. Doe (24-856) — Alien Tort Statute
- 6-3 decision, Barrett wrote majority
- Courts may NOT create new causes of action under ATS for violations of international norms
- Torture Victim Protection Act does not allow aiding-and-abetting liability
- Only three original violations recognized in Sosa remain
- Sotomayor 27-page dissent: "marks yet another low point in this Court's esteem for its precedents"
- Policy impact: Dramatically limits ability of foreign plaintiffs to sue in US courts for human rights abuses

### Exxon Mobil v. Corporación Cimex (24-699) — Cuban Sovereign Immunity
- 6-3 decision, Kavanaugh wrote majority
- Helms-Burton Act abrogates FSIA immunity for Cuban state entities
- Allows lawsuits over property confiscated during Cuban revolution
- Kagan dissent: Helms-Burton says "not one word" about sovereign immunity

### Pung v. Isabella County (25-95) — Tax Foreclosure Compensation
- Alito wrote for 8-1 Court (Thomas concurred in judgment only)
- Auction price, not fair market value, is baseline for just compensation in tax sales
- Requires "fairly conducted" sales; remanded for lower court to define fairness
- Thomas concurrence: Michigan state courts may have erred on underlying tax obligation

### Blanche v. Lau, Landor v. Louisiana DOC (June 23) — Summary dispositions
- Summary reversals/affirmances; no major new constitutional rulings

### McCarthy v. Hernandez (June 22) — AEDPA / Etan Patz case
- Per curiam reversing Second Circuit habeas grant
- Pedro Hernandez convicted in 1979 Etan Patz murder; Second Circuit exceeded AEDPA limits
- Demonstrates Court's continued strict enforcement of AEDPA
