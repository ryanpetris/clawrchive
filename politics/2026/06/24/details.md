## 8. Courts & Legal/Regulatory Developments

### Supreme Court — Cisco Systems v. Doe (24-856)
- **What happened:** 6-3 decision (Barrett) holding courts may not create new causes of action under the 1789 Alien Tort Statute for violations of international law. Also held Torture Victim Protection Act does not allow aiding-and-abetting liability.
- **Legal status:** Final; limits foreign plaintiffs' ability to sue in US courts for human rights abuses.
- **Policy/political impact:** Major limitation on international human rights litigation in US courts.
- **Source:** [SCOTUSblog](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/06/supreme-court-limits-ability-of-foreigners-to-bring-lawsuits-for-violations-of-international-law/)

### Supreme Court — Exxon Mobil v. Corporación Cimex (24-699)
- **What happened:** 6-3 decision (Kavanaugh) allowing Exxon to sue Cuban state-owned oil entities for property confiscated after the Cuban revolution, holding the Helms-Burton Act abrogates Foreign Sovereign Immunities Act protections for Cuban entities.
- **Legal status:** Final; remand for proceedings.
- **Policy/political impact:** Opens door for additional lawsuits against Cuban state entities by US companies with pre-revolution claims.
- **Source:** [SCOTUSblog](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/06/court-rules-for-exxon-mobil-in-cuban-confiscation-case/)

### Supreme Court — Pung v. Isabella County (25-95)
- **What happened:** 8-1 decision (Alito) holding that auction price, not fair market value, is the proper baseline for "just compensation" in tax foreclosure sales. Remanded for lower court to determine whether the county's sale procedures were "fairly conducted."
- **Legal status:** Final; remanded.
- **Policy/political impact:** Validates longstanding tax foreclosure practices; leaves open question of what constitutes a "fairly conducted" sale.
- **Source:** [SCOTUSblog](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/06/justices-send-compensation-dispute-over-seized-home-back-to-lower-court/)

### Supreme Court — McCarthy v. Hernandez (June 22)
- **What happened:** Per curiam reversing Second Circuit habeas relief for Pedro Hernandez (Etan Patz murder conviction) on AEDPA grounds.
- **Source:** [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/mccarthy-v-hernandez-opinion-per-curiam-scotus.pdf)

### ICE Attack Sentencing
- **What happened:** Protesters accused of Antifa ties sentenced to up to 100 years in prison for attack where a police officer was shot. Sentences reported to dwarf those given to January 6 rioters.
- **Legal status:** Sentenced. Likely appeals.
