# Daily Political Outlook - 2026-06-02 - Congressional Funding & Anti‑Weaponization Fund

## 1. Executive Summary
- The Senate reconvenes after a weekend of intense debate over the DOJ’s temporary injunction on the $1.8 billion “anti‑weaponization” fund, a settlement tied to former President Trump’s lawsuit against the IRS.
- Senate Majority Leader **John Thune** (R‑SD) signals that the $72 billion reconciliation package funding ICE and CBP can move forward if the fund is abandoned.
- Senate Minority Leader **Chuck Schumer** (D‑NY) promises to introduce an amendment that would permanently ban the fund.
- The Trump administration has publicly agreed to **comply with the court order**, effectively shelving the fund for now.
- Parallel developments: President Trump claims rapid progress in talks with Iran; a federal judge blocks the National Park Service from revoking a protest permit over the “86‑47” flag.
- These events set a legislative tone focused on immigration funding and a looming showdown over the fund’s future.
