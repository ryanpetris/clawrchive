## 2. White House And President

### Trump Delays DNI Nomination, Ties FISA to SAVE America Act

**What happened:** President Trump announced via Truth Social early Wednesday that he is cancelling Jay Clayton's scheduled Senate Intelligence Committee hearing for DNI and will not support FISA Section 702 renewal unless it comes with the SAVE America Act attached. He also conditioned Clayton's confirmation on James McDonald's approval as US Attorney for SDNY. Bill Pulte will remain as acting DNI. [Source](https://punchbowl.news/article/senate/clayton-nom-blowup/)

**Why it matters:** This three-way standoff—Trump vs. Senate Republicans vs. Democrats—paralyzes both FISA reauthorization (already lapsed since Friday) and the DNI succession. Pulte, who has no national security experience, is set to assume the acting DNI role on Friday. Democrats have made clear they will not pass FISA with Pulte in the job.

**Policy or political implications:**
- Trump is directly challenging Thune's leadership and the Senate GOP's refusal to eliminate the filibuster or pass the SAVE Act
- FISA 702 remains lapsed for the first time since 2008, which intelligence officials warn creates a gap in foreign intelligence collection
- The "full version" of the SAVE Act Trump is demanding would also ban automatic mail-in ballots and restrict transgender procedures—language that lacks sufficient GOP support to pass either chamber

**What to watch next:**
- Whether Thune can find a path to pass a clean FISA extension without Trump's support
- Whether any Senate Republicans break with Trump over national security concerns
- Pulte's assumption of acting DNI role on Friday and any resulting Democratic or Republican pushback
- House Speaker Johnson's plan to put parts of the SAVE Act in the "reconciliation 3.0" package

### Trump at G7: Iran Deal, Ukraine, Russia Sanctions

**What happened:**
- Trump warned Iran at the G7 that he is ready to "drop bombs right smack in the middle of their head" if Tehran does not abide by its obligations under the MOU. He characterized the deal as not final and said "if I don't like it we will go back to shooting at them." [Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/trump-iran-war-mou-bomb-military-action-6190266)
- Trump met bilaterally with Ukrainian President Zelenskyy and said "Russia should make a deal" to end the war. He said he would do "whatever I can." [Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/g7-pressure-russia-ukraine-war-oil-sanctions-donald-trump/)
- Trump signaled the US could soon re-impose sanctions on Russian oil that were eased during the Iran war. "Soon we'll be able to do that because the oil is now flowing," he told reporters. [Source](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-signals-he-may-reimpose-sanctions-on-russian-oil-as-g7-refocuses-on-ukraine)
- Trump said the MOU does not include immediate sanctions relief for Iran and praised the deal as "very strong." He predicted oil prices could go "lower than where they were before the war." [Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/trump-iran-war-mou-bomb-military-action-6190266)

**Why it matters:** Trump's dual track—finalizing a peace deal with Iran while increasing pressure on Russia—marks a significant shift in US foreign policy posture. His willingness to threaten a return to bombing Iran if talks fail keeps the framework fragile.

### Vice President Vance's Role

**What happened:** Vance has become the White House's top messenger on the Iran deal, granting interviews across all major networks. He is slated to attend the Geneva signing ceremony on Friday alongside Jared Kushner. [Source](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/news/white-house/4610684/vance-hitches-political-wagon-iran-deal-fate/)

**Why it matters:** Vance is hitching his political future to the deal's success. He said he and his family will decide on a 2028 run after the 2026 midterms. Defense hawks like Sen. Lindsey Graham (R-SC) have labeled Vance the "architect of the deal" and demanded it be sent to Congress for approval.

## 3. Congress: Senate

### FISA and DNI Standoff

**Who:** President Trump, Majority Leader John Thune (R-SD), Minority Leader Chuck Schumer (D-NY), Sen. Tom Cotton (R-AR), Sen. Mark Warner (D-VA)

**What happened:** Trump's early morning Truth Social posts blew up the plan to quickly confirm Clayton as DNI and pass FISA renewal. Thune had told reporters Monday that the Senate would move a FISA extension without the SAVE Act attached. Now the path is unclear. [Source](https://punchbowl.news/article/senate/clayton-nom-blowup/)

**Why it matters:** FISA 702 has been lapsed since June 12. Intelligence officials have warned this creates a "significant gap in foreign intelligence collection." Cotton and Grassley wrote to National Security Advisor Marco Rubio to prepare for this contingency. [Source](https://abcnews.com/Politics/democratic-revolt-trumps-dni-pick-pulte-derails-fisa/story?id=133682783)

**Expected next step:** Unclear. Thune likely needs to find a procedural path that either satisfies Trump or isolates him. The House is in recess until June 23, which means no legislative action is possible until then at the earliest.

### Todd Blanche Confirmation

**Who:** Acting AG Todd Blanche, Sen. Chuck Grassley (R-IA), Sen. John Cornyn (R-TX), Sen. Thom Tillis (R-NC), Sen. Susan Collins (R-ME), Sen. Bill Cassidy (R-LA)

**What happened:**
- Blanche assured GOP senators at private meetings Tuesday that the $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization" fund is dead. [Source](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/5927061-blanche-senate-confirmation-concerns/)
- Sen. Cornyn had withheld support until DOJ provided Trump family tax information—a request some view as related to the IRS audit shielding agreement.
- Sen. Cassidy wants Blanche to assure him his loyalty is to the Constitution over Trump.
- Sen. Collins is waiting until after the Judiciary Committee hearing.

**Why it matters:** Blanche needs every Republican on the Judiciary Committee to advance. Just one "no" vote kills his nomination in committee. Grassley has set a hearing for July 15.

**Expected next step:** Grassley's July 15 hearing will be critical. Blanche continues to face skepticism over his close ties to Trump as his former criminal defense attorney.

### Democrats Force CRA Vote on ACA Rule

**Who:** Minority Leader Chuck Schumer (D-NY), Sen. Tammy Baldwin (D-WI), Sen. Ron Wyden (D-OR)

**What happened:** Senate Democrats introduced a Congressional Review Act resolution to block a Trump administration rule affecting ACA enrollment. If 30 senators sign a discharge petition, Democrats can force a floor vote without needing committee approval. [Source](https://kffhealthnews.org/insurance/aca-payment-parameters-rule-congressional-review-act-resolutions-democrats/)

**Why it matters:** The resolution is unlikely to pass the Republican-controlled chamber, but Democrats will use the vote in campaign ads. Healthcare costs are the top concern for voters, and a recent Pew poll found 75% of adults see healthcare costs as a "very big problem."

**Expected next step:** Democrats will push for the discharge petition signature threshold. Companion resolution expected in the House today.

### Key Individual Senator Actions

- **Sen. Lindsey Graham (R-SC):** Demanded Vice President Vance present the Iran MOU text to Congress for approval; expressed skepticism about the deal. [Source](https://responsiblestatecraft.org/trump-criticized-iran-deal/)

## 4. Congress: House Of Representatives

### Speaker Johnson's SAVE Act Strategy

**Who:** Speaker Mike Johnson (R-LA-4)

**What happened:** Johnson said Sunday that he plans to include at least some pieces of the SAVE America Act in a "reconciliation 3.0" bill, sidestepping the need for 60 Senate votes. Johnson told Fox News he has communicated this strategy to Trump. [Source](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jun/15/gop-leaders-nix-trump-plan-pair-save-america-act-spy-law-extension/)

**Why it matters:** This parallel track gives Trump a legislative vehicle for his priority voting bill while keeping the FISA fight from linking the two issues. However, reconciliation is limited to budget-related provisions, which may not accommodate the full SAVE Act.

**Expected next step:** House Republicans begin assembling the reconciliation 3.0 package. Timing is unclear.

### McCarthy-Gaetz Book Revelations

**Who:** Former Speaker Kevin McCarthy (R-CA-20, retired), former Rep. Matt Gaetz (R-FL-01, resigned)

**What happened:** An advance copy of John Leganski's book "Glory, Grief and the Gavel" reveals that McCarthy believed Gaetz targeted him over a House Ethics investigation into Gaetz's alleged misconduct. McCarthy texted Minority Leader Jeffries on the morning of the October 2023 vote: "Just my opinion—don't split your conference. This is my fight in my party." [Source](https://www.notus.org/congress/kevin-mccarthy-matt-gaetz-vote-to-oust-speaker-hakeem-jeffries)

**Why it matters:** The book, set for release June 23, provides new detail on the internal dynamics that led to the first Speaker ouster in House history. It also shows Jeffries' calculation about whether to rescue McCarthy.
