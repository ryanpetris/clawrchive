## FISA Section 702 — Deeper Context

### What Section 702 Does
- Allows warrantless surveillance of non-U.S. persons located outside the U.S. to collect foreign intelligence
- Expired for first time since 2008
- Intelligence community says it's critical for counterterrorism, cyber threat detection

### Path to Renewal
- **Clean renewal:** Needs Trump's support. He's currently demanding Save America Act (voter ID) attached.
- **Save America Act:** Would impose stricter voter ID requirements, citizenship verification for registration — needs 60 votes in Senate, which isn't there.
- **Possible compromise:** Trump could back down (as he did on the $1.8B fund) once political pressure mounts. Senate may try to pass clean FISA and challenge Trump to veto it.
- **Jay Clayton factor:** Once confirmed as permanent DNI, he may be able to rebuild trust with congressional negotiators that Pulte destroyed.

### Political Dynamics
- Democrats are unwilling to negotiate on FISA while Pulte is acting DNI (calling him unqualified)
- Even some Republicans (per NBC) called Pulte unqualified
- The terrorism threat framing gives urgency but may not break the logjam
