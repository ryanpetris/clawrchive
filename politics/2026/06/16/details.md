## 2. White House And President

### U.S.-Iran Framework Peace Deal Announced
- **What happened:** President Trump and Iran announced a framework peace deal to end the war that began Feb. 28. The deal extends the ceasefire for 60 days, will reopen the Strait of Hormuz, and calls for a formal signing ceremony Friday in Switzerland. Pakistan's PM Shehbaz Sharif and Qatar mediated.
- **Why it matters:** The war rattled global energy markets, shut roughly 20% of global oil transit through Hormuz, and caused widespread destruction in Iran and regional instability. This deal, if it holds, represents a major foreign policy achievement but leaves critical questions unresolved.
- **Key details per NPR/NYT:** The MoU is described as "about a page and a half" (per VP Vance). Iran would be permitted low-level nuclear enrichment (per Trump to NYT). Iran wants billions in frozen assets and sanctions relief. Strait of Hormuz mine removal to begin after Friday signing. Hezbollah-Israel fighting in Lebanon must stop.
- **Policy implications:** Markets reacted positively. Oil prices dropped ~5%. However, Israel was not directly involved and voiced strong reservations. Israeli Defense Minister Katz said Israel would keep troops in southern Lebanon indefinitely.
- **What to watch next:** Friday signing in Switzerland; 60-day negotiation window for permanent ceasefire; whether Israel and Hezbollah halt hostilities; release of full MoU text.
- **Sources:** [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/15/nx-s1-5858590/us-iran-deal-updates), [NBC News](https://nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/3-takeaways-new-poll-politics-desk-rcna350201), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ce8mv6l6eezo), [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jun/14/trump-calls-for-restraint-israel-airstrikes-beirut-us-iran-peace-deal), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-peace-deal-agreed-israel/)

## 3. Congress: Senate

### FISA Section 702 Expired — Renewal Standoff
- **What happened:** FISA Section 702, the government's warrantless surveillance authority for foreign intelligence, expired Friday — first lapse since 2008. The expiration came after Trump's appointment of Bill Pulte as acting DNI (widely seen as unqualified) stalled bipartisan negotiations, and Trump later demanded that any renewal be paired with the Save America Act (election/voter ID bill).
- **Why it matters:** The intelligence community warns the lapse harms counterterrorism and foreign intelligence capabilities at a time when terrorism threat levels are at their highest. The 2026 World Cup security preparations are also affected. Senate Majority Leader Thune says Save America Act cannot pass (needs 60 votes).
- **Expected next step:** Senate hopes to fast-track Jay Clayton (nominated permanent DNI) confirmation this week, then attempt to unlock FISA renewal votes. Trump's current position makes a clean renewal unlikely.
- **Key players:** Sen. John Thune (R-SD, Majority Leader), Sen. Chuck Schumer (D-NY, Minority Leader), Jay Clayton (DNI nominee), President Trump.
- **Source:** [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/reporters-notebook-lawmakers-scramble-fisa-fight-comes-worst-possible-time), [NBC News](https://nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/3-takeaways-new-poll-politics-desk-rcna350201), [The Center Square](https://www.thecentersquare.com/national/article_a5729c43-8193-45ba-b7e5-2c8be0c6d832.html)

### Jay Clayton DNI Nomination
