## 2. White House And President

### Trump's Iran Deal Defense and Conflicting Statements
- **What happened:** Trump gave an Axios interview Thursday in which he appeared to contradict himself multiple times on the Iran MOU. He said the war taught him "there are no limits" to his power, then immediately said "I know there are." Asked about his "unconditional surrender" demand, he said the MOU "really, probably is unconditional surrender" despite leaving Iran in control of the Strait of Hormuz and providing $300B in reconstruction funds.
- **Why it matters:** The interview adds to GOP concerns about the deal's substance and the administration's strategic coherence. Multiple House and Senate Republicans have expressed private or public doubt.
- **What to watch next:** Senate Majority Leader Thune said an administration briefing on the MOU is expected "early next week." The degree of GOP buy-in will affect Trump's ability to pass any related legislation.

### OMB Redirects $352M from Secret Service to White House Ballroom
- **What happened:** The Office of Management and Budget redirected $352M from two accounts designated for Secret Service hiring and training, drawn from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (Trump's 2025 tax law), to the "East Wing Modernization Project" — a 90,000 sq ft ballroom. - **What to watch next:** Potential oversight hearings or an appropriations rider to block the transfer.

## 3. Congress: Senate

### Pentagon $80 Billion Supplemental Request
- **What happened:** Deputy Defense Secretary Stephen Feinberg told lawmakers this week that DoD needs $80 billion to cover Iran war costs and other bills (per WSJ). A larger omnibus supplemental including farm aid and disaster relief is expected soon. If Congress does not act, operational budgets could be depleted "as early as this summer."
- **Why it matters:** Sen. Chris Murphy (D-CT) said "there are not 60 votes in the Senate to pass an additional funding bill." The Iran war has cost an estimated $29B through mid-May (DoD figure), though independent analysts say the real number could be $50-100B. The request faces steep bipartisan headwinds.
- **Source:** [The Economic Times/WSJ](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/pentagon-tells-us-lawmakers-it-needs-80-billion-for-iran-war-and-other-bills-wsj-reports/articleshow/131846044.cms), [NewsNation](https://www.newsnationnow.com/politics/trump-hegseth-gop-defense-dollars/)

### Senate Advances Housing Bill (H.R. 6644)
- **What happened:** The Senate voted 87-8 on June 16 to invoke cloture on the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act. Debate began this week; final passage expected Monday.
- **Why it matters:** Bipartisan bill addressing the 2-6 million home shortage via zoning reform, streamlined environmental reviews, $200M/year in construction grants (2027-2031), $100M/year for commercial-to-residential conversions, a ban on large investors owning >350 homes from buying more, and expanded FHA mortgage limits.
- **Who:** Lead sponsors include Sen. Tim Scott (R-SC), Sen. Mark Warner (D-VA). Senate Majority Leader Thune (R-SD) championed the floor process. Trump reversed initial opposition after House-added investor restrictions.
- **Expected next step:** Final Senate vote Monday, then House vote on Senate version, then to Trump's desk. Congress expects to send it to the president by next Friday.
- **Source:** [Deseret News](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/06/19/senate-advances-housing-bill-hopes-to-send-it-back-to-house/), [Legis1](https://legis1.com/news/housing-supply-bill-senate-clears-path-for-after), [Punchbowl News](https://punchbowl.news/article/senate/thune-schumer/)

### Wicker (R-MS) Breaks with Trump on Iran Deal
- **What happened:** Senate Armed Services Committee Chairman Roger Wicker (R-MS) issued a sharply critical statement calling the Iran MOU "completely out of step with the president's goals." He opposed lifting sanctions and criticized the $300B reconstruction fund. Other GOP senators also expressed reservations.
- **Why it matters:** Open GOP Senate criticism of a Trump foreign policy action is significant. Wicker's position as armed services chair gives his critique institutional weight.
- **Source:** [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/live/2026/jun/18/supreme-court-birthright-citizenship-ruling-tps-mangione-iran-latest-updates)

### Senate Confirms Michelle Steel as Ambassador to South Korea
- **What happened:** The Senate confirmed former Rep. Michelle Steel (R-CA) as U.S. ambassador to South Korea.
- **Source:** [Southeast Missourian](https://www.semissourian.com/news/congressional-votes-june-12-18-2026-0aca4fe9)

### Senate NDAA: Armed Services Committee Advances $1.15T Defense Bill
- **What happened:** The Senate Armed Services Committee released its FY2027 NDAA on Tuesday, authorizing $1.15T. It proposes a flat 3.6% military pay raise, rejecting the White House's tiered 5-7% plan.

## 8. Courts And Legal Or Regulatory Developments

### Biden Ghostwriter Tapes (D.D.C.)
- **Court:** U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia
- **Judge:** Dabney L. Friedrich (Trump appointee)
- **What happened:** Judge Friedrich ruled on June 19 that DOJ could release redacted audio recordings and transcripts of Biden's interviews with ghostwriter Mark Zwonitzer to the Heritage Foundation, citing strong public interest in understanding special counsel Hur's prosecutorial decisions. Later that day, she granted Biden's request for a 3-week injunction pending appeal.
- **Next step:** Biden's legal team will file an expedited appeal at the D.C. Circuit Court of Appeals. If the D.C. Circuit declines to intervene, the 3-week stay expires and release proceeds.
- **Source:** [CBS News](https://cbsnews.com/news/biden-justice-department-conservations-ghostwriter-heritage-foundation), [Newsweek](https://newsweek.com/biden-ghostwriter-tapes-classified-documents-heritage-foundation-12097220)

### Mail-in Voting Lawsuit (D. Mass.)
- **Court:** U.S. District Court for the District of Massachusetts
- **Judge:** Indira Talwani (Obama appointee)
- **What happened:** Judge Talwani ruled that 23 Democratic-led states and voting rights groups can proceed with their lawsuit challenging Trump's executive order restricting mail-in voting. She found the order's potential impact on the November midterms means the case cannot wait.
- **Next step:** Plaintiffs may seek a preliminary injunction to block the order before the November midterms. USPS proposed rule comment period ends July 2.
- **Source:** [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/live/2026/jun/18/supreme-court-birthright-citizenship-ruling-tps-mangione-iran-latest-updates)
