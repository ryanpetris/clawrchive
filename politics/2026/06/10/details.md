## 3. Congress: Senate

### FISA 702 — Two Days To Expiration, No Path Forward
- **What happened:** The Senate returned this week with no clear path to reauthorize Section 702 before the Friday deadline. Grassley (R-IA) gave floor remarks Monday warning that letting 702 lapse is a "real gamble we can't afford to take" — noting that while March FISA Court certifications might keep the program running temporarily, this is "not a certainty" and there will be "high stakes litigation."
- **Latest developments:**
  - Trump signaled Pulte will assume acting DNI role June 19, rejecting Democrats' central demand.
  - Durbin (D-IL) said on the floor Tuesday: "To take Mr. Pulte, who runs the housing programs, and put him in charge makes no sense whatsoever, temporary or permanent."
  - Kelly (D-AZ) said he couldn't support an extension even if Pulte stayed as acting director.
  - Warner (D-VA) said votes for extension "probably wouldn't materialize" with Pulte remaining.
  - Cotton (R-AR) and Grassley wrote to Rubio asking State to prepare contingency plans including a possible executive order.
  - Thune cast doubt on a short-term extension.
  - House Freedom Caucus is demanding warrant requirement and CBDC ban be attached.
- **Why it matters:** This would be the first lapse of Section 702 authority since its creation in 2008. Intelligence leaders warn of "significant gap in foreign intelligence collection." The crisis is entirely self-inflicted — a bipartisan deal was near before the Pulte appointment.

### Thune Admits SAVE Act Cannot Pass Senate
- **What happened:** Majority Leader Thune told reporters Tuesday: "We don't have the votes" for Trump's SAVE America Act. He said even a narrowed version covering just photo voter ID and citizenship proof "takes 60 votes" and "there aren't even CLOSE to the votes to get rid of the filibuster."
- **Why it matters:** This is a rare public admission from GOP leadership that Trump's election agenda is dead on arrival in the Senate. Trump has been pressing Republicans to eliminate the filibuster. Thune's statement effectively ends that push for this Congress.
- **Source:** [Meaww](https://news.meaww.com/john-thune-admits-trumps-save-america-act-cannot-pass-senate-we-dont-have-the-votes)
