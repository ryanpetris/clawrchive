## 3. Congress: Senate

### Reconciliation 2.0 Passes (ICE funding)
- **What happened:** The Senate passed a $69.5B budget reconciliation package funding ICE and Border Patrol through 2029 early Friday morning, 52-47. Only Sen. Lisa Murkowski (R-AK) voted against.
- **The vote-a-rama:** An 18-hour marathon of amendments. Highlights:
  - Schumer's amendment to kill the "anti-weaponization" fund — failed 49-50
  - Cassidy's (R-LA) amendment to redirect fund to Capitol Police/injured officers — failed 52-47 (needed 60)
  - Merkley's (D-OR) amendment to block the White House ballroom — 6 GOP defectors (Collins, Husted, Sullivan, Murkowski, Moran, Tillis)
  - Coons' (D-DE) amendment to bar payouts to Jan. 6 rioters who assaulted police — Moody (R-FL) was among 8 GOP supporters
  - SAVE America Act (voter citizenship proof) — failed 48-50 (needed 60)
- **Why it matters:** Republicans passed the bill without any constraints on Trump. But the amendment votes exposed deep fissures ahead of the midterms, with vulnerable incumbents repeatedly breaking ranks.
- **Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5912887-republicans-immigration-fisa-crunch/), [AOL](https://www.aol.com/news/senate-passes-immigration-enforcement-funding-085558237.html), [Punchbowl](https://punchbowl.news/article/finance/economy/2-0-v-3-0/)

### FISA 702 Blocked — Pulte Standoff
- **What happened:** The Senate rejected a motion to proceed on extending Section 702 of FISA (47-52). Seven Republicans joined all Democrats except Fetterman in blocking it:
  - GOP "no" votes: Hawley (MO), Schmitt (MO), Lee (UT), Paul (KY), Scott (FL), Kennedy (LA), Tuberville (AL)
- **Why:** Democrats refuse to extend surveillance powers while Bill Pulte (FHFA director, no intel experience) remains acting DNI. Privacy-minded GOP also want warrant requirements.
- **Deadline:** June 12 at 11:59 p.m.
- **Cotton-Grassley letter:** Asked Secretary Rubio to plan for a "potential significant gap in foreign intelligence collection."
- **Sources:**

  - [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/senate-push-reauthorize-nations-spy-powers-weathers-another-controversial-trump-decision)
  - [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/media/news/gop-senators-warn-marco-rubio-to-plan-for-significant-gap-in-intelligence-collection-as-fisa-is-set-to-expire/)
