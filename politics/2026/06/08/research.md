## FISA 702 — Path Analysis

### Current state
- Section 702 expires 11:59 p.m. Friday June 12.
- Senate voted 47-52 against motion to proceed (needed simple majority).
- 7 GOP no votes: Hawley, Schmitt, Lee, Paul, Scott, Kennedy, Tuberville.
- 1 Dem yes vote: Fetterman.

### Why it failed
1. **Pulte factor:** Democrats (led by Warner) refuse to extend surveillance while Pulte, with no intel background and a history of weaponizing records, would oversee it.
2. **Warrant requirement:** GOP privacy hawks (Lee, Paul, etc.) want a warrant requirement for querying U.S. person data. The Cotton-Warner bill has no such provision.
3. **CBDC fight:** House version includes CBDC ban that Senate Republicans oppose.

### Possible outcomes
1. **Short extension with Pulte removed:** Most likely path. If Trump replaces Pulte with someone acceptable, Warner says Dems would provide enough votes.
2. **Short extension with warrant requirement:** Would pick up GOP privacy hawks but lose some Dem support.
3. **Lapse:** If nothing changes by Friday. Cotton and Grassley have already asked Rubio to plan for this.
4. **Executive Order gap-fill:** Cotton-Grassley letter suggests this as an option.

### Consequences of lapse
- Section 702 would sunset but existing authorized surveillance/data searches could remain valid for up to a year.
- Companies like AT&T might refuse to cooperate during a lapse due to liability concerns.
- Other FISA authorities (Title I) unaffected.
