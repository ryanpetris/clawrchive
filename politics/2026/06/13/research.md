# Research Notes — Daily Political Outlook — June 13, 2026

## 1. FISA Section 702 Expiration: The Key Flashpoint

### What Happened
- **House vote (June 11):** Short-term extension (through July 2) failed 198-218 under suspension of rules requiring 2/3 majority. 199 Democrats + 19 Republicans voted no. House adjourned for weeklong recess.
- **Senate (June 11):** Sen. Ron Wyden (D-OR) objected to unanimous consent request for extension.
- **Result:** Section 702 expired at midnight Friday June 12 — first lapse in its ~18-year history.
- **Trump's response:** Finally nominated Jay Clayton (current SDNY US Attorney, former SEC Chair) as permanent DNI on Thursday afternoon *after* House had left town.

### Key Dynamics
- **Democrats' position:** Will not extend FISA unless Trump withdraws Bill Pulte's appointment as acting DNI. Pulte (FHFA head) has no intelligence background. Democrats (and some Republicans) say Pulte politicized intelligence.
- **Republicans' position:** Accuse Democrats of holding national security hostage. But 19 House Republicans also voted no — some privacy/liberty concerns, some anti-Pulte.
- **Pulte timeline:** Will replace Tulsi Gabbard as acting DNI on June 19. Senate cannot confirm Clayton before then. So Pulte will serve at least some period as acting DNI over a lapsed FISA.
- **Clayton hearing:** Senate Intelligence Committee hearing set for Wednesday June 17 at 2pm.
- **Brennan Center analysis:** Existing FISA court certifications remain valid for up to one year even after sunset. Sen. Wyden says "ongoing investigations or imminent threats can be investigated."

### Political Implications
- First-ever Section 702 lapse is unprecedented — creates legal gray area.
- Democrats more unified and emboldened than a year ago (after 2025 shutdown criticism).
- Trump's primary challenges against sitting GOP senators (Cornyn of TX, Cassidy of LA) have weakened his Senate relationships.
- Midterm elections approaching — Democrats hope base rewards hardball tactics.

### What to Watch
- If Pulte takes over June 19 as planned, what does he do with intelligence powers during a lapsed FISA?
- Clayton confirmation timeline — could Senate fast-track after June 17 hearing?
- House returns June 23 — could try another extension then.
- Can Warner and Cotton reach a deal that bypasses Pulte?
