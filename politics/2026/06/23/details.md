## 2. White House and President

### Trump Signs Quantum Computing Executive Orders

**What happened:** President Trump signed two executive orders on Monday, June 22, aimed at boosting quantum computing research and national security preparedness. The first order sets a goal of developing a "commercially relevant" quantum computer by 2028. The second accelerates the government's timeline for transitioning to post-quantum cryptography to 2031.

**Why it matters:** The orders signal an administration push to keep the U.S. ahead in a strategically critical technology race, particularly against China. The 2031 cryptography deadline addresses long-standing concerns about quantum computers eventually breaking current encryption standards.

**What Trump said:** "We are going to be investing in American quantum leadership like never before."

**What to watch next:** Implementation details and funding mechanisms; congressional oversight of quantum spending.

## 3. Congress: Senate

### Senate Passes Bipartisan Housing Affordability Bill (85-5)

**What happened:** The Senate voted 85-5 on Monday evening to pass the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, a sweeping bipartisan bill aimed at lowering housing costs. Key provisions include barring institutional investors from buying more than 350 single-family homes, boosting housing supply through zoning reform incentives, and funding affordable housing construction.

**Why it matters:** This is the first major housing legislation since the 2008 financial crisis. The rare bipartisan margin reflects the political salience of housing affordability ahead of the midterms. The bill now heads to the House.

**Key players:** Sens. Tim Scott (R-S.C.), Sherrod Brown (D-Ohio), Brian Schatz (D-Hawaii), and Todd Young (R-Ind.) led negotiations.

**Next step:** House leadership said they will accelerate consideration, with final passage possible as soon as Tuesday or Wednesday.

**Source:** [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/senate-passes-bill-lower-housing-costs-restrict-wall-street-buying-hom-rcna350753) | [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/06/22/senate-housing-bill-targets-wall-street-investors-boost-affordability/) | [Politico](https://www.politico.com/live-updates/2026/06/22/congress/house-to-accelerate-housing-bill-consideration-final-passage-as-soon-as-tuesday-00970321)

## 8. Courts and Legal/Regulatory Developments

### SAVE Database Blocked

**Court:** U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia

**Judge:** Sparkle L. Sooknanan

**What happened:** Judge Sooknanan ruled that the Trump administration's upgraded SAVE program — a key pillar of Trump's second election executive order — can no longer be used for citizenship verification. She found that Congress expressly prohibited the government from centralizing Americans' personal identifying information and that agencies "knew that the database violates those statutory protections."

**Policy impact:** Major legal setback for Trump's efforts to nationalize election administration and encourage state-level voter roll purges of noncitizens.

**Next steps:** DOJ may appeal; ruling leaves the program's future uncertain.

**Source:** [AP News](https://apnews.com/live/trump-administration-updates-06-23-2026)

### CNN Report: 77 Court Rulings Criticize Trump Administration
