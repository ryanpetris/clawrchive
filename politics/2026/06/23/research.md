## Iran Deal Details: The Discrepancies

### What Vance Said (June 22, Switzerland)
- IAEA inspectors will visit Iranian nuclear sites "this week"
- Unfrozen Iranian assets would buy U.S. soy, corn, wheat
- U.S. and Qatar would approve how Iran uses unfrozen funds
- High Level Committee established; 60-day roadmap agreed
- "De-confliction cell" for Lebanon agreed

### What Iran Says (June 23)
- FM Spokesperson Baghaei: No IAEA visits scheduled
- Ambassador Bahreini: Iran alone decides how to use its assets
- No current demand for U.S. crops
- Decisions based on "prices and quality"
- Baghaei sarcastic: "enriching American farmers"

### Assessment
This is a material discrepancy that could significantly undermine the peace framework if not resolved. The administration may have overstated what was agreed, or Iran may be walking back commitments. Either way, the gap between Vance's claims and Iran's denials is unusually wide for a post-negotiation period.
