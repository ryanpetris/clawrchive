## 2. White House And President

### Vice President Vance Leads Iran Talks in Switzerland
- **What happened:** VP JD Vance arrived Sunday at Emmen Air Base in Switzerland and traveled to the Bürgenstock resort near Lucerne for formal negotiations with Iranian officials on the interim deal signed last Wednesday. Vance joins special envoy Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, who were already on the ground. [Source: AP via The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/ap/ap-international/ap-us-vice-president-jd-vance-arrives-in-switzerland-to-launch-talks-with-iran-on-its-nuclear-program/)
- **Why it matters:** The 60-day sprint to finalize technical details has massive implications for global energy markets, nuclear nonproliferation, and Middle East stability. Oil futures dropped 8% after the deal was announced, but market confidence depends on progress this week. Strait of Hormuz carries one-fifth of the world's traded oil.
- **What to watch:** Whether the talks produce a workable framework for Iran's nuclear program verification. Iran's Foreign Ministry said talks would last only one day and focus strictly on enacting the agreement. Vance said he plans to be in Switzerland for "a day or two."
- **Policy/political implications:** Republican hard-liners are sharply critical, likening the deal to the Obama-era JCPOA. Trump faces rare intraparty opposition on foreign policy. Sen. Bill Cassidy (R-LA): "worst foreign policy blunder in decades." Sen. John Kennedy (R-LA): "give peace a chance."

### Trump Threatens Tolls on Strait of Hormuz
- **What happened:** Trump posted on social media Saturday threatening US tolls on the Strait of Hormuz if no deal with Iran is reached in 60 days, calling it payment for "services rendered as the Guardian Angel to the countries of the Middle East." [Source: AP](https://www.witn.com/2026/06/21/us-vice-president-jd-vance-arrives-switzerland-launch-talks-with-iran-its-nuclear-program/)

- **What to watch:** Oil market reaction when trading opens Sunday evening.

### Trump Blames Reflecting Pool Woes on Vandalism
- **What happened:** Trump announced Saturday that "multiple arrests" had been made of people vandalizing the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool, without offering substantiation. One arrest identified: David Hearn, 67, of Bethesda, MD, a former Olympic canoeist who said he stopped to look. Trump claimed chemicals were used to "destroy and demean our beautiful work." [Source: AP](https://www.witn.com/2026/06/21/trump-tries-blame-reflecting-pool-woes-vandalism-without-offering-substantiation/)
- **Why it matters:** The $14M+ rehabilitation project for the nation's 250th anniversary has become a political liability. Reports indicate hydrogen peroxide was used to treat algae, and the protective coating is peeling. The no-bid contract ($1.7M to Atlantic Industrial Coatings, a company with no prior federal contracts) raises oversight questions.
- **What to watch:** Whether federal agencies (US Park Police, NPS, Interior Department) provide details on the alleged vandalism. "86 47" found in grass on National Mall remains under investigation.
- **Note:** Claims are unsubstantiated. Agencies responsible for enforcement have not commented.

### White House Establishes UAP Governance Board

Harvard astrophysicist Avi Loeb, skeptic Michael Shermer, and retired Rear Admiral Gallaudet are among the advisers. [Source: IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/white-house-uap-governance-board-avi-loeb-1804002)

## 6. Federal Agencies: Official Announcements

### Department of Education
- **Announcement (June 16):** Signed interagency agreements to transfer OSERS (special education) programs to HHS and OCR (civil rights) functions to DOJ. The Education Department now has 14 IAAs with 6 other federal agencies. [Source: EdWeek](https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/education-department-moves-special-ed-and-civil-rights-to-other-agencies/2026/06)
- **Impact:** Education Department retains statutory responsibility but HHS will administer IDEA grants, compliance, and monitoring going forward. DOJ Civil Rights Division will investigate and resolve school discrimination complaints.
- **Effective date:** Ongoing; HHS will manage future IDEA grant allocations.
- **Affected:** ~7.5 million students with disabilities under IDEA.

### Department of Health and Human Services
- **Announcement (June 16):** Through RFK Jr., HHS accepted administration of special education programs via the Administration on Disabilities, led by Rebecca Hines (sister-in-law of RFK Jr.). [Source: Disability Scoop](https://www.disabilityscoop.com/2026/06/16/ed-department-strikes-deal-to-offload-special-education/32051/)
- **Oversight concerns:** RFK Jr.'s past statements about autism, ADHD medication, and special education as "health-related programs rather than particularly educated programs" have alarmed disability advocates.

### Office of Personnel Management
- **Announcement (June 3):** Implementing Schedule Policy/Career for ~8,000 federal positions (97% at GS-15 level). 229-page appendix lists affected positions. [Source: Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/workforce/2026/06/trump-moves-about-8000-federal-positions-to-schedule-policy-career/)
- **Impact:** Employees lose MSPB appeal rights for adverse actions. Cannot challenge reclassification.
- **Effective date:** 7-day deadline for agencies to update personnel records (from June 3).

### Department of Homeland Security / CBP
- **Announcement (June 3):** Executive Order on Strengthening Customs Enforcement. New requirements for importers of record (IORs), minimum 50% penalty floor, enhanced disclosure mandates. [Source: White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/strengthening-customs-enforcement/)
- **Key deadlines:** 45 days for legislative recommendations, 90 days for enhanced disclosure, 180 days for revised importer eligibility.
