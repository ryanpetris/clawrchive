### FISA Section 702 and DNI Nomination Fight

- **What happened:** Section 702 of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act expired Friday after Democrats refused to support an extension, citing Trump's plan to install Bill Pulte — his FHFA chief and a loyalist with no intelligence experience — as acting director of national intelligence. Trump ultimately nominated SDNY U.S. Attorney Jay Clayton as his long-term DNI pick. However, Trump now says he will not support FISA renewal unless the SAVE America Act, a controversial voter ID bill Democrats widely oppose, is attached.
- **Why it matters:** Section 702 allows warrantless surveillance of foreign nationals' communications, including when they communicate with Americans. Its expiration is the first-ever lapse of this authority. The standoff creates a national security gap and a high-stakes legislative battle.
- **Trump's Truth Social posts:** Trump asked "Why are the Dumocrats so afraid of Bill Pulte at DNI???" and stated he's against FISA without the SAVE America Act.
- **Sen. Mark Warner's position:** The Virginia Democrat said on Face the Nation he hopes the Senate can confirm Clayton "this week" via unanimous consent, and wants Trump to keep Gabbard on until then or allow her deputy to serve.
- **What to watch:** Whether Clayton can get confirmed quickly, and whether the SAVE America Act demand changes the legislative dynamics.
- **Sources:** [Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/politics/what-are-they-hiding-trump-blasts-democrats-opposed-to-bill-pulte-leading-intel/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-14/trump-ties-spy-powers-extension-to-controversial-voter-id-bill), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/mark-warner-trump-dni-pick-jay-clayton-senate-confirmation-face-the-nation/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/14/trump-fisa-renewal-save-america-act)

- **Drivers:** Economic concerns and the Iran war are the primary drags. A Forbes poll from June 9 showed Trump's approval on the economy at 30% and on inflation at 22%. Multiple May surveys found over 60% of voters opposed Trump's decision to go to war with Iran.

- **Why it matters:** With Republicans holding razor-thin majorities in both chambers, these numbers signal substantial midterm risk for the GOP. Democrats need a net gain of just 3 House seats and 4 Senate seats to retake control.
- **What to watch:** Whether the trend continues or stabilizes, and whether the midterm messaging shifts.

## 8. Courts And Legal Or Regulatory Developments

### Supreme Court — End of Term

The Supreme Court has 23 cases remaining and faces a self-imposed end-of-June deadline. Major cases include:

- **Birthright citizenship (Trump v. U.S. et al.):** Challenge to Trump's Executive Order 14160 limiting automatic citizenship for children born in the U.S. to parents who entered illegally or are temporarily present. Every lower court judge who has considered it has found it violates the 14th Amendment. Trump became the first sitting president to attend oral argument in a case where he is a party. Key justices appeared skeptical during April arguments.
  - **Status:** Awaiting decision.
  - **What to watch:** Decision expected by end of June. Could rescind or limit 14th Amendment birthright citizenship — a landmark ruling.

- **Presidential firing power:** Cases testing Trump's ability to fire officials from independent agencies.

- **Transgender athletes:** Whether federal law allows schools to ban transgender athletes from sports teams consistent with their gender identity.

- **Mail ballots:** Disputes over mail ballot deadlines and procedures.

- **Second Amendment:** Supreme Court declined certiorari in *U.S. v. Cockerham* (denying DOJ's petition on a Fifth Circuit ruling striking down a federal gun law), a "sneaky-important" win for gun rights per analysts.

- **Lisa Cook / Federal Reserve Board:** Cook's seat awaits a Supreme Court ruling affecting the structure of independent agencies.

- **Sources:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/regulation/court-battles/5922350-supreme-court-final-opinions-trump-agenda/), [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/11-upcoming-supreme-court-decisions-could-make-break-trumps-second-term-agenda), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/09/nx-s1-5847967/supreme-court-major-cases-left-2026), [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/06/14/donald-trump-legacy-executive-actions-lawsuits/90456611007/), [Ammoland](https://www.ammoland.com/2026/06/the-supreme-courts-sneaky-important-2a-ruling-is-a-big-win-for-gun-rights/)
