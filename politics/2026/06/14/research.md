## U.S.-Iran Deal Deep Dive

### The Islamabad Agreement
- Brokered through Qatar, Iran, and senior Trump envoys
- Pakistan PM Shehbaz Sharif confirmed text agreed; could be signed electronically
- Framework: 60-day ceasefire extension, Strait of Hormuz reopening, nuclear talks restart
- Nuclear uranium disposal: major sticking point. Trump says "nuclear dust" — dilute within Iran or ship to US. Iran insists on dilution within its borders under international supervision.
- Strait of Hormuz: US and Iran would lift dueling blockades. But "how it opens" — navigation fees — is unresolved.
- Israel concerns: Netanyahu reportedly not briefed; sought details from Trump admin contacts. Israeli officials warn about ballistic missile program and proxy support left unaddressed.
- Market reaction: oil prices likely to drop significantly if deal signed.

## Legal Developments

### H-1B Fee Confusion
- Trump Proclamation 10973 (Sept 2025): $100k supplemental fee on new H-1B petitions
- D.D.C. (Chamber of Commerce case): upheld fee as valid entry restriction
- D. Mass (20 states case): struck down as unconstitutional tax without congressional delegation
- N.D. California (religious groups/labor): pending
- Created circuit split; Supreme Court appeal possible
- Fee scheduled to expire September 2026

### USCIS Travel Ban Policies Struck
- Chief Judge McConnell (D.R.I.) found four policies unlawful:
  1. Benefits Hold Policy: indefinite pause on 39-country benefit adjudications
  2. Global Asylum Hold Policy
  3. Comprehensive Re-Review Policy: re-reviewing approved benefits since Jan 20, 2021
  4. Country-Specific Factors Policy: directing negative weight to travel ban factors
- Ordered agency to process applications from affected countries
