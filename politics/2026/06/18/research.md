# Research Notes — June 18, 2026

## Background Context

### The Iran War Timeline
- The U.S.-Iran war began approximately 7 weeks before the MoU (around late April/early May 2026).
- The U.S. conducted a seven-week strike campaign against Iran.
- CSIS analysis from April found the U.S. used nearly half its Patriot interceptor stockpile and >50% of THAAD systems.
- Pakistan served as the primary mediator, with Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Egypt also facilitating.
- The MoU is being called the "Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding" by Pakistan.

### Gabbard's Departure
- Tulsi Gabbard announced her resignation as DNI last month, citing her husband's cancer diagnosis.
- She was originally a Democrat turned Trump supporter, nominated to the role.

### The Ballroom Project
- East Wing demolished October 2025.
- National Trust for Historic Preservation sued, arguing bypassed review processes.

- Trump says ballroom needed for security; will include 6-story underground complex with military hospital, research facilities, drone/missile defense.
