## Deep Context: Alabama Redistricting

The Alabama case (Allen v. Milligan) has a 5-year history:
- 2021: Alabama enacts map after 2020 census with 1 of 7 districts as majority-Black
- 2022: Supreme Court temporarily blocks 2nd majority-Black district (Purcell principle)
- 2023 (June): Supreme Court reverses itself — 5-4 ruling upholds VRA Section 2 challenge; orders new map
- 2023 (July): Alabama legislature passes new map still with only 1 majority-Black district
- 2024: Lower court blocks it again; 2024 elections held under special master's map
- 2025: Permanent injunction issued against 2023 map
- 2026 (Apr 29): *Louisiana v. Callais* — Supreme Court (6-3) significantly narrows Section 2 of VRA
- 2026 (May 11): Supreme Court vacates Alabama injunction, remands for reconsideration under *Callais*
- 2026 (May 26): Lower court (incl. 2 Trump appointees) re-affirms block citing intentional discrimination (14th Amendment)
- 2026 (Jun 2): Supreme Court (6-3, shadow docket) reverses lower court, allows 2023 map

**Key irony:** The lower court panel included two Trump appointees (Manasco, Moorer) who found intentional discrimination. The Supreme Court overruled them. The dissenting opinion by Sotomayor noted the "crystal clear" record.

**War Powers Act timing:** The 60-day clock started Feb 28. Under WPA, president must withdraw forces within 60 days unless Congress authorizes or declares war, or if Congress is unable to meet due to armed attack. The administration claims the April ceasefire "reset" the clock. Pentagon/State/USAID IGs appear to disagree by launching mandated review.
