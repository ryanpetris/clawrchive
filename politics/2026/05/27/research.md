## OPM Governmentwide NDA

### Details
- Released Tuesday, to be published in Federal Register Wednesday
- Standardized form, agencies opt in
- Applies to current employees and new hires at opting agencies
- Covers: internal operations, personnel matters, procurement, "sensitive, pre-decisional or deliberative material" not publicly available
- Remains in effect after changing jobs/employers
- Violations: disciplinary action including termination, civil and criminal penalties
- OPM claims it merely documents existing obligations, preserves whistleblower protections
- AFGE: calls it "an attempt to silence and purge nonpartisan civil servants"

### Broader Context
- Administration already using polygraphs at DHS for leak investigations
- FBI raided WaPo reporter's home earlier this year in leak investigation
- 2025 OPM proposal on suitability/fitness regulations tied to NDA compliance
- OPM cited specific leaks: ICE employee identities (~4,500), Venezuela strike plans, immigration enforcement actions
