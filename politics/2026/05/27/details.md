## 6. Federal Agencies: Official Announcements

### OPM — Governmentwide NDA for Federal Employees
- **Type:** Proposed rule (to be published in Federal Register May 27)
- **Summary:** OPM released a proposal for a standardized NDA for all federal employees, covering internal operations, personnel matters, procurement, and "sensitive, pre-decisional or deliberative material." Agencies may opt in. Violations could result in termination or criminal penalties.
- **Impact:** Applies to current federal employees and new hires at agencies that adopt the NDA. Remains in effect after changing jobs. OPM says it preserves whistleblower protections and merely codifies existing obligations.
- **Reaction:** AFGE calls it "an attempt to silence and purge nonpartisan civil servants." Attorneys say litigation is likely.
- **Source:** [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/workforce-rightsgovernance/2026/05/trump-administration-pushes-governmentwide-nda-for-federal-employees), [Time](https://time.com/article/2026/05/26/trump-administration-federal-workers-ndas)

### USCIS — Green Card Process Change
- **Type:** Policy memorandum (issued May 22)
- **Summary:** USCIS announced most green card applicants must now apply from outside the U.S. through consulates rather than adjusting status while living in the country. Adjustment of status is now "extraordinary relief" rather than routine path.
- **Impact:** Affects 500K+ applicants annually. Immigration attorneys, refugee groups, and Cato Institute criticize the move. Legal challenges expected.
- **Source:** [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/trump-administration-orders-most-green-card-applicants-apply-outside-us-3803218)

### White House — Task Force to Eliminate Fraud
- **Type:** Official summary of accomplishments
- **Summary:** The White House released a comprehensive list of anti-fraud actions from the past three months, including: halting $260M in Medicaid payments to Minnesota; suspending 447 hospices in LA; charging 15 individuals in Minnesota healthcare fraud; identifying 10K fraud cases in student work programs; blocking $60M in fraudulent student loan applications; and confirming 8,000 active DOJ fraud cases.
- **Source:** [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/05/trump-administrations-full-scale-war-on-fraud)
