Trump suffered a series of major court losses on Friday with three separate rulings against his administration. A federal judge temporarily blocked the $1.8B "anti-weaponization" fund from making any payouts, while another judge reopened Trump's IRS lawsuit and launched a fraud investigation into whether the deal that created the fund was a "collusive" scheme. A third judge ordered Trump's name removed from the Kennedy Center and blocked its closure — Trump responded by saying he'd abandon the project.

Trump signed an executive order directing the CDC to align with a Health Secretary RFK Jr.-backed study calling for fewer childhood vaccines. The order adds White House weight behind the vaccine overhaul, though a prior attempt was blocked by a federal judge.

On Iran, Trump held a two-hour Situation Room meeting but made no decision on a potential deal to end the conflict. The White House said he'll only agree if his "red lines" are met, including Iran never developing nuclear weapons and reopening the Strait of Hormuz.

Senate Republicans return from recess Monday facing a political knife-edge: deciding how to handle the anti-weaponization fund dispute that's holding up a $71B immigration enforcement bill. Majority Leader Thune has asked DOJ for guardrails but has gotten silence so far.
