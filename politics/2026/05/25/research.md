## Fact-Check Notes

1. **Trump Iran MOU claims** — Conflicting messaging between Saturday and Sunday is well-documented across multiple outlets. The senior admin official briefing is consistent but not on the record by name.

2. **GOP reconciliation crisis** — Widely confirmed. Punchbowl's direct reporting from the Blanche conference meeting is the primary account. Multiple GOP senators confirmed the details in subsequent interviews.

3. **PRA injunction** — Court orders are publicly available on CourtListener. The effective date of May 26 at 9:00 AM is explicit in the order.

4. **USCIS green card policy** — Based on a single Foreign Policy Journal article citing a May 21 memo. The policy is independently referenced by Jefferson City News Tribune. Specific implementation details remain unclear (effective date, exact exceptions).

5. **NSA-Anthropic waiver** — Lowest-confidence item. Single-source reporting from FinanceFeeds citing "confidential" documents. No official confirmation. The related court ruling (Anthropic v. DoD preliminary injunction) is a separate but relevant datapoint.

6. **Fifth Circuit mifepristone stay** — Court document is publicly available on CourtListener. The ruling is confirmed.
