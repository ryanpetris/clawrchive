# Political Outlook Briefing — May 25, 2026

**Mon May 25, 2026 — Memorial Day**

---

## Topline

**Iran deal talks reach critical moment.** Trump declared an MOU "largely negotiated" Saturday, then told negotiators "not to rush" on Sunday. Iran says deal "not imminent." A two-step framework is being discussed: reopen the Strait of Hormuz → lift blockade, then 30-day negotiation on enriched uranium. Rubio signaled possible announcement this week. GOP hawks (Cruz, Graham, Wicker) are furious.

**Senate GOP reconciliation bill in open crisis.** Leader Thune pulled the $72B ICE/CBP bill after a hostile meeting with Acting AG Blanche over the $1.776B "anti-weaponization" fund. Over 20 GOP senators spoke out. Thune must find consensus language by the June 1 return. Tensions between Trump and Senate Republicans are at an all-time high.

**Court orders White House to comply with Presidential Records Act.** Judge Bates (D.D.C.) issued preliminary injunctions requiring PRA compliance by 9:00 AM May 26, including a ban on Signal/WhatsApp for presidential records. Compliance report due May 28.

**USCIS ends half-century green card tradition.** Memo dated May 21 effectively ends adjustment of status for most applicants, requiring them to return home for consular processing. Expected to face immediate litigation.

**Fifth Circuit stays FDA mifepristone mail-order rules.** Appeals court froze 2023 REMS changes that allowed remote prescribing and mail dispensing of the abortion drug.

**Courts reject two more DOJ voter roll lawsuits.** Federal courts in Wisconsin and Maine dismissed DOJ demands for unredacted voter registration lists — the third and fourth such losses for the administration.

**Reported: White House overrides Pentagon to finalize NSA-Anthropic AI deal.** Chief of Staff Wiles authorized a classified waiver for Anthropic's AI tools in signals intelligence. Not officially confirmed.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Iran deal is the dominant story** — framework MOU appears close; Trump under pressure from both hawks and a war-weary public
2. **GOP civil war brewing** — anti-weaponization fund is the flashpoint; midterm implications significant
3. **Judicial pushback on executive power** — PRA, mifepristone, voter rolls, tariffs — courts are actively checking the administration
4. **Legal immigration upended** — USCIS green card policy change is the most consequential in decades
5. **Midterm subtext to everything** — 6 months out, every development carries electoral weight
