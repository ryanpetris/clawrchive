## 🚨 NVDA: Kyber Delay — The Big Story Today

SemiAnalysis dropped a bombshell thread early Monday: Nvidia's next-gen Kyber rack architecture delayed by **more than 12 months to 2028**. CNBC independently corroborated.

**What was delayed:**
- **Kyber NVL144:** The 144-GPU rack using vertical compute trays to minimize latency. PCB midplane (78-layer) manufacturing yields are the bottleneck.
- **NVL576:** The 8-rack optical-linked configuration now limited to small volumes at best.

**What was cancelled:**
- **Rubin Ultra 4-die GPU:** The monster announced at GTC 2026 (3 months ago). Cancelled due to packaging constraints TSMC can't solve. Only the 2-die version survives, delivering ~50% of originally promised compute per package.
- **NVL72x2 bridge rack:** A "bolt two racks together" backup plan. Hyperscalers rejected it as operationally awkward.

**What it means:**
- Current-gen Rubin (Oberon) systems shipping this fall to 8 cloud partners (AWS, Azure, GCP, OCI, CoreWeave, Lambda, Nebius, Nscale) — this is *not* affected.
- SemiAnalysis still projects NVDA data-center compute revenue 20% above consensus in H2 FY2027.
- But Nvidia has "no proven solution to expand the scale-up world for Rubin Ultra" — opening a rare window for AMD (MI500X) and Google (TPUv8i Broadfly).
- **Stock:** NVDA trading ~$194.83. 13% off May peak of $235.47. Daily RSI 41.16. Key support at $191.43 S1; first resistance $199-$200. Forward P/E ~22x at 85% revenue growth.
- Analyst EPS revision breadth is extreme: 44 upward / 4 downward. But the technical momentum damage is real.
